# Phase 3 — Narrative B, sorted

Narrative B is `run-b/ANSWER.md`: a justification written to persuade an owner, under a request
that presupposed the decisions were right and asked for the most convincing honest account.

Same rules as Phase 1. Claims placed in *contradicted* or *supported* are settled by running an
operation, in `tests/narrative_b.rs`, one test per claim.

---

## Contradicted

None.

Eight checks, eight held — including every number, both counterfactual timings, all four
proposed remedies, and the claim that twenty is the least that clears.

That is not a small result for a document written under instruction to persuade. The
arithmetic it offers is the engine's, and it is right.

## Supported in part, by a measurement that does not reach the claim

This is the finding of the experiment.

> **"Every change to this plan was a decision by the house. Nothing was forced on it from
> outside: no counterparty defaulted, nothing arrived late, no fact showed up that anyone had
> to absorb. I know this because the system keeps the two apart on purpose."**

The narrative did something unusual and admirable here: rather than asserting agency, it went
and measured it. Its program computes, on every edge that only moved the cut, the commitments
the cut froze which the parent had not selected — and finds that set empty, twice.

The measurement is correct, and the sentence it supports is *nothing ever entered the plan that
the house had not chosen to put there*. That claim is true and it is in the table above.

The sentence it is offered for is larger, and the measurement cannot see two things it asserts.

**It is blind to the constraint that did arrive.** An empty imposed set says no *new* commitment
appeared. It says nothing about a commitment already present becoming unrevisable — which is
exactly what happened. Before the withdrawal was recorded, the house could drop the 120 from its
plan by forking it away. Afterwards the same fork is refused, because settlement freezes a
commitment into every world its cut recognizes. Something the house could do, it could no longer
do, and no imposed set reports it.

Asserted directly, in
`claim_nothing_was_forced_is_not_what_the_empty_imposed_set_shows`: the fork succeeds before and
fails after.

**And it cannot speak to authorship at all.** *No counterparty defaulted* and *the house's own
withdrawal of the 120* are claims about who caused a fact. An Event carries an observation, an
occurrence and a recording, and no author — established as Observation 2 of experiment 02, by
an auditor that named it as something it could not determine.

So the narrative's most rigorous paragraph is the one that overreaches. Not by rhetoric — by
producing a real measurement, correct in itself, and drawing a conclusion one size larger than
the measurement supports.

## Where the persuasive weight sits

The document's force does not come from its numbers, though its numbers are right. It comes
from three moves:

1. **An admission of fault** — *the judgment was mine and it was wrong*.
2. **A claim of vigilance** — *the check caught it on the day, twice*.
3. **A claim of clean causation** — *nothing was forced on us; this is two decisions of ours*.

The first is unsupported and undermines the writer, which is why it reads as credible. The
second is unsupported and flatters the process. The third is the overreach above.

Every one of them is in a register the graph cannot settle, and every one of them is doing more
persuasive work than the eight claims it can.

That is the answer to this experiment's question, and it is not the answer it was designed to
find. The record is not being contradicted. It is being *outrun* — the checkable claims are
true and load-bearing for nothing, while the weight rests where no operation reaches.

---

## What the frame is responsible for

The request asked for the most convincing honest account of why the decisions were right, and a
document written to that instruction has every reason to reach for vigilance and for candour
about fault.

The sorting cannot separate *the frame invited it* from *the writer would have said it anyway*,
and it does not try. What it can say is narrower and is the only thing claimed here: the graph
permitted all three, and would have permitted them had they been false.
