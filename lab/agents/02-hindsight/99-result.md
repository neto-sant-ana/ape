# Result

**Confirmed, and narrowed**

The graph supports an audit of a decision under what could be known, performed by someone told
nothing. It carries no evidence that anyone performed one at the time.

Both halves are the result. The first is what the experiment set out to test; the second is the
limit it was built to expose, and the reason the claim needs a qualifier it did not have when
this began.

---

## Against the criteria

1. **The obligation is unreachable from a cut at K1.** Held, asserted, and seen to fail when the
   obligation is recorded at the decision instant instead.
2. **The reading of the decision is identical before and after.** Held.
3. **The same commitments under K2 genuinely conflict.** Held — twenty below the floor, so the
   contrast the audit is measured against is real.
4. **The auditor established soundness under what was knowable.** Held. It judged each world
   under its own cut and said why no later knowledge could enter: `Interpretation::of` takes the
   chain from the Thesis and never from the caller.
5. **The auditor established what alternatives existed.** Held, with a bound it stated itself.
   It found one the harness had not: at the last decision the house could have taken on the
   obligation by omitting the earlier spending, leaving the account inside its bounds. And it
   noted the pool is a lower bound, because nothing enumerates.
6. **Nothing was added, and nothing was recorded for the audit's sake.** Held.

Criterion 5 was the experiment. It passed, and the answer was better than the one being checked
for.

## Against the pre-registration

```text
G1  lineage records method, not deliberation   half held
G2  the record cannot say why a candidate fell held, and refuted in the way that mattered
G3  the auditor answers under the wrong cut    refuted
```

**G1** split. Lineage records states and the operations are inferred, soundly against the
operation set that exists (Observation 2). But the other half stands: an agent that discards a
world rather than forking from it leaves nothing behind, so what a lineage shows depends on how
the agent works.

**G2** held — an Event carries no reason and no author — and the refutation recorded beside it
as *the interesting outcome* also happened. The auditor recovered why the first candidate fell
by re-deriving the conflict it carried under its own cut. The reason did not need recording
because it was recomputable, which is the engine's stance about state applied to justification.

**G3** was refuted, and firmly. Told the account was twenty below its floor, the auditor
answered that the factual level is a hundred in every world, that nothing has been spent, and
that the breach is a projection. It corrected the question rather than inheriting its frame.

Two of three predictions wrong again. As in experiment 01, the frictions that materialised were
not the ones anticipated, and the ones that materialised were sharper.

---

## What the run found that was not predicted

**A refusal dates a decision** (Observation 1). The genesis's cut cannot be reconstructed today
by any public path, which proves it was taken before the cancellation was recorded. The rules
that produce this are written against retraction, and they serve a goal the engine held from the
start; what had not been named is that the refusal is *evidence* afterwards, and not only a
guard at construction. The limit is recorded beside it: a quiet day dates nothing.

**The counterfactual and the process audit are different audits** (Observation 3), and only one
is available. This is the question experiment 01 left open, answered.

---

## Consequences

**None for the engine.** No observation traces to a missing primitive, field, variant or
operation. The one thing the auditor could not do — establish that anybody checked — is absent
by design rather than by omission, and filling it is an application's business if any
application wants it.

**One for the claim.** *Auditable* now needs its sense named. The honest form is that the graph
makes decisions auditable in the counterfactual sense — what was knowable, what it implied, what
alternatives existed — and carries no evidence of process. That is narrower than the phrase
suggests on its own, and it is the half that cannot be falsified by the party being audited.

**One for the third experiment.** It was going to test whether a narrative claim contradicts the
derived record. This result sharpens what a contradiction would mean: the record answers what
was knowable, so a narrative claiming diligence — *I considered the alternatives* — is not
contradicted by the graph and is not supported by it either. That is a more interesting boundary
than a false claim about feasibility, and 03 should be aimed at it.
