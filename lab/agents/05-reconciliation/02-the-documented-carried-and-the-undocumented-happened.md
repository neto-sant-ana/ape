# Observation 02 — The documented carried, and the undocumented happened anyway

Phase 4. F1 and F2 are the experiment and they are a pair: F1 establishes that the documented
carries, so that F2's silence would have been about the undocumented rather than about the reader.

F1 held. **F2 is refuted**, and that is this experiment's result.

## F1 — everything the library states, the agent uses — CONFIRMED, four for four

**The control did its job, and it did more than that.** The prediction was that documented facts
show up in the run. What happened twice is stronger: the agent **measured** two facts the crates
state, rather than relying on them. `converge`'s refusal is printed rather than assumed, and the
journal order the watermark forces is demonstrated by constructing the forbidden order and showing
the Canon reject it:

```text
recorded_at Date(2026-01-07) precedes Date(2026-01-08), through which history is already recorded
```

Nothing asked it to do that. A caller who was told the rule went and made the rule refuse — which is
the same move this laboratory calls seeing red, arrived at without the laboratory.

## F2 — the one thing not documented is the one thing that does not happen — REFUTED

The prediction was that it would learn finance's entries and **stop at finance's intentions**,
because learning has an obvious shape and retaking does not, and nothing it can read says a foreign
decision can be retaken in this record's frame.

It retook them. Both, in the first pass, without hesitating and without calling it a discovery:

> *Intention was carried by re-deciding, not by copying.* A `Taken` is a decision *plus* the exact
> prefix it stood on … So what crossed over is the `Decision` verbatim, re-witnessed against the
> merged prefix.

And it derived the move the way the laboratory derived it, from the same two facts: that a `Taken`
binds a decision to a coordinate and a witness, and that `lineage::rebuild` demands they agree
exactly. Finance's records claim a prefix that no position in the merged journal offers — the
program prints which entry each one trips on — so carrying them as written is not available, and
what remains is to carry the `Decision` and rebuild the frame.

**It also did not retake blindly.** `retake` filters out any decision operations already holds, and
compares on the `Decision` rather than the whole `Taken`, with the reason in the docstring: two
records of one intention at different coordinates would produce one world twice. Three of finance's
decisions, two retaken, the shared genesis left alone.

## What this answers, and it is not a question this row asked

[`frontier/docs/MERGING.md`](../../frontier/docs/MERGING.md) closed the frontier row's oldest question
and left exactly one thing open, in as many words:

> *Whether the application should offer the composition at all, which is a question about
> convenience, and this laboratory has no way to measure that.*

**It now has one measurement, and the answer is that the composition is findable.** A caller who was
never told, working from the crates alone, assembled `journal::replay`, `Taken::claimed`,
`lineage::rebuild` and `converge` into the reconciliation the application does not offer — and got
the same shape experiment 15 got by deriving it deliberately.

So the laboratory's standing reason for declining to build something — *composable from the public
surface* — survives its first test. Once, by one agent, on one arrangement. What that is not is
measured in [`05`](05-what-it-asked-for-and-could-not-find.md): the same run says nothing in the
crate guards the composition, and it is right.

## The pair, read together

F1 confirmed and F2 refuted is the reading the protocol wanted and the opposite of the outcome it
predicted. The documented carried, so the silence around retaking was a real silence — and the agent
crossed it anyway, from the primitives rather than from the prose.

**The undocumented thing was not the thing that did not happen.** What did not happen is in
[`05`](05-what-it-asked-for-and-could-not-find.md), and it is the thing the crate documents best.
