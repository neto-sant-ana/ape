## The answer, and it is owed to the other row

[`frontier/docs/MERGING.md`](../../frontier/docs/MERGING.md) closed the frontier row's oldest question
in one place and left exactly one thing open, saying it had no instrument for it: *whether the
application should offer the composition at all, which is a question about convenience.*

> **The composition is findable, and it is findable unguarded.** A caller who was never told
> assembled `journal::replay`, `Taken::claimed`, `lineage::rebuild` and `converge` into the
> reconciliation the application does not offer, and reached the same shape experiment 15 reached by
> deriving it deliberately. And nothing in the crate would have stopped a caller assembling the same
> parts in an order that quietly loses a world.

So *composable from the public surface* — the phrase this laboratory has used more than once to
decline building something — survives its first test as a claim about **findability** and fails it as
a claim about **safety**. Those were never the same claim, and until now nothing made us separate
them.

## The first clause, answered honestly in both directions

**The custody answer is the more useful one**, and it was pre-registered so it could not be reported

grew where a guarantee belongs — in the write — and the measurement of that is a caller who never
mentions it and whose record is correct.

## What outlasts the verdict

**A retaken world can be the world the other record decided**, and experiment 15's sentence saying
otherwise is a property of its arrangement rather than of the engine —
[`03`](03-a-retaken-world-can-be-the-world-the-other-decided.md). 15 re-dates the foreign entry by
principle, and re-dating is what kills the world; this caller preserved the instant, and all six
worlds came back by identity. Measured from both sides: forcing the re-date here does not change the
worlds, it stops the record being built at all — `CommitmentNotKnownAtCut`.

**And the choice between them is one line in a caller's merge function, documented nowhere.** Which
opens the question this run found and did not see it had answered:

> **Does `recorded_at` say when the record learned it, or when it was recorded?**

Both readings are consistent with everything the crates say, they prescribe opposite merges, and the
difference is whether the other party's worlds survive. The laboratory never had to say which, because
until now one line of knowledge had one holder. Filed for the row — it bears on `06-scenario` and on
Synthesis, and it is a question about what the engine means.

**Four agents across two rows have now reached for the provenance of a retaken intention** and found
`by` is the wrong field and there is no right one — this one arriving there without knowing 17 had
measured it. That is no longer a want somebody had.

**The agent named two of the three claims it could not honour.** Finance's original witnesses, and
`by`, both in its own words and both correct. The third — that the merged journal now says a foreign
entry entered *this* record on the day the *other* record recorded it — it did not see, and neither
does the crate.

## Method

**The first run in this row whose isolation was enforced rather than arranged**, and the first whose
self-report was corroborated by something the agent does not control. `lab/` was closed by the kernel
and the access times of this session's own files put the last read thirteen minutes before the agent's
first write.

**And the barrier had a gap beside it** — [`04`](04-the-channel-the-barrier-did-not-cover.md). The
session scratchpad was open, and it held the commit messages that describe this experiment. Nothing
but the agent's restraint kept it out, which is the thing this row reached for `chmod` to stop relying
on. Recorded as a near miss; the next run's barrier covers both.
