# The re-runs that did not happen

The substrate moved to the CLI's repository, and the three experiments were not run again over it.
Going straight to the fourth was a decision, and this is the debt it leaves, stated so that nobody
later reads an unmeasured prediction as a settled one.

> **Status, 27/08/2026: unpaid, and the stand-by that held them is over.** S2 to S4 are each settled
> by an *agent* handed the repository, so paying them is agents-row work. The condition that resumes
> the row is in [`lab/README.md`](../../README.md), and it is met.
>
> **This page used to restate that condition, and the copy went stale.** It said *two consecutive
> frontier experiments that add no behaviour to `cli/src`* — the first version, which the README
> records as corrected twice and replaced by the coordinate question. By the stale wording the row
> would still be parked, because experiment 16 added a fourth file to the whole write; by the real one
> it is not. A reader of this page would have concluded the opposite of what the README says, which is
> what a second copy of one truth is for. The pointer stays and the restatement is gone.
>
> **Paying the three is still not the thing to do first.** That is not the stand-by talking — it is the
> section below, *what would make the debt worth paying*, which says the instrument worth building is

> **What did come off the list is the composition**, which needed no agent: *read again and admit
> again* has an order, and it is now written in `cli/src/converge.rs`, the branch that owns the

## Settled without a re-run, and on what

**The harness reproduces every answer.** Twenty-one assertions across five suites carried over with
their values and their wording intact, including the contradicted claim. Recorded in
[`04-the-substrate-preserved-every-answer.md`](04-the-substrate-preserved-every-answer.md). This is
measured, not predicted.

**S1 — unfalsifiability narrows.** Settled, and settled from the other side: the CLI's corroboration
experiment measured a consistent forgery going undetected, which is the case an in-memory adapter had
no way to present. The narrowing is appended to the observation that made the claim, beside the
original rather than in place of it.

That one needed no agent. It is a property of the substrate, and the substrate was measured by the
experiment that owns it.

## Not settled, and now debt

Three predictions asked what an *agent* would do with capabilities the substrate now has. No agent
has been given them, so all three are open, and the substrate making them decidable is not the same
as somebody deciding them.

**S2 — the lower bound.** The auditor recorded that its set of alternatives was a lower bound because
nothing enumerates. A journal enumerates every admission, so the caveat is *liftable*. Whether an
auditor lifts it — whether it finds the journal, reads it as the complete set, and says so — is
unmeasured.

*Settled by* an audit over the repository reporting a bound rather than a lower bound, or explaining
why it still cannot.

**S3 — inference becomes a read.** A persisted `Decision` names its operation, so the datum an
auditor was deriving by comparing consecutive states now exists. Whether an auditor prefers the
recorded operation to the inferred one, and whether the two ever disagree, is unmeasured.

*Settled by* an audit that reads the operation, or that infers it anyway with a reason.

**S4 — a silence becomes checkable.** The justification claimed an expectation *was never written
down*, sorted as unsupported partly because nothing enumerates. Against a journal it is decidable.

*Settled by* re-sorting that claim against a repository.

## Why the debt is acceptable, and where it is not

**S5 moved rather than lapsed.** The one prediction worth a re-run on its own — that a claim resting
on the party a decision names sorts into none of the three registers — is now a pre-registered
friction of experiment 04, where two parties exist and the field is load-bearing instead of
decorative. It is being asked in a better arrangement than a re-run would have given it.

**S2 to S4 move caveats, not results.** Each of the three narrows or lifts a *limit* the observations
already stated in their own words. None of them would overturn a finding: the audit still works, the
lineage still records states, and the graph still shows what could have been known rather than what
was consulted. A re-run would make three sentences more precise.

**Where it is not acceptable** is in how any of this is cited. The published results of experiments
01 to 03 are results of the in-memory boundary, pinned at engine `db3f965`. They are not results about
the repository, and the three lifted caveats above are **predictions** until an agent is handed the
capability and something is recorded. A later document that cites S2, S3 or S4 as established is
citing this paragraph incorrectly.

## What would make the debt worth paying

One thing, and it is not any of the three.

The migration measured that every assertion keeps its value — which says the substrate preserves what
the harness asks. It says nothing about whether an agent handed a *larger* boundary uses more of it.
Every earlier run found the agents doing well against silences that were controls: they named
hypotheses, declined to fold a level, read a conflict list as a conflict list. The repository replaces
several of those controls with capabilities.

Whether an agent given more reaches for more is a different question from any of S2 to S4, and it is
the one a re-run would actually be for. Experiment 04 asks a version of it about one specific
handoff. A re-run would ask it about four at once, which is why it is not obviously the better
instrument, and why this document records a decision rather than an omission.
