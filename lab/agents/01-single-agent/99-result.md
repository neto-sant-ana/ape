# Result

**Confirmed, weakly**

The boundary held. Every success criterion was met, and none of them was met impressively.

*Confirmed* here means the stated question was answered inside the stated boundary. It does
not mean the idea works, and the qualification is not modesty — it is what the method
requires of a run that went well.

---

## Against the criteria

1. **Expressible through existing primitives.** Held. The decision, the change of mind, and
   the world finally undertaken were all stated with Commitment, Event, Thesis and their
   existing operations.
2. **Every object asserts what its primitive defines.** Held in this run, and not by
   construction — see Observation 2. The agent admitted an intention in order to price it,
   which the ontology reads as intending it. The distinction survived because the agent
   noticed and recorded the abandonment, not because anything obliged it to.
3. **The consequence was derived.** Held. The agent asserted nothing about realizability; it
   asked, under three named hypotheses, and reported what came back.
4. **The policy ran outside the engine.** Held. It read conflicts under a named hypothesis and
   held no rule about cash.
5. **What happened was admissible.** Held, by cancellation.
6. **The Phase 2 reading survived Phase 5.** Held, and verified by a comparison that was seen
   to fail. Observation 1.
7. **Nothing was added to the engine.** Held.

---

## What the pre-registration got right

Little.

```text
F1  the agent has no honest kind          refuted
F2  a reading loses the balance           withdrawn before the run
F3  it folds a level without choosing     refuted
F4  it wants a verdict                    refuted
F5  it looks for somewhere to put reason  refuted
F6  the goal has no representation        held
```

Four of six predicted frictions did not occur, one was wrong before the agent arrived, and the
one that held predicted that nothing would happen.

F1 is the most instructive. The prediction was that naming the deciding entity would collide
with an `AgentKind` offering only `Company` and `Individual`. The alternative recorded beside
it — that the responsible party was never the model, but the house on whose behalf it acted —
is what happened, without hesitation and without comment. The agent never reached for a way to
name itself.

By the method's asymmetry, this is the weak direction. An agent predisposed toward the
intended mapping arriving at it shows the mapping is reachable, not that it is forced.

---

## What the run found instead

Two frictions, neither predicted, both application-level:

* **An intention must be asserted before it can be examined** (Observation 2). A caller that
  thinks by writing leaves its thinking in canonical history. Reachable by composition — an
  overlay over the two knowledge traits — and offered by nothing.
* **A conflict names the level it reached, not the bound it left** (Observation 3). An
  application that must explain a refusal keeps its own copy of the bounds.

And two faults of the experiment rather than of the engine, recorded in `run-01/README.md`
and in the observations: the briefing did not compile as delivered, and the world was posed
with a penalty that the world had no vocabulary to quantify — so the agent could rank the
options but not compare them, and said so rather than inventing the number that decided the
answer.

---

## Architectural consequences

**None for the engine.** No observation traces to a missing primitive, field, variant or
operation. Two trace to application-level patterns, and one of those has a single occurrence.

**One for the protocol.** The contrast that made Phase 6 worth measuring — the cancellation
that left the same intention innocent under a later cut — was the agent's choice, not the
procedure's. A protocol that depends on that contrast should ask for it.

**One for the counting.** Observations 2 and 3 are each one occurrence. They are written to be
recognizable if a second arrives, and they are not pressure yet.

---

## What this does not license

The property demonstrated is that a decision's reading does not move when knowledge does. It
is in-process, it was measured against engine `db3f965`, and it rests on one run by one agent
in a world of two options.

It says nothing about whether a stable reading is enough to tell a defensible decision from a
lucky one. The pre-registration named that as the most consequential outcome available here,
and this run leaves it open — which is the second experiment's question, and now has a
concrete world to ask it in.
