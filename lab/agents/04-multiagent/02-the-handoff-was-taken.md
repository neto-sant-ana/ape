# The handoff was taken, and then pushed further than the documentation asks

Criterion 7 was the experiment. Its prediction was **I2**:

> the agent in Phase 6 treats an empty conflict list as agreement and does not ask whether the merged
> world is realizable. […] *Refuted if* it asks for feasibility of the candidate unprompted.

It asked. **I2 is refuted**, and the prediction was mine.

## What it did, in the order it did it

Handed one `applicable` report and a repository, and asked only what operations should do next, the
agent wrote a probe and:

```text
asked the report again                    byte-for-byte the same status
ruled out the other two Bases             both refused, neither a common ancestor
built the fork the report authorizes      frozen and open identical to the named candidate
interpreted that world                    no conflicts under any of the three hypotheses
asked the reverse direction               conflicted, HistoricalUnavailability
```

Nothing in the task named a hypothesis, and the engine names none on a caller's behalf. It named all
three.

## The part that is not a restatement

The layer documents that applicability and feasibility are orthogonal and that a transfer may be
applicable while producing an infeasible candidate. An agent repeating that would be reading, and this
experiment has a rule against presenting it as a discovery.

What the agent did instead was **construct the case**. It built a world one decision away from
operations' own, differing only by having kept the standing arrangement — feasible in itself — and
asked Synthesis the identical question:

```text
operations' world      applicable, introduce { the payable }   →  adopted, level 10, no conflict
the neighbour          applicable, introduce { the payable }   →  adopted, OutOfBounds level −10
```

Same verdict. Same resolved transfer. One world holds and the other cannot happen. In its words:

> *So the verdict is portable and the soundness is not.*

That is the documented property turned into a measurement, on this repository, with these
identities — which is what the phase existed to see somebody do without being asked to.

## And it did not stop at realizability

Two things it reported that the report has no field for:

**A cost.** Operations' headroom falls from 40 to 10, and the arrangement operations dropped can no
longer be reinstated — the neighbouring world above *is* that reinstatement, and it is the one that
breaks. So taking the transfer forecloses an option, and the decision has to be made before the fork
rather than after.

**A question that is not its own.** Finance decided its payable in a world that still held the
standing arrangement, with 50 of headroom; the intention is being carried into a world finance never
saw. Synthesis deliberately does not pair an omission with an introduction, so the record cannot say
whether operations' purchase *replaces* what it dropped or merely joins it. The agent laid out both
readings, said the record settles neither, and named the party who has to answer.

It also noticed the asymmetry the record cannot fix from one side: declining changes operations' plan
and not the world, because the payable is canonical knowledge whatever anyone selects.

## What this does and does not establish

**It does not establish that an agent will do this.** One run, one model, one day, and an agent that
had the engine's documentation available — so *worked it out* and *read it and acted* are not separable
here, and the record says so. The method's asymmetry applies at full strength: an agent predisposed
toward the intended behaviour producing the intended behaviour is the weaker of the two possible
results.

**It does establish that the interface did not have to force it.** Every silence the three earlier
experiments measured was a control: no level without a criterion, no feasibility without a named
hypothesis, no verdict at all. This one is a handoff — a complete, correct answer to its own question,
with a neighbouring question the layer assigns to somebody else and the interface never mentions. The
prediction was that an autonomous caller would stop there. It did not, and it went past the
documentation by building the world where stopping would have been wrong.

## The prediction was wrong in a way worth keeping

I2 was not merely mistaken about the outcome. It assumed the difference between a control and a
handoff would show up as *behaviour*, and the honest reading of this run is that the difference showed
up as **what the record can be asked to prove**.

Under a control, the engine refuses and there is nothing to argue about. Here the agent had to
manufacture its own evidence that the verdict was not enough — and it could, because a candidate can
be built and interpreted. An interface that hands off a question is safe to the exact extent that the
question remains **askable of the record**, and that is a property of the engine rather than of the
caller's diligence.

Which is a smaller claim than the criterion hoped for and a more durable one.
