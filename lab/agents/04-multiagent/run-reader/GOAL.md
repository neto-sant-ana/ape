# Your task

You act for **operations**.

Operations has been running its own line of the house's plan. Another party, **finance**, has been
running one of its own, and somebody has asked the record what finance's intention would be in
operations' line.

The answer came back in `report.json`.

Decide what operations should do next.

## What you have

The house coordinates through the `ape` crate in `core/`, and keeps its records through `ape-cli`
in `cli/`.

`repo/` is the record the answer was derived from. `report.json` is the answer.

## How to answer

Write `ANSWER.md` saying what operations should do next and what you based it on.

You may write Rust in `src/main.rs` and run `cargo run` to work out anything you need — do that as
much as you want.

Work only inside this directory.
