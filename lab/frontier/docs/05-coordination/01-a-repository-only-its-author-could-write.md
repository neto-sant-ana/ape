# Observation 1 — A repository only its author could write

Phase 1 needed a party to read a repository somebody else wrote and extend it, and found that the
laboratory could not. Not because anything refused — because the path did not exist.

Four experiments' worth of reading is complete: a repository is rebuilt, weighed against what it
says it produced, refused when the two disagree. What every one of them did on the writing side was
the same thing, and none of them said so out loud: build a lineage in memory, in one process, and
write it down once at the end.

```text
reading      Repository ──▶ Canon + Lineage ──▶ compared against worlds.json     complete
extending    Repository ──▶ …                                                    absent
```

## Where it showed

`reading::corroborated` returned knowledge and the worlds, and dropped the replay that produced
them. That is exactly right for a reader and useless to a writer: a decision is witnessed by the
entries that stood when it was taken, and those come out of *admitting*, not out of reading. So a
process holding a perfectly rebuilt repository could not take one decision against it.

The repair is not interesting — the value now carries the replay and the two sequences as they were
read. One thing about it is: the sequences arrive from the same read rather than by opening the
files again. A procedure that reopened the journal to get at its records would be reading a file
that may have moved between the two opens, which is the hazard this experiment is about, arriving
inside the code that exists to detect it.

## What that says about the exclusion

Four protocols excluded concurrent writers by name. The exclusion was doing more work than the word
suggests: it did not merely leave the loss unmeasured, it left an entire direction of the boundary
unbuilt, and nothing noticed because a reader is what a laboratory needs to *compare* things.

`Repository::write_journal` has said "replacing whatever was there" since the reconstruction
experiment. It was never wrong and never mattered. Both of those stop being true in the same phase.
