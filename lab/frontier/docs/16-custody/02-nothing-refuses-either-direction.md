## The three guards, over all eight states

Closed. Seven answer nothing; one answers, and it is the prefix.

```text
  coordinate   replay_through reaches every named entry in all eight
  witness      fires once, on the prefix
  worlds       fires never — no world is a function of anything that moved
```

**Why the reading is unchanged where the journal is not.** A world's cut is resolved from the prefix
the decision was taken at, and `rebuild` admits the journal *in step with* the lineage — so the
recorded world is produced against exactly the knowledge that stood, whatever came afterwards. That is
the repair experiment 05 earned, working as designed. What it also means is that the part of the
journal after the last coordinate has no bearing on anything the record already answered, which is
what makes its loss invisible rather than merely unnoticed.

**One thing does move, and nothing compares it.** A `Reading` reports `canonical_head`, the head of
canonical history after the whole journal is admitted, and that is not a world coordinate: it moves
when the tail's Event moves. It is reported and weighed against nothing, because `worlds.json` holds
no such field. A report is not a claim.
