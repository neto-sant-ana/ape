# Observation 4 — A witness cannot reach the tail, and the reason is bigger than the prediction

**U4 confirmed**, and the phase answers it by what a witness is written **from** rather than by
failing to think of a wider one.

`Taken::now` takes its witness from the replay standing at the moment the decision is taken. So a
witness naming more than that is not a wider claim about the same thing — it is a claim about
knowledge the record cannot show stood.

Measured, by widening the last decision's witness by exactly one tail address and writing the record
whole: refused with `WitnessedKnowledgeAbsent`, naming that address. The record refuses it, and the
refusal comes from `corroborate` walking the journal only as far as the coordinate.

## So the only witness that covers a tail is a decision taken after it

And then there is a new tail. Measured:

```text
  before deciding again          4 entries past the coordinate
  after deciding again           0
  after one admission more       1
```

> **The tail is not a corner of a record's life. It is a fixed point of it.** Every record has one,
> its size is zero only in the instant a decision is taken, and one admission afterwards reopens it.
> Every repository in this laboratory is in that state right now.

This is stronger than the prediction, which said only that a witness could not be made to cover the
tail. What it could not have said is that no *sequence* of decisions closes the gap either: covering
the tail is an operation that creates a new one, so the uncovered region is a permanent feature of the
shape rather than a lag that a diligent writer catches up on.

Which also settles what the answer cannot look like. Whatever covers the tail cannot be authored by a
decision, because a decision's authority is *what stood when it was taken*, and by then the tail is
what stood after. The author has to be something that knows the journal entire — and there is exactly
one such moment in this application: the write.
