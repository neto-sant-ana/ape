# Observation 9 — The sequence witness is not redundant, and this subject is why

The sixth success criterion — every persisted derived value compared on every read, and every one
that is not compared removed — had been argued rather than measured. Phases 4 and 5 reasoned that
the report is not written and the rest is weighed. Reasoning is what this laboratory spends its
time refusing to accept from itself, so the list was closed and walked.

Eleven corruptions, one value each, over a repository otherwise intact. All eleven are refused,
and each refusal names what disagreed.

```text
worlds.json    the instant a world recognizes         world 6 … in the instant it recognizes
               the chain it recognizes                world 6 … in the chain it recognizes
               what history made unavoidable          world 6 … in what history made unavoidable
               what it still proposes                 world 6 … in what it still proposes
               ancestry                               world 6 … in ancestry
               identity alone                         world 6 … in identity alone
               how many worlds there are              the decisions produce 7, and 6 were recorded

lineage.json   an entry dropped from a witness        …was admitted, and not taken against
               an entry invented in a witness         …which the journal does not offer
               a reference to a world nothing makes   …which the lineage does not hold
               a coordinate moved along the journal   …was admitted, and not taken against
```

Every coordinate of a world record is reached on its own, including identity — which
`disagreement` weighs **last**, so an ordering that masked it would be invisible in any test that
moved more than one field at a time.

The check runs inside `reading::corroborated`, which is reconstruction and not a harness. Stubbing
it to always agree turns the whole audit red, which is what says the audit measures the code that
runs rather than a comparison the test performs.

## And then the case the previous experiment could not build

The corroboration experiment found that the sequence witness caught nothing the world records did
not, and said plainly that its subject could not express a case where it would. This one can.

The inventory line's second decision is moved forward past the contingency's admission. It selects
what it names, it inherits a cut at the tenth, and the contingency is neither selected by it nor
an Event — so nothing about the world it produces changes. Every world record still agrees.

With the witness stubbed, that repository reads **clean**:

```text
a decision relocated where no world changes    → nothing noticed
```

So the witness is not redundant. It is the only thing that sees a decision moved to a place in the
sequence where it was not taken.

## Why this subject could and the other could not

The divergence subject admits nothing that its worlds do not go on to select, so moving a
coordinate always moved a world. This one admits a commitment **after** both lines have forked,
and one of those lines never takes it up — which is not an arrangement built to catch this, but
the arrangement built to make a transfer refusable. The case fell out of it.

That is worth keeping as a caution rather than a triumph. A witness was carried for two
experiments on the strength of an argument, was measured as redundant once, and turned out not to
be — and the thing that decided it was a property of the subject nobody had been tracking:
**whether the arrangement admits knowledge that some of its worlds decline.**

An experiment that concludes a corroborator is unnecessary is making a claim about its subject as
much as about the corroborator, and this is what that looks like when the subject changes.

## What is still not measured

That the reference and the world record cover each other completely. Each was moved on its own
here; a repository where two coordinates move together so that their disagreements cancel is a
consistent forgery, which the corroboration experiment measured and left open as authenticity.
Nothing in this experiment narrows that.
