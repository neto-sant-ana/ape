# Observation 3 — A world reproduced in every coordinate still comes back a different world

Phase 8 rebuilt three worlds in a process that shared nothing with the one that reasoned.

Not one of them survived, and only one of them was wrong about anything.

```text
                   identity   cut     partition   conditions   level   verdict
genesis            ≠          ≠       ≠           ≠            =       ≠
advancement        ≠          =       =           =            =       =
fork               ≠          =       =           =            =       =
```

The advancement and the fork are reproduced in every coordinate that describes what they
mean. They recognize the same instant and the same head, they partition the same commitments
into the same halves, they project the same conditions, they fold to the same level, and they
reach the same verdict. They are still not the same worlds.

---

## Why, and why it is not a defect

A `Thesis` is content-addressed over what it is: its parent, its cut, and its selection.
Ancestry is part of what a world *is*, not an annotation on it — an edge that said nothing
about the world it points into could not tell a fork from an advancement, which is the
distinction the whole layer exists to keep.

So a genesis that re-derives differently changes the identity of everything descended from
it, without any descendant being wrong. The divergence has one cause and three victims.

---

## What that costs the experiment, and what it saves it

It saves the repair. Three broken worlds would suggest three things to fix; one cause with a
cascade suggests one. Whatever closes this has to be applied to the genesis, and the rest
follows without being touched — and a fix that repaired each world locally would be repairing
two worlds that were never broken.

It costs the comparison its simplest reading. Reproducing a lineage is not reproducing each
of its worlds: the worlds can each be right and the lineage still be wrong.

---

## The criterion that caught it

Two of the three failures are visible only in identity. A comparison that walked the
coordinates — cut, partition, conditions, level, verdict — would have declared the advancement
and the fork reproduced, because by every one of those measures they were.

The protocol lists identity first among the coordinates and again in the success criteria, and
this is the case that shows the two are not redundant. Identity is derived from ancestry,
which no other coordinate reports.

---

## Consequences to carry

* The repair belongs at the genesis. Any coordinate added to a decision has to be one the
  genesis can carry; a form that only works for advancements and forks would leave the cause
  in place.
* Comparing worlds is not comparing lineages. A later experiment holding several lineages —
  Synthesis has three — cannot conclude from matching worlds that it matched what produced
  them.
* One thing this phase did not exercise: a fork's recorded request. The repository holds what
  was asked for, and here the same result is reachable by comparing the parent's selection
  with the child's, because the fork only introduces. A fork that omitted something never
  open, or introduced something already selected, would tell the two apart. This subject does
  not.
