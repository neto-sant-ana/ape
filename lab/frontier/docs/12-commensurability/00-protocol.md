# Commensurability

## Abstract

Three results have named one item and each of them sharpened it rather than repeating it.

```text
09 collision   blocked three capabilities on one line, and gave the request its sharp
               form: what is needed is NOT a looser comparison

10 witness     derived the thing that would state it — and measured two boundaries
               around it, one of which is a disease

11 veracity    measured that any comparison over two records is over ADDRESSES unless
               it says otherwise
```

Experiment 09 asked for a **partial meeting**: two repositories that hold one world between them are
refused at the first entry they do not share, and the refusal is all-or-nothing. It then found the
form of what was missing, and the sentence is this experiment's whole subject:

> The union of two twinned repositories' knowledge *is* an admissible journal, and handing it to a
> rebuild with both lineages is refused at `UnwitnessedKnowledge` — because a decision is a fact
> about *exactly* the prefix that stood, and the witness is tested for set **equality** rather than
> containment. So what this request needs is not a looser comparison: it is a way to say **this
> insertion cannot have changed what I decided**, which nothing in the record can currently express.

Experiment 10 then established that the record can compute it. A world is a function of its cut and
its selection; both are derived from the coordinate and the journal; and the entries a decision's
world actually reaches are a strict subset of the prefix that stood — fourteen of twenty-three,
seventeen of twenty-nine — derivable, and demonstrated by sufficiency rather than by inspection.

**So the thing 09 could not express, 10 can compute.** Nobody has put the two together.

---

## Question

**Can a record say that an insertion cannot have changed what a decision decided — and is the
agreement of two records actionable on that basis, without either of them promising less about its
own history?**

The second clause is not a caveat. Experiment 10 measured that a comparison weak enough to admit an
imported witness is *exactly* weak enough to admit a record whose journal grew underneath a local
decision — the same shape, measured, indistinguishable. That was measured against a comparison put in
the witness's **place**. Whether one put **beside** it has the same disease is the experiment.

---

## What *unchanged* means here, decided before any phase runs

Experiment 11's first obligation was to define *false*, because two protocols had used the word and
neither had. The same obligation falls here on a different word, and for a sharper reason: *this
insertion cannot have changed what I decided* is a claim the record would be **making**, so a phase
that ran without the word settled would be measuring an operation against whatever it happened to do.

> **A decision is unchanged by an insertion when the world it produces against the extended journal
> is the same world by identity as the world it produced against the journal it was taken against.**

Identity, because a `ThesisId` is derived from the parent, the cut and the selection, and those are
the whole of what a world is. Two things are deliberately **not** what this means:

```text
answering the same        two different worlds can report the same level. Experiment 06
                          measured a repository that weighed twelve candidates and one that
                          never explored, byte-identical, and 11 measured a pair of records
                          answering one number for different reasons. A comparison over
                          answers would admit a changed world for arithmetic

still admissible          that a decision can be re-derived at all is a question about the
                          journal, not about the decision. An insertion that leaves every
                          decision derivable and moves one world has changed it
```

And one case the definition has to survive, because it is the named hazard: an insertion **before** a
decision's coordinate is the only kind at issue here — an insertion after it is in nobody's prefix and
was never refused. So every state this experiment measures is one the witness refuses today, and
correctly.

The two lines point at opposite answers and that is the experiment. If the first holds and the second

## Pre-registered predictions

Five, written before anything runs, each with the observation that would refute it. Lettered **M**
because `C` is taken twice already, by contention and by collision.

**M1 — the claim is derivable, for every insertion and every decision.**

*Prediction:* *this insertion cannot have changed what I decided* is computed from the coordinate, the
journal and the decision, with nothing added to either record and neither writer consulted. Experiment
10 derived the closure; this asks whether the derivation answers 09's question and not only 10's.

*Refuted if* the derivation needs a value the record does not hold, or if it can answer for some
insertions and not others.

**M2 — and it is decidable in both directions.**

*Prediction:* a record can be caught claiming an insertion is harmless when it is not, and claiming it
is harmful when it is not. Experiment 10 measured that a dependence claim is falsifiable both ways;
this asks the same of a claim about an insertion, which is the form that matters — a check that can
only fail one way is a check somebody can lean on.

*Refuted if* one direction is unreachable.

**M3 — the three capabilities unblock together.**

*Prediction:* 09's Requests 1, 2 and 3 are blocked by one line and admit on one claim. A partial
meeting becomes statable, knowledge becomes takeable without its lineage, and the duplicate of 09's
Observation 6 never arises.

*Refuted if* one of them needs something the other two do not.

**M4 — and admitting an insertion on that basis does not reopen what experiment 10 measured.**

*Prediction:* the states 10 measured a narrowed witness admitting are still refused, because the
witness is still there and still tested for equality against what each decision was taken against.
What changes is what the **journal** may hold, and each excess is justified per decision rather than
tolerated in general.

*Refuted if* a state exists that satisfies the derived justification and is one no writer produced.
**This is the severe condition**, and it is the same shape as experiment 11's — a state reachable
without a hand that every check accepts.

**M5 — because the disease came from removing the record of what stood, not from weighing an
insertion against a derivation.**

*Prediction:* stated as the reason rather than as the result, so it can be wrong separately. What
made a narrowed witness dangerous is that it **discarded** the only claim in a repository whose
subject is its history. A comparison that keeps that claim and adds a second one is not the same
operation, however similar the admission table looks.

*Refuted if* the two turn out to admit the same states, which would say the danger was in the
admission and not in the discarding.

**M4 with M5 is the experiment.** M4 says whether the operation is safe; M5 says why, and separating
them is what stops a safe table from being read as a proof.

## What is carried forward, and is not available as a finding

Usable in reasoning, and not reportable as a result:

* **A decision's world is a function of a strict subset of what stood**, derivable from the coordinate
  and the journal, and reproducible from that subset alone — 10, by sufficiency.
* **The derivation restates the engine's admission rules** rather than walking references, because
  nothing in the record points at an eligibility — 10.
* **The witness is the only claim in a repository whose subject is its history** — 10, and the reason
  M4 is about keeping it rather than about replacing it.
* **Two repositories agree about a world exactly where they agreed about the knowledge under it**, and
  `converge::holds` answers it read-only — 09.
* **A merge takes the longer journal entire and never splices**, which is why nothing so far could
  break a dependency even in principle — 09.
* **The union of two twinned journals is itself admissible**, and is refused at
  `UnwitnessedKnowledge` when handed both lineages — 09, Observation 10.
* **A world's identity is blind to everything but its selection and its cut** — 09.
* **An address omits the recording instant**, and `converge` refuses two journals that agree by
  address and disagree about one — 11.
* **The Event chain up to a cut is dependence entire**, because an Event's identity contains its
  predecessor — 10.

Before any sentence here is allowed to be a finding, ask what would have to be false for it to be
false.

## The two hazards, named before the run

Both were handed over by the results that found them, and neither has been measured. They are named
here so that reaching them is a phase rather than a surprise, and so that *not* reaching them is
reportable.

**An Event arriving before a non-Event coordinate.** A cut resolves its head from an instant, so an
Event inserted before a decision's coordinate and recorded no later than its `known_at` moves
`event_head` — and produces a world nobody decided. Handed over by 10, which did not reach it.

**Any comparison is over addresses unless it says otherwise.** Experiment 11 measured that an
`EntryId` omits the recording instant, so two journals can be equal entry for entry and not be the
same journal. A comparison built to admit two records' knowledge is over addresses by default, and
would reopen exactly the state 11's Part B closed.

---

## Motivation

It goes now because it is the ripest item in the row by the only signal available — three concluded
results named it, and each sharpened it rather than repeating it — and because the two boundaries it
has to respect were both **measured** rather than argued, which is what a protocol needs and what a
candidate cannot supply.

It goes before authenticity for the reason experiment 11 gave and nothing has changed: that
candidate's own material argues its outcome, four of its five faces answer *no* to *is the
distinguishing fact in the record?*, and the likely result is a boundary. Boundaries keep.

And it goes before the address question that experiment 11 queued, because that question is a
**variable of this one**. Whoever builds a comparison over two records decides in its first phase
whether it is over addresses as they are; answering that inside the experiment that needs the answer
is a measurement, and answering it alone first would be a repair looking for a reason.

---

## Experimental Boundary

This experiment asks whether a claim is **statable and checkable**, and what admits on it.

It includes:

* constructing two records that agree about a world and disagree about knowledge, as 09 did;
* deriving the claim from what each record already holds, with neither writer consulted;
* reproducing 09's three refusals by value before anything changes, so a change in outcome is
  attributable;
* every state put to all three guards and to the reading, as a closed table — 10's apparatus;
* the two boundaries 10 measured, re-measured against a comparison that sits **beside** the witness
  rather than in its place;
* the two hazards above, reached or reported as not reached.

It deliberately excludes:

* **naming the operation's shape before the criterion is applied to it.** 09 said so in the request
  itself, and this protocol repeats the exclusion rather than inheriting it quietly: what the
  operation is called, where its seam is, and what it returns are Part B's, decided by Phases 1 to 6;
* **a looser comparison.** The request is explicitly not for one, and an experiment that produced one
  would be answering a question nobody asked;
* **the network, a transport, more than two records.** Excluded throughout the row;
* **the engine.** Whether two records can be weighed is a question about records;
* **authenticity**, and **benchmarking.**

## Procedure

### Phase 0 — The two records, and what each answers

Read once. Every world identity and every level recorded as a literal, for both records and for the
union. The anchor.

---

### Phase 1 — The three refusals, reproduced by value

09's Requests 1, 2 and 3, each blocked today, each reproduced by producing the refusal **by name**
before anything changes. A phase that measured an unblocking without first measuring the block would
be reporting a capability against an arrangement that never lacked it.

---

### Phase 2 — The claim, derived

M1 and M2. For every insertion and every decision, compute whether the insertion can have changed
what the decision decided, from the coordinate and the journal alone. Then break it in both
directions: a record claiming an insertion is harmless when it is not, and the reverse.

---

### Phase 3 — What admits

M3. The three capabilities, put to the claim. This is the quick and satisfying phase, and Phase 5 is
the one that decides the answer.

---

### Phase 4 — The closed table

Every state each capability reaches, put to all three guards and to the reading. Where a state
reproduces one experiment 09 or 10 already measured, it is reproduced by value first.

---

### Phase 5 — The two boundaries, re-measured

M4 and M5. The states experiment 10 measured a **narrowed** witness admitting, put to a comparison
that sits **beside** the witness. Whether they are still refused is the answer, and it is a silence
rather than a count — which is what the pressure point below is about.

---

### Phase 6 — The two hazards

The Event before a non-Event coordinate, and the address that omits the instant. Reached or reported
as not reached, with the space named — the constraint experiment 11 earned.

---

### Phase 7 — Name what an application would need, and build none of it

Requests, verbatim, in the vocabulary of the need rather than of a solution.

---

### Phase 8 — Part B, if it was earned

A repair whose shape is decided by Phases 1 to 6 and named nowhere above. It stays only if it meets
the criterion five experiments have inherited:

> **It removes a state a reader can be misled by, and what the repair replaces survives.**

And this experiment's own, which inverts the row's ripeness signal on purpose:

> **A capability three results asked for is still a want.** The number of arrivals measures how often
> the record could not say something. It does not measure whether saying it is safe. Part B is refused
> if the safety is asserted rather than measured, however many results asked — and the pull to build
> it is stronger here than anywhere in the row, because the thing is wanted, derivable, and cheap.

---

## Success Criteria

1. *Unchanged* is defined before any phase runs, and the definition excludes **answering the same**
   and **still admissible** — with an instance of each produced, so the exclusions are measured rather
   than asserted.
2. The claim is derived from what each record already holds, with neither writer consulted and nothing
   added to either record.
3. The three refusals are reproduced by name before anything changes.
4. Every state goes to all three guards and to the reading, reported as a closed table.
5. The two boundaries experiment 10 measured are **re-measured** here rather than inherited, against a
   comparison that sits beside the witness.
6. The two hazards are reached, or Phase 6 says they were not and what that leaves open.
7. Every literal — identities, levels, counts — is written before the run.
8. Nothing is added to the APE engine.
9. Part B is built only against both criteria above, and its absence is a reportable result.
10. The eleven earlier experiments' conclusions stand, or the change is recorded as a result of this
    one. *A comparison weak enough to admit an imported witness is the same shape as one that admits a
    journal grown underneath a local decision* is the one at risk, and it is stated either way.

**Criteria 2 and 5 are the experiment.** The rest is the arrangement holding.

---

## Failure Conditions

The severe one:

> If a state satisfies the derived justification and is one no writer produced, then the claim is not
> a check and the capability is not available on it. That is reported as a failure of the **claim**,
> not of the arrangement, and every capability that admitted on it is withdrawn with it.

Its neighbour, which is this experiment's own:

> If *cannot have changed* cannot be told from *did not happen to change* — if the derivation answers
> for the insertions this arrangement holds and not for insertions in general — then the claim is a
> property of the subject and the finding is why.

And the ordinary ones:

* a capability reported as unblocked without the block having been reproduced;
* a boundary inherited from experiment 10 rather than re-measured, when the whole question is whether
  it survives a different arrangement of the same parts;
* an arrangement whose insertions are all of one kind, making *the comparison admits* a property of
  the subject;
* Part B built because three results asked, rather than because the safety was measured;
* reporting *the operation is safe* when what was measured is *these insertions were refused*.

**A refuted M1 is the cheapest interesting result.** If the claim cannot be derived after all, then
experiment 10's derivation answers a narrower question than three results have assumed, and the
capability needs a record rather than an operation — which is the answer 10 argued against and would
be the largest correction available here.

## Variables Deliberately Left Open

### What the operation is called, and where its seam is

Part B's, and naming it here would be choosing the answer. 09 said so in the request itself.

### Whether a partial meeting should be offered at all

The capability being available is not the same as an application wanting it, and this experiment
measures the first. The `unblocked` section of the queue holds one item that stays unbuilt for exactly
that reason.

### More than two records

Excluded throughout the row, and this is the first experiment with a concrete reason to want it: a
claim about an insertion is pairwise, and what three journals owe each other is not answered by it.

### Cost

Deferred by twelve experiments.

## Methodological Constraint

> *Do not introduce an abstraction intended to solve an experiment that has not yet been performed.*

Structure may be introduced only when required by the current experimental procedure. The previous
experiments' conclusions are not revised here; where this one finds something that would have changed
them, it is recorded as a finding of this experiment, against the implementation as it then stood.

And the rules the last six experiments earned, which apply here without amendment. Before recording
anything as a finding, ask what would have to be false for it to be false. Every literal is written
before the run, and a wrong prediction is corrected in the open rather than adjusted. A prediction's
justification must quantify over as much as its claim does. A friction is evidence of a want, not of a
need. A phase must not be satisfied by an arrangement it could not have failed. A negative result must
name the space it searched.

## Expected Pressure Points

### Phase 3 will work, and Phase 5 is the experiment

Three refusals become three admissions, and the duplicate 09 measured never arises. That is quick,
visible, and satisfying, and it is not the answer — the same shape experiment 10 met, where an
experiment that had stopped after its Phase 3 would have had every number right and the conclusion
backwards. It is written here before anything runs for the same reason it was written there.

### *Cannot have changed* will be asked to stretch

From identity to *answers the same number*, which is the exclusion above, and from *this insertion* to
*insertions like this one*. The second is the more tempting because it is what an application would
want, and it is the difference between a check and a policy.

### The thing is wanted, and that is the danger

Three results asked for it, the material is complete, the derivation exists and it is cheap. Every
condition that usually justifies building something is present, and none of them is evidence that
admitting an insertion is safe. This is the experiment in the row most likely to produce a Part B that
nothing measured.

### The comparison is over addresses unless it says otherwise

Experiment 11 measured it and it is a hazard rather than a finding here. A first draft of any
comparison over two records will be over `EntryId`s, will look correct, and will have reopened the
state 11's Part B closed. It is named in Phase 6 so that meeting it is a measurement rather than a
regression.

## Observations

Each observation is recorded as its own numbered document beside this one, as the experiment produces
it. Record facts rather than decisions retroactively presented as inevitable.

Useful observations include: what the derivation answers and what it declines to answer; which of
09's three capabilities needs the most; what a guard covers that nobody had noticed; and what the
record cannot distinguish, stated as the query that has no answer.

Where possible, record the smallest reproducing case.

## Open Questions

* Is *this insertion cannot have changed what I decided* a claim a record should **hold**, or one it

* If two records can be partially merged, what does the result owe a reader about which parts met?
* Does a partial meeting have an inverse — can a record say which of its own entries it could give
  away?

These are candidates for later experiments. They are not requirements for this one.

## Experimental Principle

Reconstruction asked whether meaning survives.

Divergence asked whether deliberation survives.

Corroboration asked whether the record can be trusted without being believed.

Convergence asked whether two lines of deliberation can still reach each other.

Provenance asked whether a record that can be rebuilt perfectly is thereby understood.

Coordination asked whether a record built for one mind can hold two.

Exploration asked what a record owes the worlds nobody chose.

Atomicity asked who the record's promises are to.

Contention asked whether a promise made to one writer is a promise at all when there are two.

Collision asked whether two records that have never met were already the same record.

Witness asked whether a decision is about everything it happened to come after.

Veracity asked whether a record that agrees with itself has thereby told the truth.

```text
This one asks whether two records that
disagree have a common measure anyway.
```

Collision found that two records already agree about more than either can act on. Witness found the
thing that would let them act on it, and one reason not to reach for it. Veracity found that the
comparison they would act through was blind in a way nobody had measured. Between them the material is
complete and the operation is not built — which is either three experiments' worth of caution about to
be repaid, or three experiments' worth of warning about to be ignored.
