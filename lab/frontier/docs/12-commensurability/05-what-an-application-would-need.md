# Observation 5 — What an application would need, and why none of it was built

Requests, verbatim, in the vocabulary of the need rather than of a solution. None was built, and
Observation 4 is why: every one of them needs the same thing the record does not have.

**Request 1 — a decision that can say what it was taken against *relative to* what it depends on.**
A `witness` states one fact — these entries stood — and a partial meeting needs two: *these entries
stood* and *these are the ones that mattered*. The second is derivable and the first is not, so the
pair cannot be reconciled by computing harder. What is wanted is a record whose history claim
survives an entry arriving in front of it, and that is a shape rather than a check.

**Request 2 — a coordinate that is not a position.** Every difficulty here reduces to a decision
naming an entry and meaning *the prefix ending there*. Experiment 01 named this and was refused it;
experiment 05 gave it a second representation; experiment 11 found what the representation omits.
This experiment adds the fourth face: a prefix is not stable under a meeting, so a coordinate that
means a prefix cannot survive one.

**Request 3 — to record that a decision was carried rather than taken.** Three of four decisions
survive a meeting, and the record has no way to say that one of them arrived rather than was made
here. Related to 09's Request 3 and not the same: that one asks how to say *both of us decided this*,
and this asks how to say *this came from over there*.

**Request 4 — an operation whose result is allowed not to be a repository.** The honest reading of
Observation 4: a partial meeting is a **question**, and every operation the application has returns a
record. What it could return instead — the carried set, the left-over set, and nothing written — is a
shape nothing here has.

## What is deliberately not a request

**A looser witness.** Experiment 09 excluded it from the request, and this experiment is the reason
the exclusion was right: the witness never admits a changed world, so loosening it removes a
guarantee to buy something the loosening does not deliver.

**Recording what a decision depends on.** Observation 2. It answers *harmless* for every insertion,
so a record holding it would be holding a claim nothing can be caught by.
