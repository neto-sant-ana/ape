## The answer

The question was *can a record say that an insertion cannot have changed what a decision decided —
and is the agreement of two records actionable on that basis, without either of them promising less
about its own history?*

It can say it. The agreement is actionable. And the second clause is where the answer is:

> A `witness` states **what stood**, and a partial meeting changes what stood. So an operation that
> carries a decision past an insertion — however well justified, however unchanged the world — leaves
> that decision holding a claim that is no longer true of the record it sits in. Not unverifiable,
> which is what experiment 10 measured a merge leaving. False.

Two moves and no third: keep the witness and the record refuses itself; rewrite it and the record
says a prefix stood that did not, which is the one thing this laboratory has refused since experiment
02.

**And the derivation three results assumed would answer does not.** This is the finding that outlasts
the verdict and it corrects a reading rather than a result:

> An insertion is, by construction, an entry the receiving record never admitted, so nothing in that
> record resolves against it and no reach closure over any of its worlds contains it. **A derivation
> over what a decision depends on answers *harmless* for every insertion**, including the one that is
> not. What answers is re-derivation.

The reason is sharper than the measurement: what an inserted Event changes is `head_as_of`, which
depends on **no Event existing** between the recognized head and the instant. A closure holds what a
world reaches; it cannot hold an absence.

## Against each criterion

1. ***Unchanged* is defined before any phase runs**, as identity rather than answer, and both
   exclusions are produced: a Commitment insertion leaves the world identical, and the arrangement's
   literals require the changed world to differ from every world either record decided.
2. **The claim is derived from what each record already holds** — a coordinate, a journal and a
   decision — with neither writer consulted and nothing added to either record.
3. **The three refusals are reproduced by name** before anything changes: `Diverged` at the size of
   what the two share, `UnwitnessedKnowledge` on the union, and the agreed world held twice.
4. **Every state goes to the guards and to the reading**, and the closing phase puts the carried
   record to the application's own reader rather than to the laboratory's.
5. **Experiment 10's boundary is re-measured** rather than inherited, against a comparison beside the
   witness — and it holds for a reason 10 did not have: the witness is *sound and incomplete*.
6. **Both hazards were reached.** The Event before a non-Event coordinate is the finding. The address
   that omits the recording instant is Observation 3: it is what orders the union, and therefore what
   decides which record loses a world.
7. **Every literal was written before the run**, and none had to be corrected.
8. **Nothing was added to the APE engine, and nothing to the application.** `core/` and `cli/` are
   both untouched across this branch.
9. **Part B was not built**, against the protocol's own condition, and the absence is the result.
10. **The eleven earlier experiments' conclusions stand.** The one at risk held and gained a reason.
    Details below.

**Criteria 2 and 5 are the experiment**, and both hold.

**Failure conditions.** The severe one was not met: no state satisfied the derived justification and
was one no writer produced — every carried world is byte-for-byte the world its record recorded.
Its neighbour was not met either: *cannot have changed* was told from *did not happen to change* by
producing both, and the mutation below is what says the distinction was not free. Of the ordinary
ones: every capability's block was reproduced before it was unblocked; the boundary was re-measured;
the arrangement holds insertions of two kinds and the phase that would have been vacuous was found by
mutating it; no Part B was built; and this result says *these two records* rather than *records*.

**Seen red by mutating the subject.** Turning the receiving record's decision from a genesis into a
fork makes six phases fail, and the decisive one reports a partial meeting carrying **everything** —
because a fork inherits its parent's cut and no Event in front of it can move anything. That is the
arrangement that could not have failed, and the mutation is what found it rather than the docstring
that claimed it.

## Findings that outlast the verdict

**A dependence set cannot answer a question about an insertion.** The route three results assumed,
refuted for a structural reason rather than a measured one: an insertion is unreferenced by
construction, so no closure contains it.

**What a cut recognizes is a negative, and no closure holds a negative.** `head_as_of` is *the latest
Event recorded no later than that instant*, which is a fact about absence. Everything else about a
world is a fact about presence.

**The witness never admits a changed world.** Set equality is a sufficient condition for a world
surviving an insertion, and is not a necessary one. That is the strongest thing anybody has said about
the witness and it was not known: three experiments called it load-bearing and none of them had this.

**An Event settles a commitment into a world that never selected it.** The frozen past is closed over
the chain rather than over the selection, so an Event before a coordinate drags its commitment in.
Measured: 400 settled becomes 350.

**Recording order decides which of two parties loses a decision.** The record whose knowledge is dated
later is the one a meeting cannot carry. Third experiment in a row to find `recorded_at` deciding
something nobody chose it to decide.

**Agreement between two records is still only representable as repetition.** The union holds the
agreed decision twice, and nothing should deduplicate it — the coordinate each was taken at is what
pins it to a journal.

## What changed in the experiments before it

**Nothing was overturned, and one reading was.**

**Experiment 10's *the witness is load-bearing* stands, with a stronger reason.** It was argued from
the witness being the only claim whose subject is history. This adds that it is **sound**: it never
admits a world that moved. Both halves are needed and only the first had been said.

**Experiment 09's Requests 1, 2 and 3 are one candidate, and the fold was right — the remedy was
not.** 09 wrote *record a decision against what it depends on and a partial meeting becomes
statable*. A partial meeting is statable and dependence is not what states it, and the two halves of
that sentence come apart.

**Experiment 10's hazard is measured.** *An Event arriving before a non-Event coordinate moves a cut
and produces a world nobody decided* — handed over, not reached, and now the centre of this result.

**Experiment 11's finding gains a third face.** `recorded_at` belongs to no identity; a merge adopts
one party's; and it orders a union, so it decides who loses a decision.

## Variables the protocol left open

**What the operation is called, and where its seam is.** Unanswered by design, and now unanswerable
until Request 4 is: an operation whose result is not a repository has no shape in this application.

**Whether a partial meeting should be offered at all.** Not reached. What was measured is that it
cannot be recorded, which is upstream of wanting it.

**More than two records.** Excluded, and the reason to want it is unchanged.

**Cost.** Deferred by thirteen experiments.

## What the result does not cover

**Whether a differently shaped record could carry a meeting.** Observation 5's Requests 1 and 2. This
measured what the record as it stands can do, and every request is about the shape rather than the
check.

**Insertions that are neither a Commitment nor an Event.** An eligibility, a statement, a resource —
none was inserted. The reference argument covers them; the cut argument does not, and only an Event
moves a cut.

**A meeting between more than two, a network, a transport.** Excluded.

**Benchmarking.** No timing.

# Architectural Consequences

## Established for the CLI

* **A partial meeting is computable and not recordable.** Every carried decision's witness becomes
  false, and the two ways out are a record that refuses itself or one that misstates what stood.
* **The witness is sound about worlds**: it never admits a world that moved under an insertion.
* **A dependence set cannot answer whether an insertion mattered**, because an insertion is
  unreferenced by construction.
* **Only an Event can change a decision that resolves its own cut**, and only a decision that
  resolves its own cut can be changed by an insertion. A fork is immune by construction.
* **Recording order decides which of two records a meeting can carry whole.**

## Candidates for later experiments

* **A coordinate that is not a position** — the fourth arrival of the same face, now with a reason a
  prefix cannot survive a meeting. Named by 01, 05, 11 and this one.
* **An operation whose result is not a repository** — Request 4, and the shape everything else here
  needs.
* **Recording that a decision was carried rather than taken** — Request 3, distinct from 09's.
* **Authenticity**, still named by every result since 02, and still argued by its own material.
* **Whether an address should say when the entry was recorded** — queued by 11, and this experiment
  did not need it: the union's order is decided by `recorded_at` whether or not the address carries
  it.
* **Cost as an absolute**, deferred by thirteen experiments.
