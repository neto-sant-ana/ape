# Observation 4 — The meeting computes, and its result is not a repository

Everything above says the operation is available. This is what it costs, and it is not a guarantee
traded for a capability — it is that the result cannot be written down.

A carried decision holds a `witness`: the set of entries that stood when it was taken. After a
meeting the journal holds more than that in front of its coordinate. So the field is not
unverifiable, which is what experiment 10 measured a merge leaving behind. **It is false.**

Measured, and by the record's own reader rather than by an argument: take the union without the
Event — the insertion re-derivation says is harmless, where **every** world the receiving record
decided survives by identity — write the three files, and read them back.

```text
UnwitnessedKnowledge — entry 5a7f25a3… was admitted, and the decision was not taken against it
```

The repository refuses itself.

## Two moves and no third

```text
keep the witness      the record is written and cannot be read. Every carried decision is
                      refused by the guard that reads it, on a journal where no world moved

rewrite the witness   the record reads, and says a prefix stood that did not. That is the one
                      thing this laboratory has refused since experiment 02 — a derived value
                      written to agree with what it is compared against stops being a comparison
```

There is no third because the witness is not a check on the world. Its subject is the repository's
**history**, which experiment 10 established is the only such claim a record holds, and a partial
meeting changes history by construction: putting an entry in front of a coordinate is exactly the
thing the witness exists to notice.

## So M4 and M5 are both refuted, and precisely

**M4** predicted that admitting an insertion on a derived justification would not reopen what
experiment 10 measured, because the witness would still be there and still tested for equality. The
witness is still there and it refuses — so what M4 called safety is the operation not happening. An
operation that only succeeds where the witness already agreed is not the capability 09 asked for.

**M5** predicted the disease came from **removing** the record of what stood rather than from
weighing an insertion against a derivation, and that the two are different operations however similar
their admission tables look. They are different operations with one consequence: any operation that
puts an entry in front of a coordinate falsifies a witness, whether it reached that state by
discarding the claim or by justifying past it.

> The disease was never in the comparison. It is in the **shape of the record**: a decision states
> what stood, and a meeting changes what stood.

Which is why M4 with M5 was written down as the experiment before anything ran. Phase 3 works, is
quick, and is not the answer.
