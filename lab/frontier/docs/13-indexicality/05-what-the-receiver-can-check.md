# Observation 5 — What the receiver can check, as a closed table

Every part of a completed pin, put to *what would the receiving record compare this against?* The
space is what a `Taken` carries, the attribution beside it, and what the laboratory added to complete
it. Each part is moved and each receiver is asked.

```text
                                                   own      sibling    foreign
  nothing moved                                   silent    silent     unfound
  the coordinate                                  unfound   unfound    unfound
  an entry out of the witness                     refused   refused    unfound
  an entry into the witness                       refused   refused    unfound
  the decider, to a party the journal lacks       refused   refused    unfound
  the decider, to the other party of the record   silent    silent     unfound
```

`refused` means the receiver weighed the part against something it derived and said what disagreed.
`silent` means it produced a world and had nothing to weigh the part against. `unfound` means it
reached no entry of that name.

## Two cells were predicted `refused` and measured `unfound`

The correction is the sharper half of the phase, so it is reported rather than adjusted.

A moved coordinate is not weighed against anything. The replay walks to an address and does not
arrive, and that is `UnknownEntry` — **the same refusal, by the same name, that a record holding
nothing gives a pin that is entirely true.**

Measured directly, against each other:

```text
the right journal and a false coordinate    UnknownEntry, naming the coordinate the pin holds
a true pin and the foreign journal          UnknownEntry, naming the coordinate the pin holds
```

Each names an address, which is the half of the pair that was **not** in doubt. A reader is told the
coordinate and needs to be told the journal. The refusal a coordinate produces is itself indexical,
which is the diagnosis this experiment is named for arriving in the error type.

## The last column is a constant

That is I4, stated as a measurement rather than as a claim about a capability. A record that answers
every version of a decision identically — the true one included — has not checked one. There is
nothing in the column to distinguish anything with.

## The one thing a record holding nothing can check is the pin against itself

A coordinate outside its own witness describes no journal at all, and saying so needs no journal.
Measured: the pin as written does not contradict itself, a pin missing its own coordinate does, and a
pin whose instants are simply untrue does **not**.

That third line is the whole of what the check is worth. It separates **self-contradictory** from
**false** and reaches nothing further, which is the distance experiment 01 measured, arriving where a
stranger can stand.

**It was weighed against the severe failure condition and does not meet it.** The condition was *some
part of a completed pin turns out to be checkable by a record that never held the journal*, and the
premise at risk was *what a record checks, it derives; what it derives, it holds*. What is derived
here is a relation between two parts of the pin, and the record holds both parts because the pin
handed them over. So I5 is not contradicted — it is sharpened: **the only thing a stranger derives
from a pin is the pin.**

## A recording instant is weighed against a world, and only where it moves one

The row that is silent in every column is the one the pin had to add, and this is why.

```text
moved within the cut, worlds rebuilt      nothing refuses; the record answers 400
                                          and now claims a fact was learned on a day it was not

moved across the cut, worlds kept         WorldDisagrees — "the chain it recognizes"

moved across the cut, worlds rebuilt      nothing refuses; the record answers 0
```

Three states, one value moved in each. The middle one is the only refusal, and it is not a check on
the instant: it is the worlds file disagreeing about a **chain** that was derived from the instant
earlier. The first shows what that costs — an instant that moves without moving a world is weighed
against nothing. The third shows what it is worth — rewriting the derived value, which is what any
writer does, erases the only thing that noticed.

**I5 holds, with the addendum this experiment came for.** Every guard in the record weighs a stored
value against one computed from what is present. A recording instant is computed from nothing, so it
is reachable only through a world derived from it, and only while that world is still there to
disagree.

## The two halves meet

Observation 3 measured that the pin determines the reference at the stage that carries recording
instants, and not before. This measures that a recording instant is the one value in a repository
that nothing derives.

**The single thing a reference must add in order to be fixed is the single thing nothing can ever
check.** That is not two findings next to each other: the same value answers both, and neither half
alone says it. Experiment 11 found the value; it found it by asking what a merge fails to notice, and
concluded about two journals. What is new is that a reference has no other candidate — the space was
searched over all nine kinds of entry, and the only other thing that escapes a witness is order,
which was measured to change nothing.

So the second line of the hypothesis holds, and holds for a stronger reason than it was written for.
It said a pin is unshareable because a receiver cannot derive a foreign prefix. The measurement says
the receiver's position is not special: **nobody** can derive the instants, including the record they
came from.
