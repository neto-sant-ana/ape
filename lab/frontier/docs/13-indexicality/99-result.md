## The answer

The question was *can a decision name the knowledge it was taken against in a way that survives the
journal changing under it — and if it can, is what it then holds a claim the record it arrives in can
check?*

It can. It is not.

> A pin is **portable exactly as far as the content already is**, and the last thing it carries is
> unverifiable by everyone. So a travelling decision is believed rather than checked — and the record's
> present answer to one arriving from elsewhere is to **refuse**, which the measurement says is
> correct rather than conservative.

Four results asked for a portable coordinate. What they were asking for exists, costs one instant per
witnessed entry, and buys a reference that no receiver can weigh. That is not a reason not to build it;
it is the price, written down, which is what this experiment was for.

## What outlasts the verdict

**The refusal a coordinate produces is itself indexical.** Two cells of the audit were predicted
`refused` and measured `unfound`, and the correction is the sharper half:

> A record holding the right journal and a false coordinate, and a record holding nothing and a true
> one, are refused by the same guard with the same name — `UnknownEntry`, each naming an address. A
> reader is handed the half of the pair that was never in doubt, and needs to be told the other half.

**And *produces the same world* does not mean *resolves correctly*.** Experiment 12's harmless
insertion, kept here as an instrument, is a journal offering knowledge the decision was not taken
against that produces the decision's world by identity. Which is why the final stage of the pin buys
nothing: on top of a determined reference there is nothing left to determine, and on its own it
determines the answer rather than the question.

## Against each criterion

1. ***Resolves correctly* is defined before any phase runs**, and the three weaker readings are each
   **produced**: *resolves at all* by the moved coordinate, *produces a world* by the sibling reading
   back cleanly, and *produces the same world* by the fifth candidate.
2. **What escapes the present pin is measured**, over all nine kinds of entry and two-sided — the
   instant moved leaves the address, every other field moves it — and not inferred from experiment 11.
3. **The pin is completed in stages and each stage is attributed**, by what it still admits out of five
   candidates. The determining stage was falsified on purpose and reports the row above its own.
4. **The receiving record is founded apart and holds neither journal — verified**, by intersecting the
   address sets, and founded apart in *content* because content is what an address is derived from.
5. **Every part of the pin is put to the question**, as a closed table over three receivers, and the
   answer *nothing* is reported as an answer — the recording instant's row is silent in every column,
   and the stranger's column is silent for every row.
6. **Every literal was written before the run**, and two were wrong. Both are corrected in the open and
   named as corrections.
7. **Nothing was added to the APE engine.**
8. **Part B was built only against both criteria**, and its absence is the result in Observation 6.
9. **The twelve earlier conclusions stand.** *The witness is the only claim whose subject is history*
   was the one at risk and is unmoved: this measures what that claim omits, which is what 11 already
   said it omits, and adds that a reference has no other candidate to omit.

## What this leaves for the row

The stand-by condition named this question as what the agents row waits on: *the shape changes and
settles, or it is refused with a published reason.* It is refused, and the reason is here.

The authenticity candidate arrives with a boundary rather than a want. It is not *how does a record
trust a stranger's claim* — it is narrower: the only claim in a travelling decision that holding the
content cannot settle is **when each entry was learned**, and that is what a key would be answering.
