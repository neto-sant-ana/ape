# Indexicality

## Abstract

Four results have asked one question from four sides, and the fourth of them named why the other
three could not settle it.

```text
01 divergence      a coordinate that named the STATE of knowledge rather than a position in
                   it would be checkable — named, and refused, because it is a derived value

05 coordination    made that move for an unrelated reason: `Taken::witness`, a second
                   representation of the same fact, in the form of a set

11 veracity        found what the second representation omits — no identity carries a
                   recording instant, so two journals can be equal entry for entry and not
                   be the same journal

12 commensurability  found why the whole family resists: a coordinate means *the prefix
                   ending here*, and a prefix is not stable under a meeting
```

`Taken::after` is a stable **token** — content-addressed, resolvable, comparable — whose **referent**
depends on the journal it is read against. That is what an indexical is, and it is the diagnosis
rather than a metaphor: experiment 12 measured a decision whose coordinate resolved, whose witness
matched, and whose world moved, and a decision whose coordinate resolved, whose witness did **not**
match, and whose world did not move. The token behaved; the reference did not.

---

## Question

**Can a decision name the knowledge it was taken against in a way that survives the journal changing
under it — and if it can, is what it then holds a claim the record it arrives in can check?**

The second clause is the experiment. Every face of this question asks for the reference to be pinned;
none of them has asked what a pinned reference is worth to somebody who was not there.

---

## What *resolves correctly* means here, decided before any phase runs

The two experiments before this one each had to settle a word before their first phase, and both
found the definition doing more work than the phases. The word here is **resolves**.

> **A coordinate resolves correctly in a record when the knowledge it selects there is the knowledge
> it selected in the record it was taken in.**

Three weaker things it deliberately does **not** mean, and each has already been measured to come
apart from it:

```text
resolves at all       the address is present and `replay_through` reaches it. Experiment 01
                      tampered with exactly this and nothing complained

produces a world      the decision is admissible against what it reaches. Experiment 12's
                      union produced a world for every decision in it

produces the SAME     experiment 12's own word, and it is strictly weaker: a coordinate can
world                 resolve to the wrong prefix and produce the right world, which is what
                      the harmless insertion measured. `unchanged` is downstream of this
```

The last is the one that matters. This experiment's word is about the **knowledge selected**, not
about the world produced, and 12 is the reason the two have to be kept apart.

---

## Hypothesis

```text
the reference is fixable      the prefix a coordinate means is a set, and the record already
                              writes that set down. The witness is the pin experiment 01
                              asked for, arrived by another road

and pinning it is what        a pin travels with the decision, and a record that receives one
makes it unshareable          has to believe it: a foreign prefix is not derivable here, so
                              the check the pin buys at home is exactly the check it cannot
                              buy away
```

The two lines point at opposite answers. If the first holds and the second does not, four results'
worth of circling ends with the record already holding what it needs. If both hold, a coordinate is
fixable and a fixed coordinate is not portable — and the row's remaining questions about two records
are asking for something a record cannot give.

---

## Pre-registered predictions

Five, written before anything runs, lettered **I**.

**I1 — the witness pins the reference completely.**

*Prediction:* given a decision's witness, the knowledge its coordinate selects is fully determined —
two journals satisfying one witness cannot offer different knowledge. The witness is a set of
content-addressed entries, and an Event's identity contains its predecessor, so even order is pinned
wherever order is meaning.

*Refuted if* two journals satisfy one witness and the decision resolves against different knowledge
in them.

**I2 — and what escapes the pin, if anything, is what no identity carries.**

*Prediction:* stated separately from I1 so that a refutation is informative rather than merely
negative. Experiment 11 measured that `recorded_at` belongs to no identity; if I1 fails, this is
where.

*Refuted if* something else escapes, or if nothing does.

**I3 — a completed pin makes a decision resolvable in a record that is not its own.**

*Prediction:* a decision carrying enough to determine its own reference can be read by another
record and answered for, without that record holding the journal it was taken in.

*Refuted if* a completed pin is still not enough — if resolving needs the original journal itself
rather than a description of it.

**I4 — and the receiving record cannot check any of it.**

*Prediction:* everything the pin adds is a **claim**. The receiving record can verify a claim only
against something it derives, and it derives nothing about a journal it does not hold. So a portable
coordinate is believed rather than checked, which is the authenticity candidate arriving from inside
the record rather than from a key.

*Refuted if* some part of the pin is checkable by a record that never held the journal. **This is the
one worth being wrong about**, and the largest finding available here.

**I5 — because what a record checks, it derives; and what it derives, it holds.**

*Prediction:* stated as the reason so it can be wrong separately. Every guard measured in this row —
the coordinate against the journal, the witness against the prefix, the worlds file against the
decisions — weighs a stored value against one computed **from what is present**. Nothing weighs a
value against something absent.

*Refuted if* a guard exists that checks a claim about knowledge the record does not hold.

**I3 with I4 is the experiment.** I3 says the pin can be completed; I4 says what a completed pin is
worth to a stranger. Either alone is half: a pin nobody can use is not portability, and a check
nobody needs is not a cost.

## What is carried forward, and is not available as a finding

* **A coordinate is a claim about the past and the record holds nothing to check it against** — 01.
* **The witness is the only claim in a repository whose subject is its history** — 10.
* **The witness never admits a changed world**, and set equality is sufficient and not necessary —
  12.
* **A dependence set cannot answer a question about an insertion**, because an insertion is
  unreferenced by construction — 12.
* **What a cut recognizes is a negative**, and no closure holds a negative — 12.
* **No identity carries a recording instant**, so two journals can be equal entry for entry and not
  be the same journal — 11.
* **A partial meeting computes and its result is not a repository**, because a carried decision's
  witness becomes false — 12.
* **Two records agree about a world exactly where they agreed about the knowledge under it** — 09.

Before any sentence here is allowed to be a finding, ask what would have to be false for it to be
false.

## Motivation

It goes now because both axes the queue names agree on it, and because they are different questions
that happened to have one answer rather than one confirming the other.

**By ripeness**, it has four arrivals and each sharpened it: 01 named the move and was refused it, 05
made it for another reason, 11 found what it omits, 12 found why the family resists. Only *saying two
lines agree* has more, and that item was run as experiment 12 and its shape moved.

**By what it costs if deferred**, it is the item that would change what a `Taken` is — the type every
subject in both rows writes — so everything built on the present shape is rework. That is why the
agents row's stand-by now names this question rather than a count of quiet experiments.

And it goes before authenticity for the reason three results have given, with one addition: I4
predicts this experiment reaches authenticity's boundary **from inside**, which would be a better
argument for running that candidate than any it currently has.

---

## Experimental Boundary

This experiment asks what a coordinate must carry, and what carrying it is worth.

It includes:

* measuring what the present pin determines and what escapes it;
* completing the pin, in the laboratory, far enough to test I3 — which is **not** a repair and is not
  proposed for the application;
* handing a pinned decision to a record that never held its journal, and measuring what that record
  can and cannot say about it;
* every state put to the three guards, as a closed table.

It deliberately excludes:

* **changing `Taken` in the application.** Whether the record should carry a completed pin is Part B,
  decided by the phases, and a laboratory-side pin is an instrument rather than a proposal;
* **a key, a signature, an anchor outside the record.** That is the authenticity candidate, and I4
  reaching its boundary is a finding rather than a licence to cross it;
* **more than two records, the network, a transport.** Excluded throughout the row;
* **the engine**, and **benchmarking.**

---

## Experimental Subject

**A subject of this experiment's own.**

What it must express:

```text
one decision, two journals    the same coordinate resolving against knowledge that differs,
that satisfy one witness      with the witness satisfied in both — or the demonstration that
                              no such pair exists, which is I1 holding

a receiving record that       a third record, founded apart, holding neither journal, to
never held either journal     which a pinned decision is handed. Without it I4 has nothing
                              to be about

a pin the laboratory can      completed step by step, so that what each addition buys is
complete in stages            measured rather than the whole being reported as sufficient

answers that differ           every journal, every record and every resolution answers a
                              different number, written as literals before the run
```

The first is where a subject could quietly make the experiment un-failable in **either** direction.
If the two journals are trivially distinguishable, I1 holds for a reason about the subject; if they
differ in something no coordinate could ever be about, I1 fails the same way. So the pair differs in
exactly one thing, it is named before the run, and both journals are legitimate records that a
writer could have produced.

## Procedure

### Phase 0 — What the present pin determines

Read once. What a coordinate plus a witness fixes about the knowledge a decision selects, and every
answer as a literal.

### Phase 1 — What escapes it

I1 and I2. Construct the two journals, or establish that they cannot exist. Put both to the record's
own reader and record what it says.

### Phase 2 — Completing the pin, in stages

Each addition, and what it buys, measured one at a time. A phase reporting that a completed pin works
without saying which part did the work would be reporting an arrangement.

### Phase 3 — Handing it to a stranger

I3. A record that never held the journal, given the decision and the pin, answering for it.

### Phase 4 — What the stranger can check

I4 and I5. Every part of the pin, put to the question *what would the receiving record compare this
against?* — as a closed table, and the closed part matters more than the table.

### Phase 5 — The three guards

Every state to the coordinate, the witness and the worlds file, and to the reading.

### Phase 6 — Name what an application would need, and build none of it

Requests, verbatim, in the vocabulary of the need.

### Phase 7 — Part B, if it was earned

A repair whose shape is decided by Phases 1 to 5 and named nowhere above. It stays only if it meets
the inherited criterion:

> **It removes a state a reader can be misled by, and what the repair replaces survives.**

And this experiment's own, which is the sharpest version of a condition the row keeps needing:

> **A repair here changes what a `Taken` is, and a `Taken` is what every concluded experiment in both
> rows wrote.** So Part B is refused unless the phases show the present shape admitting a state a
> reader can be misled by — not merely failing to offer something four results have wanted. The
> difference is that the first is a defect and the second is an absence, and this row has published
> eleven results whose answer was an absence.

---

## Success Criteria

1. *Resolves correctly* is defined before any phase runs, and the three weaker readings are each
   produced rather than asserted.
2. What escapes the present pin is measured, not inferred from experiment 11's result.
3. The pin is completed in stages, and what each stage buys is attributed to that stage.
4. The receiving record is founded apart and holds neither journal — verified, not arranged.
5. Every part of the pin is put to *what would the receiving record compare this against?*, and the
   answer *nothing* is reported as an answer.
6. Every literal is written before the run.
7. Nothing is added to the APE engine.
8. Part B is built only against both criteria above, and its absence is a reportable result.
9. The twelve earlier experiments' conclusions stand, or the change is recorded as a result of this
   one. *The witness is the only claim whose subject is history* is the one at risk.

**Criteria 2 and 5 are the experiment.**

---

## Failure Conditions

The severe one:

> If some part of a completed pin turns out to be checkable by a record that never held the journal,
> then I4 and I5 are wrong together and this row has been drawing a boundary in the wrong place since
> experiment 02. That is reported as a failure of a **premise**, and every result that leaned on
> *what a record checks, it derives* is listed.

Its neighbour, which is this experiment's own:

> If the pin cannot be completed at all — if resolving a coordinate needs the journal itself and no
> description of it will do — then the four faces are asking for something that does not exist, and
> the finding is why. That is a real possibility: experiment 12 measured that what a cut recognizes
> is a **negative**, and a description that has to carry an absence has no obvious size.

And the ordinary ones:

* a pin reported as sufficient without the stages being separated;
* the receiving record holding, by construction, something a real stranger would not;
* *the stranger cannot check it* reported where what was measured is *this stranger did not*;
* a Part B built because four results wanted it, which is the condition above;
* the two journals differing in something no coordinate could be about, which would make I1 fail for
  a reason about the subject.

**A refuted I1 is the expected result and the cheap one.** What makes the experiment worth running is
I3 with I4, and a subject that stopped at I1 would have measured experiment 11 again.

## Methodological Constraint

> *Do not introduce an abstraction intended to solve an experiment that has not yet been performed.*

The previous experiments' conclusions are not revised here; where this one finds something that would
have changed them, it is recorded as a finding of this experiment, against the implementation as it
then stood.

And the rules the last seven experiments earned, which apply without amendment. Before recording
anything as a finding, ask what would have to be false for it to be false. Every literal is written
before the run, and a wrong prediction is corrected in the open rather than adjusted. A prediction's
justification must quantify over as much as its claim does. A friction is evidence of a want, not of
a need. A phase must not be satisfied by an arrangement it could not have failed. A negative result
must name the space it searched. A capability several results asked for is still a want.

## Expected Pressure Points

### The pin will grow until it is the journal

Each thing that escapes invites one more field, and the honest end of that road is a decision
carrying a copy of the knowledge it was taken against. Phase 2 separates the stages so that the point
where the pin stops being a description is visible rather than arrived at.

### *Checkable* will be asked to stretch

From *the record can weigh this against something it derives* to *the record can tell whether this is
well formed*. Experiment 01 already measured the distance between those two, and its whole finding
was that well-formed and false are not distinguishable.

### The answer may be a boundary, and boundaries read as failures

Four results wanted this and the likely outcome is that the thing they wanted costs a guarantee they
also want. That is a result. It is also the shape most likely to be argued past at Phase 7, which is
why the Part B condition above is written before anything runs.

### The subject is the risk here more than in any experiment of this row

Everything turns on two journals that satisfy one witness. If they cannot be built, I1 holds
trivially and the experiment is over at Phase 1; if they are built carelessly, they differ in
something a coordinate was never about. Phase 1 is where this experiment is most likely to fool
itself, and the arrangement's own literals are the only guard against it.

## Experimental Principle

Reconstruction asked whether meaning survives.

Divergence asked whether deliberation survives.

Corroboration asked whether the record can be trusted without being believed.

Convergence asked whether two lines of deliberation can still reach each other.

Provenance asked whether a record that can be rebuilt perfectly is thereby understood.

Coordination asked whether a record built for one mind can hold two.

Exploration asked what a record owes the worlds nobody chose.

Atomicity asked who the record's promises are to.

Contention asked whether a promise made to one writer is a promise at all when there are two.

Collision asked whether two records that have never met were already the same record.

Witness asked whether a decision is about everything it happened to come after.

Veracity asked whether a record that agrees with itself has thereby told the truth.

Commensurability asked whether two records that disagree have a common measure anyway.

```text
This one asks whether a decision can say where
it stood to somebody who was not standing there.
```

Experiment 01 asked for a coordinate that named a state rather than a position, and was told it could
not persist a derived value. Four experiments later the record persists two, and the question has not
moved: what it means to name a place is still a question about who is reading. If the answer is that a
reference can be carried, the row's remaining questions about two records become buildable. If it is
that carrying it turns a check into a claim, then corroboration has a horizon, and it is the edge of
the record rather than the edge of the writer.
