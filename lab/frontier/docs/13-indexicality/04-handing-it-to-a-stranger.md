# Observation 4 — Handing it to a stranger, and what a stranger turns out to be

Three receivers, ordered by how much they hold. That ordering is the experiment's instrument, because
what separates them is not distance or ownership but **content**.

```text
own        the record the decision was taken in
sibling    holds every entry by address; one recording instant differs
foreign    founded apart: its own vocabulary, parties, fund
```

The third is verified rather than arranged. Two records that admit the same content hold the same
addresses whoever wrote them — the collision experiment measured it — so being a stranger is a
property of what was admitted. The phase intersects the address sets and finds them disjoint.

## A record that holds nothing cannot resolve the decision at any stage

```text
coordinate  witnessed  dated  produced        the journal holds no entry the coordinate names
```

Four stages, one answer. The completed pin does not help, and cannot: an address is derived from
content one way, so a receiver that cannot write the entry cannot produce the entry from its name.
Even the dated pin leaves the foreign journal untouched, because it names no address that journal
holds.

**I3 is refuted for a receiver that holds no content.** A pin is a description of a journal the
receiver has to already be able to write.

## The receiver that holds the content resolves it, and resolves it wrongly

The sibling reaches the coordinate, satisfies the witness, produces a world, and answers `0` where
the decision's own record answers `400`. Nothing refuses. The knowledge the coordinate selects there
is not the knowledge it selected.

This is the state the experiment came for, and the present pin does not exclude it.

## Given the instants, the same receiver resolves it correctly — by rewriting its own past

Handed the dated pin, the sibling can build the journal the pin describes: its own entries, the pin's
instants. Replayed, the coordinate selects the knowledge it selected, and the decision answers `400`.

**I3 is confirmed, and only for a receiver that already holds the content.** The pin supplies the
instants; the receiver supplies every entry. So a reference is portable exactly as far as the content
already is — which is experiment 09's boundary, arriving from the other side. 09 measured that two
records agree about a world exactly where they agreed about the knowledge under it; this measures
that a decision travels exactly that far too, and not one entry further.

## What the receiver did in order to resolve it is the finding

It rewrote its own history.

The sibling did not learn the Event on day 4. It learned it on day 9, and its journal says so
truthfully. To answer for the decision it had to construct a journal that is **not its own record of
what it knew**, out of entries it holds and instants it was told. Nothing in that construction is
checked, and nothing could be: an instant is derived from nothing, so a receiver that re-dates itself
to match a pin has believed it.

Which makes the shape of the second hypothesis visible before the audit measures it. The stage that
made the reference portable is the stage that made the receiver's own record of the past irrelevant
to it, and there is no version of that which the receiver can verify.

## What a receiver holding *part* would do is not measured

The three receivers hold everything, everything-but-one-instant, and nothing. A receiver sharing a
base and diverging after it is the shape experiment 12 arranged, and this experiment did not build
one. So *the checkable fraction is the held fraction* is a reading the three points are consistent
with and do not establish. It is named in Observation 7 as a request rather than reported here as a
result.
