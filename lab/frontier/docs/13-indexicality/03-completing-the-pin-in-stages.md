# Observation 3 — Completing the pin in stages, and what each stage buys

A pin determines the reference when **every** journal satisfying it offers the same knowledge. That
is a claim about a family, so the measurement is over the five candidates rather than over an
argument, and each stage is weighed by what it still admits.

```text
                    candidates    bodies of      worlds
                     admitted     knowledge     produced
  coordinate            5             3            2
  witnessed             4             2            2
  dated                 2             1            1
  produced              2             1            1
```

Read the middle column. **The pin determines the reference at the third stage and not before**, and
the stage that does it is the one that carries a recording instant for every witnessed entry.

Each row is falsifiable and one of them was falsified on purpose: making the third stage carry no
instants turns it into `("dated", 4, 2, 2)`, which is the witnessed row wearing another name. So the
column is measuring the pin and not the literal beside it.

## What each stage buys, attributed

**The coordinate alone admits five and offers three bodies of knowledge.** It is an address, and an
address is satisfied by any journal that holds the entry. This is experiment 01's finding produced
rather than restated: a coordinate is a claim about the past and nothing weighs it.

**The witness excludes one candidate and no ambiguity.** It drops the insertion — a Commitment
nothing selects, admitted before the coordinate, which the prefix set catches — and leaves the two
bodies of knowledge that matter untouched. It is the strongest thing the record holds today and it
does not reach the question.

**The instants determine.** Two candidates survive, they are the same journal in two admissible
orders, and they offer one body of knowledge. The reference is fixable, which is the first line of
the hypothesis, and what fixes it is the value experiment 11 measured no address to carry.

**Naming the world buys nothing.** The satisfying set does not shrink, the bodies of knowledge do not
shrink, and the worlds do not. On top of a determined reference there is nothing left to determine.

## And naming the world, on its own, names the wrong thing

The last row would be uninteresting if it only said *redundant*. It says something else, and the
fifth candidate is what says it:

```text
inserted-early and early    one world, by identity
                            two bodies of knowledge under it
```

A pin that named only the world a decision produced would be satisfied by a journal whose knowledge
is not the one the decision was taken against. **So *produces the same world* cannot stand in for
*resolves correctly*** — which is the distinction the protocol settled before any phase ran, and this
is where it earns its keep rather than being a definitional preference.

It is also why the fifth candidate exists. Experiment 12 found the harmless insertion by asking what
a dependence set can notice; here it is an instrument, and what it holds down is the temptation to
measure a reference by its answer.

## The pressure point, and where the pin stopped

The protocol predicted the pin would grow until it was the journal. It grew for three stages and then
stopped, and the stopping point is visible: the fourth addition changed nothing measurable.

But the honest reading of the third stage is not *the pin is small*. A dated pin carries an instant
for **every** entry that stood — twelve of them here, and a prefix has no bound. It is a description
whose size is the size of the history it describes. What it is not is a **copy**: it carries no
content, which is exactly why the next phase finds it unusable by anyone who does not already have
the content.
