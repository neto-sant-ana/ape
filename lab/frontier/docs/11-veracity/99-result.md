## The answer

The question was *is there a state this record can reach by accident that passes every check it has,
is nobody's forgery, and answers a question falsely?* There is one, and it lives where the hypothesis
said it would — in the recording instant — by a route the hypothesis did not have:

> **Falsity does not need an invented value. It needs an invented combination.** Every field of the
> merged record was written by somebody: the journal is one party's entire, and each decision is the
> other party's verbatim — its coordinate, its witness, its selection, its instant, its claim of who
> decided. What nobody wrote is the **pairing**.

Which is why an audit of fields could not have found it, and why V1's question — *what does nothing
check* — was the wrong shape. The right one is narrower: *what does nothing check, and what can reach
it without a hand?*

And the mechanism, stated so that it is checkable:

> An `EntryId` is derived from what admitting produced, and no identity in the engine contains a
> recording instant. So two journals can be **equal entry for entry and not be the same journal**.
> `converge` compares by address, the witness is a set of addresses, and a merge writes its own
> `worlds.json` — which is the one thing weighing a recording instant. Three guards, and the one
> field none of them reaches is the one that decides which Event a cut resolves to.

The sharpest form is about `Taken` itself:

> `Taken` records **where** a decision was taken and **what stood there**, and both survive the merge
> intact while the answer moves. The two things the record keeps in order to say *this decision was
> taken against this knowledge* are both true and both insufficient, because *this knowledge* is
> identified by address, and an address does not carry when it was learned.

**Part B was built**, and it is one comparison: `appended` weighs the recording instant of the shared
prefix and refuses with a name of its own. Its scope is settled by the addressing rather than by
judgement — every other field is in the identity, so two journals agreeing by address can differ in
the instant and in nothing else. Observation 8, including the one place it exceeds what was measured.

## Against each criterion

1. ***False* is defined before any phase runs**, and an instance of each exclusion is produced rather
   than asserted: **incomplete** is an interrupted write and the mixture experiment 07 named;
   **differently arranged** is two contemporaneous commitments swapped, a different file answering all
   four worlds identically; **refused** is every calibration and five of the six mixtures.
2. **The faithful record is constructed by the arrangement**, and every level of every generation,
   party and mixture is a literal asserted before the run — including the requirement that the two
   generations answer differently, without which an interrupted write could not have been false.
3. **Tampering appears only as calibration**, labelled at the top of Observation 2 and in every test
   name that uses it. The finding rests on `converge` and on the laboratory's own merge, and no hand
   edits a file in either.
4. **The generators are enumerated before the search** — Observation 4, six of them — and Observation 6
   says which were exhausted, which sampled, and which not reached, along with the three nearest
   unmeasured states.
5. **Every state goes to all three guards and to the reading**, reported as a closed table per
   generator.
6. **Every literal was written before the run.** One was wrong: the mixture phase predicted two states
   would answer and three do. Corrected in the open, in Observation 5 — the third is experiment 07's
   own finding, which the prediction had forgotten rather than contradicted.
7. **Nothing was added to the APE engine.** `core/` is untouched across the branch.
8. **Part B was built against both criteria**, and against the scope condition this protocol added.
9. **The ten earlier experiments' conclusions stand**, and the one at risk is narrowed rather than
   overturned. Below.

**Criteria 1 and 4 are the experiment**, and both hold.

**Failure conditions.** The severe one **was met**, and is the result. Its neighbour was not: *false*
was told from *differently arranged* by producing both, and the definition did the work it was written
to do. Of the ordinary ones: no state produced by tampering is reported as an accident; the space is
named; the arrangement's generations, parties and mixtures were required to answer differently before
the run and do; the repair closes the state rather than the class; and this result says *these six
generators reached it* rather than *the record is unsafe*.

## What the premise failure costs, and what it does not

*Corroboration proves a repository agrees with itself* is **true and was never the claim being leaned
on**. What eleven experiments have implicitly relied on is stronger:

```text
what was proved       a repository that agrees with itself was not edited after it was written
what was assumed      and was therefore written by processes that agree about what they knew
```

The second does not follow, and the gap between them is one field wide. Every conclusion below is
narrowed by that and none is withdrawn.

* **02-corroboration — *the record can be trusted without being believed*.** Holds for **one** record.
  A merged record is trusted for what two records agreed about by address, which is not everything
  they agreed about.
* **05-coordination, 03-convergence, 08-contention — *the witness is load-bearing*.** Stands, and
  gains its limit: it is load-bearing **over addresses**, and the one thing an address omits is the one
  thing a merge adopts.
* **08-contention** asked this question in almost these words and did not answer it. It is answered
  now, and its own generator was the right one and the wrong grain: two writers, yes — but the state
  is left by the **merge**, not by the interleaving, and 08 enumerated interleavings.
* **07-atomicity** asked it too, and its surviving partial state is **incomplete**. Correct, and
  unfalsifiable as it was written, because the word was not defined.
* **09-collision — *two records founded independently agree about a world exactly where they agreed
  about the knowledge under it*.** Narrowed, and in the direction that flatters it: a world's identity
  is a function of its cut, and a cut is a function of the instant, so two records that disagree about
  a day already disagree about the world. `holds` would have reported *they do not agree*. The
  agreement that operation reports is sound; it is the merge that is not.
* **10-witness, Observation 11.** Measured that a merge dissolves the witness's checkability under a
  **narrowed** witness, and asked for a second comparison. The broad witness has the same disease and
  a worse form: not dissolved but silently satisfied.

## Findings that outlast the verdict

**An address is content-addressed over everything except when the content was learned.** The one field
of an admission that belongs to no identity, and therefore the one field two journals can differ in
while every comparison over addresses reports agreement.

**A recording instant is unchecked; its consequence is written down twice.** The one derived value it
decides is `event_head`, and that is a field of `worlds.json`. Which closes it for every record that
writes that file from a comparison, and not for the one operation that writes it from its own
derivation.

**Recording is monotonic within a journal and unbounded across two.** The watermark refuses an instant
that overtakes a later entry's — measured, the same tamper a fifteen-entry journal accepts a
seventeen-entry one refuses. Two parties whose journals agree entry for entry have no neighbours to
bound them against each other, and the merge is the only operation that puts two of them side by side.

**A refusal that cannot name what disagrees is worse than no refusal.** Mutating `appended` to see the
instant produced `Diverged { expected: EntryId("7a94…"), found: EntryId("7a94…") }` — the same address
on both sides. The hole drawn in a message.

**The record holds a third claim nothing checks, and it belongs to another candidate.** `Taken::by` can
be changed to any agent the coordinate knows; every answer holds, and the record then attributes a
world to a party that did not decide it. Unreachable by any generator here, which is what separates
authenticity's field from veracity's.

**A decision's witness is a set, which is why a readmission is silent.** Re-adding a member the set
already holds changes no set. The ambiguity experiment 10 found needs something genuinely new between
the two occurrences, and then it is refused by name — so that refusal is reachable by accident, and
10's silence is what happens when the readmission adds nothing.

**Composition was expected to be where the state lived and was not.** A merge over an interrupted
write reaches the same state by the same route. The single mechanism was enough, and the interruption
only chooses which journal is on the other side.

## Variables the protocol left open

**What would close the recording instant.** Answered for the state that was found, and only for it:
one comparison over the shared prefix. The general question — whether a record can say it was told the
same thing twice, on two days — is Request 3 and is a design question, not a repair.

**More than two writers.** Excluded, and this result gives the first concrete reason to want it: the
comparison built here is pairwise, and what three journals agreeing by address owe each other is not
answered by it.

**Whether a record can say it might be false.** Untouched.

**Cost.** Deferred for the twelfth time. The repair adds one string comparison per shared entry per
merge; nothing was timed.

## What the result does not cover

**A Commitment's recording instant rather than an Event's**, and **a disagreement no cut straddles**.
Named in Observation 6 as the nearest unmeasured states. The repair refuses both, which is wider than
what was measured, and Observation 8 says so rather than defending it.

**Request 2 — that a merge weigh the arriving decisions against the worlds the arriving record
recorded.** It reads them and discards them. Nothing here found a second way for a merge to move a
standing decision's world; nothing here looked for one either.

**Anything about a network, a transport, or a process stopped inside a `rename`.** Excluded and named
as not reached.

**Benchmarking.** No timing.

# Architectural Consequences

## Established for the CLI

* **A journal comparison over addresses is incomplete**, and by exactly one field. Anything weighing
  two journals has to reach the recording instant explicitly, because no identity carries it.
* **`converge` refuses two journals that agree entry for entry and disagree about an instant**, with a
  refusal of its own that names the entry and both instants.
* **A recording instant is weighed only through `event_head`**, and only where something compares
  `worlds.json` against what the decisions produce.
* **A merge is the one operation exempt from the third guard**, because it writes the file that guard
  reads.
* **`Taken::by` is a claim the record cannot stand behind** — an identity that resolves and could be
  the wrong one — and no accident reaches it.
* **`Admission::recorded_at` is public**, because the one field an address omits has to be reachable by
  anything that compares records.

## Candidates for later experiments

* **Authenticity**, and this experiment sharpens rather than moves it. `by` is now measured as
  changeable with every answer holding, which is a case for that candidate produced from inside — and
  its own discriminator still says the remedy is outside.
* **A record that can say it was told the same thing twice, on two days.** Request 3. The repair
  refuses where an application would rather record; what a record owes two writers who disagree about
  an instant is not answered.
* **A merge that weighs the arriving worlds.** Request 2, and the nearest thing to a second face of
  this finding.
* **More than two writers**, wanted for the first time by something measured rather than by symmetry.
* **A Commitment's instant across two parties**, which reaches `ensure_selectable` instead of
  `head_as_of` and was predicted to refuse rather than lie. Unmeasured.
* **Cost as an absolute**, deferred by twelve experiments.
