# Merging two records

**This is not an experiment, and it is the first document here that is not one.** Every other thing in
this directory is a numbered protocol with its observations and its result. This is the assembled
answer to a question that seven of them each answered a piece of, and the row had nowhere to put one —
which is why the question kept being asked.

It is the row's oldest item, [`QUEUE.md`](../../QUEUE.md)'s *saying two lines agree*, named by
experiments 05, 06, 07, 08, 09, 12 and 15.

**It restates nothing.** Every fact below is one line and a pointer; what this holds that nothing else
does is the **shape they add up to**, which is not in any of them because each measured one face. The
contract of the operation itself is in [`cli/src/converge.rs`](../../../cli/src/converge.rs), and that
is where it stays.

---

## The answer

```text
                     two records                     two worlds
knowledge   converge  fast-forward only              —
intention   converge  union, and it is FREE          Synthesis: a real three-way merge
```

**Yes, in one case, and it is implemented.** `converge` merges when one journal **extends** the other.
**No in every other case**, and the refusal is at the first entry that differs.

**And yes, a candidate is reportable** — with nothing built, because it composes from the public
surface. What it can say splits in two, and only one half is a candidate at all.

---

## What merges

One journal extending the other: same entries, same order, same recording instants, one a prefix of
the other. Then the longer journal wins and the two lineages are unioned.

**The union is free**, and that is the first thing worth knowing:

> Two decisions cannot contradict one another. A lineage is a tree, so a second party's line is a
> branch rather than a competing version. Nothing is arbitrated because there is nothing to arbitrate.

Measured over all six orderings of two writers — [08](08-contention/99-result.md) — and the write is
whole, so a merge that does not reconstruct leaves the repository exactly as it was.

**So the inversion is the finding.** The part everyone expects to be hard — reconciling intentions — is
free. The hard part is **knowledge**, and it is the part that is refused.

---

## What is refused, and why each

```text
Diverged                the two journals disagree at a position
RecordedDifferently     they agree entry for entry and disagree about when
```

**The second is not fussiness.** Two parties can learn one fact on different days and write journals
that are equal address for address — no identity in the engine contains a recording instant. Before
that refusal existed, a merged record answered a settled level of **0** for a world its decider had
settled at **120**. [11](11-veracity/99-result.md) built it.

**And the first used to say too little**, which is the one **defect** this row found rather than an
absence: it named a position and nothing else, and a caller reading that concludes the two records are
incompatible. Measured against two records where ten entries and both decisions were takeable —
[15](15-assimilation/99-result.md).

> Repaired. `Diverged` now carries how many entries the two journals **hold in common**, which is what
> says they are divergent rather than incompatible. Symmetric on purpose: a count of what one side
> lacks would have to pick a side, and which record is *the other one* depends on which argument the
> caller passed.

That repair landed together with [13](13-indexicality/99-result.md)'s Request 1 — a coordinate the
journal does not hold used to be one refusal for two faults, and a true coordinate in somebody else's
journal now says so. The queue held the two as one item and said they were to be answered together or
not at all.

## What a candidate can say

Composable from `ape_cli`'s public surface with nothing added, and 15 proved it by rebuilding the
report **inside its own test file** — a consumer of the crate, which is what makes it proof rather
than illustration.

```text
                    learn    decide
holding everything    0        2
here                 10        2
holding nothing      12        2
```

**Knowledge is a candidate.** The first column discriminates — nothing, ten, all twelve — and it is
the useful thing a report would say.

**Intention is an instruction.** The second column is the whole of the other lineage, always. A retaken
world is never a world the other record decided: a world's identity comes from its cut, a cut from an
instant, and the instant a receiver may claim is never the instant the original claimed. No amount of
overlap reduces it.

> **Knowledge is negotiable and intention is not.**

---

## What cannot be said

Where any of it came from. A record that took another's material and a record that never met anybody
are **byte-identical**, in all four files — [17](17-imputation/99-result.md).

> The merge is reportable. **The fact of having merged is not.**

---

## What is still open

* **A name for a record.** The record names a party and a world and has nothing for a record, which is
  what both shapes 17 measured were reaching for.
* **Membership rather than sequence.** `converge` still refuses a reordering the record's own reader
  accepts — sound and not complete, and the incomplete side costs a party an attempt rather than a
  decision. [12](12-commensurability/99-result.md), [13](13-indexicality/99-result.md).
* **An operation whose result is not a repository.** Every operation the application has returns a
  record; a partial meeting is a **question**. [12](12-commensurability/99-result.md).
* **Whether the application should offer the composition at all**, which is a question about
  convenience, and this laboratory has no way to measure that.

---

## And one thing that is not open

Neither `converge` nor Synthesis is *merging two repositories* in the sense the word usually carries.
The application merges **two lines of deliberation over one line of knowledge**. The engine has a real
three-way merge and it is about intention **within** one canonical history —
[`07-synthesis`](../../../core/src/docs/07-synthesis.md).

The thing in between — two lines of knowledge reconciled — is refused, and every result above is a
measurement of why rather than a step towards it.
