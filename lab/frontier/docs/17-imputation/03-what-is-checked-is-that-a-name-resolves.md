# Observation 3 — What is checked is that a name resolves, and that is not nothing

**P3 is refuted in its literal form**, and the refutation is worth more than the prediction was.

P3 said *neither is checkable*. The record checks one thing, and the thing it checks rules out a class
of false provenance rather than none.

`attributed` weighs the party against the knowledge that stood **when the decision was taken** — not
against the record entire. So:

```text
  the source's party, on a decision taken before the crossing   REFUSED, DeciderNotKnown
  the source's party, on the decisions taken after it           accepted
  a party of the base, on a decision taken before it            accepted
  a party of the base, on the decisions taken after it          accepted
```

> **You cannot have got this from somebody you had not met yet.** That is a real sentence the record
> refuses, and it is not a sentence about knowledge — it is about a claim whose subject is another
> record's past, bounded by this record's own prefix.

What it cannot do is choose among the parties the record *had* met. Two of the three accepted rows are
false, and the record's own reader takes all three, with every world identical and every answer
identical.

## Experiment 13's premise stands, exactly where 13 put it

> *What a record checks, it derives.*

The check here is derived: the party is or is not among the agents admitted by the coordinate, which
comes out of the journal prefix. So the checkable half of a provenance claim is the **derivable** half,
and the boundary this experiment found is the boundary 13 drew. The severe failure condition — that
13's premise would be wrong for a third time — does not fire.

**Seen red by removing the check.** Disabling `attributed`'s one condition turns exactly one row of the
table above and one refusal test red, naming the row. Twelve of the fourteen phases stay green — which
is the size of what the record checks, stated as a measurement rather than as an impression.

## P4 confirmed, and the class is better stated than it was

Nothing the record *derives* moves when the claim changes. Closed, over all four files a whole write
puts on disk:

```text
journal.json   identical      lineage.json   DIFFERS — the file the claim is written in
worlds.json    identical      custody.json   identical
```

Every coordinate, every witness, every intention and every address is the same. So the third value
joins the recording instant and the decider rather than forming a new kind — which is what P4
predicted. But the kind is not the one experiment 14 named:

> Not *nothing weighs them*. **Resolution is weighed and the sentence is not.**

It covers all three. A recording instant's *ordering* is weighed — the Canon refuses one dated before
its watermark — and the instant itself is not. A decider's *resolution* is weighed, coordinate-
relatively, and the attribution is not. A provenance relation would be the third of exactly that shape.

14 measured that the three are one kind; this says which kind, and the difference matters because the
weighed half is the half a reader might mistake for the whole.
