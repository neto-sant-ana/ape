# Observation 10 — What an application would need, and none of it built

Phase 6. Verbatim requests, in the vocabulary of the need rather than of a solution, and nothing here
was implemented.

The answer to this experiment's question is *no*, so these are not requests for the change it weighed.
They are what is left over once the change is refused — which is a different and smaller list than the
one the collision experiment handed over, because three of its four items turn out to be one operation
away rather than one record away.

## Request 1 — A comparison whose subject is what two decisions are about

**The need.** A caller holding two repositories wants to know whether their decisions can be put
together. The record can only answer by comparing journals, entry for entry, as far as the shorter one
goes — which refuses two repositories that agree about every world either of them holds.

**What this experiment adds.** The comparison that would answer it needs **no change to the record.** A
dependence set is derived from the coordinate and the journal, both of which are already written down,
so what is missing is an operation and not a field. Observation 5 measured all three of the collision
experiment's blocked cases admitting under exactly such a comparison, with the record untouched.

**What it must not do.** Replace the witness. Observation 7 measured what that costs and no other guard
in the record covers it. A second comparison beside the first is a different proposal from a narrower
first, and only the second one is refused here.

**The open question inside it.** Two repositories agreeing about what their decisions are about is not
the same as agreeing about their history, and a record that merged on the first basis holds a journal
neither side witnessed. What such a record should say about that is not answered, and is the reason this
is a request rather than a design.

## Request 2 — A way to say that knowledge was present and not depended on

**The need.** The three silences of Observation 7 are one silence: knowledge that arrived, was never
depended on, and left. Today the witness refuses it by refusing everything. If a narrower comparison is
ever added beside the broad one, a reader needs to know which of the two answered.

**What it is not.** A weaker witness. The fact is already in the record; what is missing is a way for a
read to say *this was checked against dependence, not against presence*, so that a repository which has
been merged on the narrow basis does not read like one that never was.

**Where it belongs.** Beside the authenticity candidate, which is the next experiment and has been named
by nine results. A merged repository does not say it met anything, and a repository compared narrowly
does not say it was compared narrowly. Those are the same gap.

## Request 3 — A named cause for a readmission that a narrow comparison can still produce

**The need.** `ReadmittedEntryIsAmbiguous` is the one refusal this experiment measured as *lost* rather
than *traded*. It names a real cause — an address the journal admits twice, resolved to its earlier
occurrence — and it sends a reader somewhere useful. Under dependence there is nothing for it to fire
on.

**Why it is a request and not an objection.** A readmission is a property of the journal, not of any
world, so it is detectable without a witness at all: one pass over the addresses a journal produces
answers it. Nothing needs a decision's claim in order to notice that an address occurs twice.

**What that implies.** The diagnosis is in the wrong place — it is reachable only through a refusal it
happens to be able to explain — and that is true **today**, independently of anything this experiment
proposed. Measured, because it was asserted first:

```text
journal with one filing readmitted past the last coordinate

30 admissions, 29 distinct addresses
every decision corroborates, the record rebuilds, and nothing says the journal repeats itself
```

`diagnosed` runs only on the error path of `corroborate`, so a readmission nothing refused is a
readmission nothing names. It does not qualify for a repair here — two identical admissions are visible
in the file, which is the standard nine experiments have applied — but it is the one thing in this list
worth fixing whatever happens to the witness.

## What is deliberately not requested

**Recording the dependence set.** It is derivable, so writing it down buys falsifiability and nothing
else — and Observation 9 measured that a claim is worth what it can be wrong about. Whether that is
worth a field is a question for whoever builds Request 1, and choosing it here would be choosing the
shape of a remedy this experiment declined.

**Pruning.** Named in the protocol's open variables and untouched. What may be dropped is what nothing
points at, and this experiment moved the line that sits on — the closure says what points at things, and
it is narrower than the witness. The interaction is real and unmeasured.

**Anything in the engine.** A witness is the application's record of the application's own act. Nothing
here asks the ontology to know it exists, and the `core/` tree is untouched across the branch.
