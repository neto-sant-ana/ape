# Observation 8 — The trade, one sentence per side

Phase 5. Not a recommendation: what a narrower witness buys and what it sells, each stated as the query
that gains an answer and the query that loses one.

## What it buys

> **A caller can ask whether two records may be put together on the strength of what their decisions are
> about, rather than on the strength of having admitted the same things in the same order.**

That one query is all three of the collision experiment's requests. A partial meeting becomes statable
because the subject of the comparison is a set the decision names rather than a prefix nobody chose;
knowledge becomes takeable without its lineage for the same reason; and two records of one decision
become one record wherever the coordinate also agrees.

## What it sells

> **Nobody can ask whether the knowledge that was present when a decision was taken is still present.**

And nothing else in the record can be asked in its place. The coordinate answers *which knowledge was
this produced against*, the worlds file answers *is this still the world you said*, and neither of them
notices knowledge that arrived, was never depended on, and left.

## Why the two are not comparable in a currency

This is the honest core of the experiment, and it is why the bytes are the weakest number in it.

The buy is a **capability**: something a writer cannot currently do, and would be able to. The sell is a
**guarantee**: something a reader currently gets, and would stop getting. Nine experiments have applied
one criterion to that asymmetry — *it removes a state a reader can be misled by, and what the repair
replaces survives* — and the criterion exists because the two are not interchangeable. A record that
gains three capabilities and quietly loses one guarantee has not made a trade; it has moved a cost from
the person who writes to the person who reads, and only one of them was in the room.

## And the shape that would have hidden it

The buy is measurable in one afternoon. Three cases, three refusals, three admissions — visible,
countable, satisfying. The sell is a **silence**, and a silence has to be looked for on purpose. Had
this experiment measured only Phase 3, every number in it would have been true and the conclusion would
have been the opposite one.

Which is what the protocol meant by *W3 with W4 is the experiment*, written before anything ran, and
why the four arrivals were treated as one question asked four times rather than as four reasons to say
yes.
