# Result

**Confirmed** for both lines of the hypothesis — and because both held, the answer to the question is
*no*.

```text

and derivable                subset of the entries that stood, and derivable from what is
                             already on disk                                     CONFIRMED

and the difference is        the entries a decision does NOT depend on are exactly the ones
what the witness is for      whose disappearance the witness exists to refuse    CONFIRMED
```

Both held, which is why the answer is no. The tension the protocol built the experiment around was
real, and the second line won it.

```text
W1  dependence is narrower than presence, and derivable         CONFIRMED, and sharpened
W2  the difference grows with the journal, the dependence does not      CONFIRMED
W3  dependence in its place admits all three blocked cases      CONFIRMED, with a limit
W4  and it stops refusing something the broad witness refuses    CONFIRMED, three times
W5  the readmission diagnosis survives                           REFUTED
```

**W1 is sharpened rather than qualified.** Derivable, yes — and not by walking references. Nothing in the
record points at an eligibility; it is reached by restating the rule that admitted the commitment. A
closure that followed pointers only comes back looking well formed and produces a journal that does not
admit.

**W5 is refuted in the direction the protocol said would cost something.**
`ReadmittedEntryIsAmbiguous` becomes silence, and it names a real cause.

**Part B was not built**, against both criteria. The inherited one — *it removes a state a reader can be
misled by, and what the repair replaces survives* — is not met, because every state this experiment found
is either refused by name or visible in the file. And this experiment's own condition forbids the change
outright: *a repair may not remove a refusal without putting the fact it protected somewhere*, and
Observation 7 measured that there is nowhere else in the record for that fact to be.

One change was made to the application, and it is not a repair: a sentence in `cli/src/lineage.rs` said
that a repository holding only what produces its worlds cannot contradict itself, and the instrument of
this experiment is the counter-example. Observation 9.

## The answer

The question was *can a record say what a decision depends on, rather than what stood when it was taken
— and keep what the witness buys?* It can say it. It cannot say it **instead**.

```text
what dependence is       a set the record can derive, claim, and be caught being wrong
                         about in both directions — and it is what unblocks all three of
                         the collision experiment's requests, with the record untouched

what it cannot be         the answer to "was this knowledge here when I decided", which
                         is a different fact and the only one the record does not derive
```

And the finding that decides it is not about dependence at all. It is about **who else was watching**:

> **Narrowing the witness does not weaken a chain of guards. It removes the only guard whose subject is
> the repository's history rather than its intentions.** `Taken::after` says which knowledge a world was
> produced against. `reading::corroborated` refuses any change that moves a world. Between them, a change
> that no world is a function of is invisible — and the three states dependence stops refusing are that
> one state seen from three angles.

Which reframes the whole candidate. What the three blocked capabilities need is a **second comparison**,
not a **different record**: the dependence set is derived from the coordinate and the journal, both
already written, so nothing has to be stored and nothing has to be given up. The mistake in the four
arrivals was not wanting dependence. It was assuming dependence had to arrive in the witness's place.

**And the pressure the protocol named did run the way it said.** Phase 3 is quick, visible and
satisfying: three refusals become three admissions, the duplicate vanishes, the lineage shrinks by 32%.
Phase 4 is a silence and had to be looked for. An experiment that had stopped after Phase 3 would have
had every number right and the conclusion backwards — which is what *W3 with W4 is the experiment* meant
when it was written down before anything ran.

**The severe failure condition was not met.** Dependence is derivable from the record, without the
writer's help, and the derivation was demonstrated rather than argued: prune the journal to the closure,
replay it, apply the decisions, and the same `ThesisId` comes back.

## Against each criterion

1. **The dependence set is derived, not declared.** Shown by sufficiency rather than by inspection: the
   pruned journal replays and reproduces the same world identity, and the pruned length equals the
   closure exactly. The one edge that is not a reference — the eligibility rule — is measured by removing
   it and watching the journal stop admitting.
2. **Both comparisons run over the same repositories.** Every row of Phase 4 is one mutation of one
   arrangement, put to the record's own comparison, to the dependence comparison, and to the worlds file.
   Only the `witness` field is ever substituted, so no difference is a difference of encoding.
3. **The cost half is in entries and bytes**, comparable to the exploration experiment's numbers: 76
   addresses against 48, 6,489 lineage bytes against 4,417. No timing.
4. **What stops being refused is a closed table**, five rows, including one that was already silent
   before this experiment existed and one where dependence refuses and presence does too.
5. **The three cases are reproduced before they are re-run.** Each begins by producing the collision
   experiment's own refusal, by name, so a change in outcome is attributable.
6. **Nothing was added to the APE engine.** `core/` is untouched across the branch.
7. **Part B was not built, against both criteria, and the absence is the result.**
8. **The nine earlier experiments' conclusions stand**, and *the witness is load-bearing* — the one at
   risk, concluded by three of them — holds, with its reason replaced. Details below.

**Criteria 1 and 4 are the experiment**, and both hold.

**Failure conditions.** The severe one was not met. Its neighbour **was** met, and was handled as the
protocol required: a narrower witness admits a state a reader cannot tell from a legitimate one, so the
remedy is refused and the state is reported — it belongs to the veracity candidate, and it is that
candidate's first reachable case from the writing side. Of the ordinary ones: no dependence set was
supplied by the arrangement; every outcome is reported from what the record holds rather than from the
absence of an error; no repair was chosen at all, before or after Phase 4; the broad witness is reported
as buying a guarantee rather than as wasteful; and the four arrivals were weighed as one question asked
four times.

**Three mutations, and each turned the right guards red.** The chain walk reduced to its head:
`[13, 16, 16]` against `[14, 17, 17]`, eight guards, *the whole chain, not the head*. The eligibility
edge dropped: `[12, 15, 15]`, and the closure stops admitting. The derivation check removed from the
narrow rebuild: exactly one guard, the one written for it.

## Findings that outlast the verdict

**A decision is about far less than it came after.** Fourteen entries of twenty-three, seventeen of
twenty-nine, and the difference is a whole second vocabulary nothing reaches.

**A dependence set is derived by restating the rules that admitted the knowledge, not by walking the
references it holds.** Every rule that consults knowledge by query rather than by reference adds an edge
no pointer records.

**A chain is reached whole, and pruning one link makes another world.** An Event's identity contains its
predecessor, so the Events before a cut are dependence whether or not any of them settled a commitment
the world holds — and a record that dropped one would come back agreeing with itself about a different
world rather than missing an entry.

**The same decision costs more for having waited.** One `Decision`, one `ThesisId`, five more witnessed
entries for having been written later. The gap is a function of the journal and not of the decision.

**Two repositories deciding independently wrote the same record twice.** Two of the three decisions in
the union were already byte-identical on both sides, and `converge`'s own deduplication found them
without being told. The duplicate arises only at the decision taken after the journals diverged.

**A `Taken` holds two facts about knowledge and they answer different questions.** The coordinate says
which knowledge a world is produced against; the witness says which knowledge was there. Narrowing the
second leaves the first, which is why deduplication by dependence works exactly where two records agree
about *where* they were taken and not otherwise.

**A record holding only what produces its worlds can contradict itself.** The closure is a function of
the world and the world is produced from the coordinate and the journal, so a dependence claim is
falsifiable in both directions — a record claiming what its world does not reach, and a record short of
what its world does.

**The witness is the only guard whose subject is history.** The one that reframes the candidate, and the
one nobody had asked for.

**A readmission nothing refused is a readmission nothing names.** `diagnosed` runs only on the error path
of `corroborate`, so a journal that admits an address twice past the last coordinate rebuilds in silence.
True today, unrelated to anything this experiment proposed.

## What changed in the experiments before it

**Nothing was overturned, and one reason was replaced.**

**Convergence's, coordination's and collision's *the witness is load-bearing* stands, with a different
argument under it.** All three reached it by saying the witness is what makes knowledge append-only in a
checkable way. That is true and it was never the whole reason: what makes it load-bearing is that it is
the **only** claim in the record whose subject is not derived from the worlds. The three earlier
statements are correct and were arguing from the weaker half.

**The lineage module's docstring said something false**, and this experiment is where it was measured.
*A repository holding only what produces its worlds cannot contradict itself* is the counter-example the
instrument is. Corrected, and the correction names the class exactly: what it cannot notice is a change
no world is a function of.

**Collision's three requests are one candidate, and now one operation.** The fold was right; what is new
is that the operation needs nothing added to the record. All three admit under a comparison over what is
already written down.

**Exploration's cost observation acquires its other half.** *A decision should cost what the decision is,
not what has been admitted by the time it is taken* — and this experiment measures what the excess buys,
which is the thing that observation could not say.

## Variables the protocol left open

**Whether *what was present* deserves its own record.** Answered in the negative for the substitution and
untouched for the addition: it already has its own record, and this experiment found nothing that would
be improved by a second one.

**Pruning.** Named and unmeasured. The closure says what points at things, and it is narrower than the
witness, so the line pruning sits on moved. Nothing here measured where.

**Cost as an absolute.** Deferred for the eleventh time. This measures a ratio in the units exploration
used, over a tail this experiment chose.

**Two repositories as somebody else's.** Authenticity, deferred deliberately, and now next.

## What the result does not cover

**A second comparison beside the first.** Request 1, and the nearest unanswered question after this one.
Everything measured here says it is available; nothing here built it, and what a record merged on that
basis owes a reader is not answered.

**Whether a claim of insensitivity could be checked rather than derived.** Untouched.

**Anything about more than two repositories, a network, or a transport.** Excluded.

**Benchmarking.** No timing was taken.

# Architectural Consequences

## Established for the CLI

* **A decision's world is a function of a strict subset of what stood**, derivable from the coordinate
  and the journal, and reproducible from that subset alone.
* **The derivation restates the engine's admission rules**, so it cannot be written as a reference walk —
  and a copy of it inside the application would be a second representation of the Axiom's conditions.
* **The Event chain up to a cut is dependence entire**, and cannot be narrowed by anything.
* **The witness is the only claim in a repository whose subject is its history**; the coordinate and the
  worlds file are both functions of what the worlds are.
* **Replacing the witness with a dependence claim is refused**, and the reason is a silence rather than a
  count.
* **A dependence claim is falsifiable in both directions**, which is what makes a second comparison
  possible without a second file.

## Candidates for later experiments

* **Authenticity**, named by nine results and next in line.
* **A comparison whose subject is what two decisions are about** — Request 1, promoted out of the witness
  candidate, which this experiment closes. It needs an operation and not a record.
* **Veracity**, with its first reachable case from the writing side: knowledge present at a decision, not
  depended on, and later removed, is indistinguishable from a legitimate record under any comparison
  narrower than presence.
* **The readmission diagnosis is reachable only through a refusal** — small, real, and unrelated to the
  witness.
* **Pruning**, whose line this experiment moved without measuring.
* **Cost as an absolute**, deferred by eleven experiments.
