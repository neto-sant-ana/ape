# Observation 6 — And the coordinate still tells two records apart

A limit on the third claim of Observation 5, measured rather than left implicit.

A `Taken` is three things: a decision, a **coordinate**, and a claim about knowledge. Narrowing the
claim makes two records of one decision into one record — as long as the coordinate agrees.

In the union it did agree, because both sides took the late decision after the same entry. But the
subject holds a case where it does not, and it is the same case W2 is built on:

```text
                            decision     after     claimed dependence
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
taken after its plan          identical      G          identical
taken at the end              identical      H          identical
```

Two records, still. The claim about knowledge is the same claim; `after` is not, so nothing collapses.

## Why this is not a defect of the narrowing

`after` is what makes the world producible at all. `lineage::rebuild` admits the journal in step with
the lineage — up to the entry each decision was taken after, then the decision, then on — and that is
the whole of the repair the divergence and coordination experiments earned. A cut resolves against the
Event chain that stood, so the coordinate is not redundant with the claim: it says which knowledge the
world is produced **against**, and the claim says which of that knowledge the world is a function **of**.

So the record holds two facts about knowledge and they answer different questions. What this experiment
narrows is the second. The first stays, and it is why the third capability of Observation 5 is
conditional rather than general:

> **Deduplication by dependence works exactly where two records agree about where they were taken.**
> Two repositories that decided the same thing at different points still hold two records, and neither
> is redundant — because each says something true that the other does not.

## The nearest thing this leaves open

Whether *decided after this entry* and *decided about this knowledge* are two facts a record needs both
of, or whether the first is derivable once the second is written down. The closure is a function of the
world, and the world is produced against the prefix the coordinate names — so the derivation runs from
the coordinate to the claim and not back. Nothing here found a way to run it the other direction, and
nothing here needed to.
