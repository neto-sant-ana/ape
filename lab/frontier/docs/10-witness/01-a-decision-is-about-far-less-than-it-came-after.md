# Observation 1 — A decision is about far less than it came after

W1, first half, and the number is the observation.

```text
world                   stood      reached     the difference
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
base   { F₁, F₂ }          23          14          9
early  { F₁, F₂, G }       24          17          7
late   { F₁, F₂, H }       29          17         12
```

Predicted before the run as `DEPENDED = [14, 17, 17]` and measured exactly. Every set is a **strict**
subset of the prefix that stood, so the first half of the hypothesis holds without qualification.

What the reached entries are, for the base: the two inflows, both Events, and the vocabulary those
resolve through — two roles, two agents, one resource, one instance, one action, one statement, and the
two eligibilities. A plan adds its own statement and its own action, and nothing else. Nothing about
`paper` is ever reached: not the role, not the agent, not the resource, not the instance, not the
action, not the statement, and none of the four filings.

## What makes this a measurement and not an arrangement

The protocol's own constraint — *a phase must not be satisfied by an arrangement it could not have
failed* — bites here, and the subject answers it by saying how much tail it holds before anything runs.
`TAIL_VOCABULARY = 7` and `FILINGS = [5, 7, 11, 13]` are the amount of knowledge no decision is about,
written down as literals. An arrangement whose journal were all dependence would have reported *the
sets coincide* and proved nothing.

The second resource is the part that would have been easy to leave out. Had the filings moved `cash`
through the same statement, they would have been reached anyway — by the statement, by the instance, by
the eligibility — and the difference would have collapsed to the four commitments. Nine entries of
difference under the base world is what a *separate vocabulary* buys, and it is the shape a real
history has: most of what an organization admits is about something else.

## The nesting, which is not free

```text
base ⊂ early        base ⊂ late        early ⊄ late        late ⊄ early
```

Measured rather than assumed. Two worlds forking one parent need agree about nothing but the parent, and
here they do not: each holds its own plan and the statement and action that plan resolves through. What
follows is that a world's own reach is the whole of what producing it needs — the ancestry adds
nothing — which is what makes the closure a usable claim rather than a fact about one lineage.

## What it does not say

That the ratio generalizes. Nine entries and twelve entries of difference are properties of *this*
tail, and the exploration experiment's 18,226 of 23,553 bytes is a property of that one. Cost as an
absolute stays deferred, for the eleventh time. What generalizes is the **direction**: the reach is
bounded by what the decision names, and the prefix is bounded by nothing.
