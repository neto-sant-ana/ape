## The answer

The question was *should an address say when its entry was recorded — and can it, without costing two
records the ability to recognize each other?*

It should not, and the second clause is not why.

> Two records do stop recognizing each other — thirteen shared addresses become zero for two founded a
> day apart, twelve of thirteen for two that disagree about one instant — and their worlds stay equal
> by identity throughout. That is a trade, and a trade is reported rather than taken. What settles it
> instead is that **an address stops being a name**: the map from identity to address, free today,
> becomes a lookup with nowhere to look for seven of the nine families.

Three conditions were written before anything ran and all three fire: the trade condition, the
published-result condition — every one of the seven tests in a concluded agents-row suite fails against
its four committed repositories — and the boundary, because completing the change needs the engine.

## What outlasts the verdict

**The buy is real and is now priced.** The witness completes the pin at a stage the record already
writes, with no field added to a `Taken`. That measurement stands whatever happens to the address, and
it is the strongest argument the question of a portable decision has ever had. What is refused is
buying it *there*.

**And the shape of where else to look is specific for the first time.** Something that individuates a
**prefix** rather than an **entry** would leave an address a name and still fix a reference. That is
named as a direction and not as a design, which is what the methodological constraint requires — and
this experiment is the argument for the constraint: a shape that looked obviously right cost something
nobody had thought to look for.

**One sentence in the application stopped describing the workspace.** `cli/src/lineage.rs` says that
keeping already-written records readable is not a reason to hold a field's shape, because a suite that
writes and reads with one version is not constrained by it. Four committed repositories are now written
by one version and read by a live suite. The note is right about `Taken.by` and wrong about where such
records are.

## Against each criterion

1. ***The same entry* is defined before any phase runs**, in the Canon's terms —
   `AppendOutcome::AlreadyPresent` — and the three weaker readings are named apart from it.
2. **The five sites are derived** by a scan of `cli/src` rather than listed, and the count is the
   guard. The subject's own list of five had the right number and the wrong members, which the
   derivation caught: idempotence is the Canon's and not an address comparison at all, and
   `lineage::diagnosed` and `converge::ordered` were missing.
3. **Both columns are measured the same way**, by the same phases, run against two shapes — the shape
   derived from an address rather than from a build flag, so neither column is a suite deciding which
   one it is in.
4. **The arrangement cannot confirm N1 by itself**, and the phase that says so runs before the build:
   a second instant varies, at position 11, and the candidate carrying it shares the record's
   coordinate exactly.
5. **The blast radius is counted**, compilation and runtime separately, and weighed against the count
   taken before the build.
6. **Every literal was written before the run**, both columns, and no pre-registered one had to be
   corrected. Two things measured *after* the run did: the runtime cost was first read as six of eight
   tests, from a truncated grep, and is seven of seven; and the engine's shape was first read off a
   trait and is corrected in Observation 4. Neither was a prediction, and both are the kind of thing
   this laboratory's rule about diagnosis exists for.
7. **Nothing was added to the APE engine** — and the finding is that completing the change would have
   required it.
8. **The decision cites the table**, and the revert is the result.
9. **The fourteen earlier conclusions stand.** Experiment 09's was the one at risk and it is unmoved:
   what N4 measured is what *would* happen to it under a shape that was refused.
