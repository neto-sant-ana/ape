# Observation 5 — Candidate or instruction, and what does not survive

**S3 is half confirmed and half refuted**, and the half that fails does so for a reason that is not
about the arrangement.

```text
                       learn    decide
  holding everything     0        2
  here                  10        2
  holding nothing       12        2
```

The first column discriminates: three receivers, three answers, and the middle one is neither empty nor
everything. **What to learn is a candidate.**

The second column is one number in every row, and it is the whole of the other record's lineage. **The
report always says take all of their decisions**, which is not a candidate — it is an instruction with a
count in front of it.

## Why the second column cannot discriminate

> A **retaken** world is never a world the other record decided. A world's identity is derived from its
> cut, a cut is resolved from an instant, and the instant a receiver may claim is never the instant the
> original claimed. So every decision is always left to take, because none of them can already have
> been taken.

Measured for all three receivers, including the one that had nothing to learn: not one retaken world is
one of theirs.

Synthesis has an `AlreadyApplied` status and this cannot. Its Base, Source and Target sit in **one**
canonical history and can arrive at one world; two records cannot, and the thing that stops them is the
same value experiments 11, 13 and 14 kept arriving at.

## And *already held* is reachable — by not crossing at all

The phase above failed where it was expected to pass, and the failure is the better half.

`holding everything` admitted the same content `there` did and decided at the same instant. Its own
genesis produces `there`'s first world **by identity**, with no operation between them and nothing
retaken. That is experiment 09, and it means the status Synthesis has is not unreachable here — it is
reachable only as a **coincidence of two decisions**, never as an outcome of one crossing.

Which is a sharper statement of what two records can share than either 09 or this experiment has alone:
they agree about a world exactly where they agreed about the knowledge **and about when they held it**,
and any operation that moves material between them lands somewhere else by construction.

## What does not survive being retaken

Closed over what a `Taken` is.

```text
  the intention      survives      a selection, an omission, an introduction
  the coordinate     does not      it is this record's tip
  the witness        does not      it is this record's prefix
  the decider        does not      the candidate claims nobody
```

**S5 is refuted, and for a worse reason than the prediction.** The prediction was that the receiving
record cannot say the other is why. It can. An `AgentId` is content-addressed, the receiver learns the
agent along with everything else, and a decision claiming it is **accepted** — `attributed` checks only
that the agent was known at the coordinate, and after learning it was. Measured: the record reads.

What the record cannot do is make it true. `by` is the party that **took** the decision, and the party
that took this one is this record. So the one field that could cross does not mean what it would have
to mean, and a record that filled it in would be writing a claim of exactly the kind experiment 05
measured nothing can check.

The absence of provenance is therefore not a gap in the shape. It is the shape refusing to hold a
sentence that would be false — and *recording that a decision was carried rather than taken*, which is
experiment 12's queued item, is asking for a field whose meaning this experiment just measured to be
different from the one it would occupy.
