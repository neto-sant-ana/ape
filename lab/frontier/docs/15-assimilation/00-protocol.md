# Assimilation

## Abstract

Experiment 12 asked for an operation whose result is not a repository, and concluded that nothing here
has that shape.

It does, one layer up.

```text
Synthesis    base, source, target -> a report, with a status and named conflicts
             the Base is a CHOICE, verified rather than searched
             nothing is written; the application decides
             `transfer::applied` turns the report into an ORDINARY decision in the Target
```

And the move that matters is the last one. Synthesis does not **carry** the Source's decision — it
**translates** it. What comes back is a new `Decision::Fork` taken in the Target, through the same path
every other decision has ever taken, and the record never says another line of thinking is why.

Applied to two records, that unties the knot three experiments tightened:

```text
a foreign fact       enters at the RECEIVING record's instant. `recorded_at` is how a history
                     holds a fact, not a property of the fact, so there is no slot with two
                     instants: A holds it from today, and B's holding stays B's

a foreign decision   is retaken rather than carried. A decides now, with A's coordinate and A's
                     witness and B's intention, and nothing false is written
```

Chosen by measuring vocabulary load first. `candidate` appears **737** times across the laboratory, the
application and the engine's documents — the word for the thing this experiment must produce is
unusable as a handle for it. `asymmetry` is 43 and names the method rather than the question.
**Assimilation** is 0, and it names the move and its price in one word: foreign material becoming
indistinguishable from one's own is exactly what happens here, and exactly what is lost.

The predictions are lettered **S**, because `A` belongs to atomicity.

---

## Question

**Can a record take another's material as its own — learning what it did not know and deciding what it
did not decide — without either record claiming a past that was not its? And is what that produces a
candidate, or an instruction to become the other record?**

The second clause is the falsification condition, and it is why this is an experiment rather than a
build. An operation that answers *adopt the other record* has produced no candidate; it has produced a
longer way of saying *copy*.

---

## What *taking as its own* means, decided before any phase runs

The four experiments before this one each settled a word first. The word here is **take**.

> **A record takes another's material when what it ends up holding is admissible on its own terms** —
> every entry recorded at an instant this record can honestly claim, every decision witnessed by a
> prefix that actually stood here — **and nothing in it asserts anything about the other record's
> past.**

Three weaker things it deliberately does **not** mean:

```text
copy      reproducing the other journal, which reproduces its instants and therefore
          claims its past. Refused since experiment 02

merge     the two becoming one, which is the shape experiment 12 measured cannot be
          written: a carried decision's witness stops being true

agree     experiment 09's world-level agreement, which needs no operation, changes
          nothing and takes nothing. It is what two records already have
```

The middle one is why *take* is not a softer word for *merge*. A merge asks what the two hold together;
this asks what one of them can hold on its own terms, having been shown the other.

---

## Hypothesis

```text
a foreign fact enters at     `recorded_at` is how a history holds a fact rather than a property
the receiving instant        of the fact. So the slot with two instants does not arise — A holds
                             it from today, honestly, and B's holding is B's

and a foreign decision is    Synthesis's move: the intention crosses, the decision does not. A
retaken rather than carried  decides now, with its own coordinate and its own witness, and the
                             witness it writes is true
```

If both hold, the operation experiment 12 asked for exists and needs nothing from the engine. If the
first holds and the second does not, what this produces is a diagnosis with no remedy — which is worth
knowing and is not what four results have been asking for.

---

## Pre-registered predictions

Five, written before anything runs.

**S1 — the entries a record lacks can be admitted at its own recording instant.**

*Prediction:* A, shown B's journal, can admit every entry it does not hold, dated today. Recording
monotonicity passes trivially — today is at or after A's watermark — and every reference resolves,
because B admitted them in an order that resolved and A admits them in that order.

*Refuted if* monotonicity, a reference or an identity refuses one.

**S2 — and a foreign decision becomes a decision the receiving record takes, with no witness
rewritten.**

*Prediction:* B's intention is expressible as a decision A takes: A's coordinate, A's witness, A's
lineage. Nothing in it says a prefix stood that did not.

*Refuted if* producing a candidate requires writing a witness that never stood here. **This is the
severe one.** It would mean the report is experiment 12 with a report in front of it, and the two
moves 12 measured — keep the witness and the record refuses itself, rewrite it and the record lies —
are still the only two.

**S3 — and what it produces is a candidate rather than an instruction.**

*Prediction:* for an arrangement where the two records genuinely diverge, the set of entries A must
learn is neither empty nor the whole of B's journal, and the set of decisions A could take is neither
empty nor all of B's.

*Refuted if* either is degenerate across every arrangement the subject can build. A report whose answer
is always *take everything* is `copy` with a status field.

**S4 — Events are the exception, and identity survives only where the chains are prefix-related.**

*Prediction:* `previous_event` is part of an `EventId`, so two chains that diverged cannot exchange
Events by identity — A can admit an Event settling the same commitment, and it is a different
assertion. For the other eight families identity survives, because their ids contain no chain.

*Refuted if* identity survives divergence, or fails where the chains agree.

**S5 — and the provenance does not survive.**

*Prediction:* stated separately so it can be wrong separately. The retaken decision says what it
selects and never that another record is why — which is what Synthesis already pays and says out loud.

*Refuted if* the receiving record can say the other is why, using something it already holds.

**S2 with S3 is the experiment.** S2 says a candidate can be produced at all; S3 says it is a candidate
rather than an instruction. Either alone is half: an unproducible candidate is no operation, and an
instruction is no candidate.

## What is carried forward, and is not available as a finding

* **A partial meeting computes and its result is not a repository**, because a carried decision's
  witness becomes false — 12.
* **Two records agree about a world exactly where they agreed about the knowledge under it** — 09.
* **A travelling decision is believed rather than checked**, and the only claim holding the content
  cannot settle is when each entry was learned — 13.
* **An address must stay a function of the entity it names** — 14.
* **A recording instant is derived from nothing and no record weighs it** — 13.
* **Synthesis translates rather than carries, and the provenance does not survive into the decision** —
  `cli/src/transfer.rs`, earned by 03. This experiment inherits that cost rather than discovering it.
* **A question is asked *of* a record and arrives from outside it** — 03. So *the report differs by
  which record is asking* is not a finding; it is what a question is.

Before any sentence here is allowed to be a finding, ask what would have to be false for it to be
false.

## Motivation

It goes now because the shape four results have been circling turned out to already exist, and because
what it costs is already catalogued.

**By ripeness** it is the row's oldest item: *saying two lines agree*, named by 05, 06, 07, 08 and 09,
and run once as experiment 12 — which did not close it, and moved it. This is the face 12 handed over.

**By what it costs if deferred** it is now first by default. The two items that would have changed what
a type *is* are both answered: the coordinate by 13, the address by 14. What remains adds rather than
alters, and this is the one that adds an operation rather than a field.

And it goes now because the two things that usually stop this row are absent. It needs nothing from the
engine — every part of it is composition over operations that exist. And it arrives with its price
already known, from Synthesis, rather than with a price to be discovered.

## Experimental Boundary

This experiment asks what one record can take from another on its own terms, and whether saying so
produces something an application could act on.

It includes:

* measuring what an operation between two records can do **today**, before anything is built;
* what a record can learn, and at whose instant;
* whether a foreign intention is expressible as a decision the receiver takes;
* the family table — what crosses by identity and what does not — as a closed table over all nine;
* the report, built **in the laboratory**, far enough to test whether it carries a candidate;
* both falsification conditions, and they are named above rather than reached for at the end.

It deliberately excludes:

* **the engine.** Nothing here needs a port widened, and an experiment that reached for one would be
  answering experiment 14's question again;
* **changing `Taken`.** 13 and 14 both refused it, and this experiment is the argument that nothing
  needed changing;
* **provenance.** *Recording that a decision was carried rather than taken* is 12's queued item; this
  inherits its absence and does not close it. A phase proposing a field for it is out of scope;
* **more than two records, the network, a transport.** Excluded throughout the row.

---

## Experimental Subject

**A subject of this experiment's own**, in experiment 12's shape and built again here.

What it must express:

```text
two records founded apart     sharing a base by content, so what they have in common is
on a shared base              derived rather than arranged — 09's ground, re-read

divergence in every family    at least one entry of each of the nine kinds admitted after
                              the split, because S4 is a claim about all nine and a subject
                              with only commitments would report a property of itself

a decision on each side       so that S2 can fail. If every foreign decision produced the
whose world WOULD differ      same world in the other frame, a candidate would be producible
in the other's frame          for a reason about the arrangement

a partial overlap             the two must share some of what they added, not only the base
after the split               — otherwise `learn` is *everything B added* and S3 is
                              degenerate by construction
```

The last is where a subject would quietly make S3 un-failable. An arrangement in which the two records
share nothing after the base has a `learn` set equal to the whole of B's tail, which is *take
everything* — and a phase reporting that as a candidate would be reporting the arrangement.

## Procedure

### Phase 0 — What two records can do today

Read once, before anything is built: `converge` refuses, `holds` answers about a world, and nothing
takes one decision. Every answer as a literal, so that what the report adds is weighed against
something measured.

### Phase 1 — What one record can learn, and at whose instant

S1. Every entry B holds and A does not, admitted into A at A's own instant, and the result put to A's
own reader.

### Phase 2 — What one record can decide with another's intention

S2. Each of B's decisions, expressed as a decision A takes. The phase reads the witness A writes and
asserts it against the prefix that actually stood — this is where the severe condition is measured, and
it is measured on the written record rather than on the construction.

### Phase 3 — What crosses by identity

S4, as a closed table over all nine families: admitted into A, does the address survive? The Event row
is predicted to differ and the phase must show the other eight not differing, or the table says nothing.

### Phase 4 — Candidate or instruction

S3. The two sets, for every arrangement the subject can produce, and the degenerate cases named as
degenerate rather than reported as answers.

### Phase 5 — What does not survive

S5, and the closed list of what the retaken decision cannot say.

### Phase 6 — The three guards

Every state produced here put to the coordinate, the witness and the worlds file, and to the reading.

### Phase 7 — Part B, if it was earned

An operation, whose shape is decided by Phases 1 to 6 and named nowhere above.

## The condition on building it

Different from the last two, and stricter in the direction that matters. Experiments 13 and 14 weighed
a change to what a type **is**; this weighs an **addition**, and the row has a rule aimed exactly at
additions:

> **A capability several results asked for is still a want.**

Five results asked for this. That is not evidence, and the protocol says so before the phases start. So
Part B is refused unless the phases show both:

> **The report answers something no existing operation answers** — not more conveniently, at all — and
> **it produces a candidate an application could act on without being told anything else.**

The second half is S3 wearing its other hat. An operation whose output still requires the caller to go
and read the other repository has moved the work rather than done it.

## Success Criteria

1. *Take* is defined before any phase runs, and the three weaker readings are each produced rather than
   asserted.
2. What two records can do today is measured here, not inherited from 09 or 12.
3. The witness A writes is checked against the prefix that stood, on the written record.
4. The family table is closed over all nine, and the eight that are predicted not to move are shown not
   to move.
5. Both degenerate cases of S3 are producible by the subject and are reported as degenerate.
6. Every literal is written before the run.
7. Nothing is added to the APE engine.
8. Part B is built only against both halves of the condition above, and its absence is a reportable
   result.
9. The fourteen earlier conclusions stand, or the change is recorded as a result of this one.
   Experiment 12's — *a partial meeting's result is not a repository* — is the one at risk, and S2 is
   how.

**Criteria 3 and 5 are the experiment.**

## Failure Conditions

The severe one:

> If producing a candidate requires writing a witness that never stood in the receiving record, then
> this is experiment 12 with a report in front of it, and the two moves 12 measured are still the only
> two. That is reported as the via being dead, and the row's oldest item goes back to the queue with a
> second refusal against it.

Its neighbour:

> If the `learn` set is empty or the whole of the other journal for every arrangement, the report says
> *adopt the other record* with more steps. That is not a failure of the idea; it is the finding that
> two repositories exchange all or nothing, which would be worth more than an operation.

And the ordinary ones:

* a candidate produced against an arrangement whose records share nothing after the base;
* *the eight families cross* reported where what was measured is *the commitments crossed*;
* provenance smuggled in as a field, which the boundary excludes and which the pressure point below is
  about;
* a Part B built because five results wanted it, which is the condition above;
* the report's asymmetry reported as a finding, when experiment 03 already established that a question
  arrives from outside the record.

## Variables Deliberately Left Open

### Whether the other direction is worth asking

The report is asked in one frame. Whether A-from-B and B-from-A together say something neither says
alone is a second reading, and this experiment does not take it.

### What a record owes two writers who disagree about an instant

Experiment 11's queued item. This experiment's answer may be *nothing, because it is not the record's
to hold* — which would close it. Saying so is a result of the phases and not a premise.

### Whether two repositories should ever become one

The row has assumed it. This measures what the alternative costs and does not decide it.

### Cost

Deferred by fifteen experiments.

## Methodological Constraint

> *Do not introduce an abstraction intended to solve an experiment that has not yet been performed.*

The previous experiments' conclusions are not revised here; where this one finds something that would
have changed them, it is recorded as a finding of this experiment, against the implementation as it
then stood.

And the rules the last nine experiments earned, which apply without amendment. Before recording
anything as a finding, ask what would have to be false for it to be false. Every literal is written
before the run, and a wrong prediction is corrected in the open rather than adjusted. A prediction's
justification must quantify over as much as its claim does. A friction is evidence of a want, not of a
need. A phase must not be satisfied by an arrangement it could not have failed. A negative result must
name the space it searched. A capability several results asked for is still a want. A guard whose two
halves read the same derived value must assert the mutation before it reads. And a measurement taken
with an unchecked instrument is a false conclusion wearing the clothes of evidence.

## Expected Pressure Points

### The candidate will want to carry its provenance

It is one field and it would answer 12's queued item and it is out of scope. The reason is not tidiness:
a decision that says *B is why* is a claim about B, and this whole experiment is an argument that a
record should assert nothing about another's past. The temptation and the thesis point in opposite
directions, which is why the boundary names it.

### *Take* will be asked to stretch into *copy*

The distance is one field. A record that admits a foreign entry **with the foreign instant** has copied,
and the operation looks the same from every angle except the one that matters. Phase 1 reads the
instant it wrote.

### The Event exception will be read as a defect

It is what a chain is. An `EventId` contains its predecessor, so a chain is an ordering that is part of
identity, and two diverged chains exchanging Events by identity would mean the ordering was not. The
finding, if S4 holds, is that a merge of two chains is a different operation from a merge of two sets —
not that something is missing.

### The subject can make S3 true and can make it false

An arrangement where the records share everything after the base makes `learn` empty; one where they
share nothing makes it everything. Both are producible and neither is an answer, which is why the
subject is required to produce all three and the criteria require the degenerate ones to be named.

## Experimental Principle

Reconstruction asked whether meaning survives.

Divergence asked whether deliberation survives.

Corroboration asked whether the record can be trusted without being believed.

Convergence asked whether two lines of deliberation can still reach each other.

Provenance asked whether a record that can be rebuilt perfectly is thereby understood.

Coordination asked whether a record built for one mind can hold two.

Exploration asked what a record owes the worlds nobody chose.

Atomicity asked who the record's promises are to.

Contention asked whether a promise made to one writer is a promise at all when there are two.

Collision asked whether two records that have never met were already the same record.

Witness asked whether a decision is about everything it happened to come after.

Veracity asked whether a record that agrees with itself has thereby told the truth.

Commensurability asked whether two records that disagree have a common measure anyway.

Indexicality asked whether a decision can say where it stood to somebody who was not standing there.

Individuation asked whether two records that learned the same thing on different days learned one thing.

```text
This one asks whether a record can take what
another knows without claiming to have known it.
```

Four experiments tried to move a decision from one record to another and each found the same wall: the
decision carries a claim about a past that is not the receiver's. This asks whether the wall is the
carrying rather than the decision — and whether a record that stops trying to hold another's past can
hold everything else.

If it can, the row's oldest item is answered by an operation nobody has to invent, because Synthesis
already answered the same question one layer up. If it cannot, then two records really do exchange all
or nothing, and that is a smaller system than four results have been assuming.
