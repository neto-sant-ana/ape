# Candidate — Training

**This is not a protocol.** A protocol is written when its experiment begins, with the previous
findings in hand, and is not edited afterwards to match what happened. This is the material that would
be in front of whoever writes it.

It is the only candidate here that is not about what a record must **hold**. It is about what a record
is **for**, which is why it belongs to neither row: the frontier row asks what an application must do
to carry meaning, the agents row asks whether an agent can express a decision through the primitives,
and this asks whether the thing those two built is a substrate for teaching decision reasoning.

---

## The question

> *Is APE's structure a harness for training decision reasoning?*

Stated so that it is not a hope: the claim would be that a record which keeps **the alternatives that
were weighed and not taken, under the knowledge that stood when they were weighed** is the shape a
decision-reasoning signal needs — and that APE keeps exactly that, for reasons that had nothing to do
with training.

---

## Where the question began, and it is not this file

Experiment 06, exploration, was written to ask what a record owes the worlds nobody chose. Its answer
is the material:

> **A record can drop what nothing points at. A reader can find only what something points at.**

Bounded and auditable are not two goods competing for the same bytes — they are one predicate read from
two sides. And the number that matters here is the one it measured almost in passing:

> **Abandoned siblings.** No longer deferred, and they are the majority of the record: **eleven of
> thirteen** decisions.

A record whose majority is roads not taken is unusual, and it is unusual on purpose. Every other
system of this kind keeps what happened; this one keeps what was considered, because a Thesis is
decided rather than admitted and a lineage is a tree rather than a line.

The agents row supplies the other half of the framing, from its founding question:

> What looks like operational noise under one framing — candidates weighed and abandoned, sitting
> permanently in history — is under this one the record that makes an audit possible at all.

And the property it singles out as the one that is not a restatement:

> **Auditable** is the one that is not a restatement. A record can be perfectly reconstructible and
> still not settle whether a decision was defensible, because defensibility is a claim about the
> alternatives that existed and were not taken.

*Defensibility is a claim about the alternatives that existed and were not taken* is the sentence this
candidate is built on. Anything that could teach or grade decision reasoning needs those alternatives,
and nine experiments have been keeping them.

---

## What each result contributes

**06 — the substrate, and its price.** Eleven of thirteen worlds are unchosen; what they cost is
measured on both sides of the choice; and the one thing the record cannot say is which of them was
*weighed* rather than *meant*. That last gap is exploration's request 2, and it is directly load-bearing
here: a signal that cannot tell an intention from a consideration is a signal that treats every
abandoned world as a mistake.

**01 to 04 of the agents row — four runs of a decision and a justification.** Every run produced a
decision expressible through the primitives and a justification in prose that the record has no place
for. The prose was sorted, compared against the derived record, and found wrong in specific ways — a
narrative mismatch is an experiment in that row. That is a graded pair: a decision, its stated reason,
and a derivation that can contradict the reason.

**02-hindsight — the distinction any grader needs and no graph has.** *What could have been known is
not what was consulted.* A record shows the first and not the second, which bounds what a reader can
ever conclude about how a decision was reached.

**05, 08 — a decision is placed by when, not by who.** What ordered everything in the multiagent run
was the instant each party recorded at, not the party. If the record is indifferent to *who* and strict
about *when*, then whatever a harness teaches is about reasoning under a knowledge state rather than
about an actor.

## What the row's own non-goals forbid, and this must not collide with them

The agents row excluded, in writing:

```text
an agent framework
a benchmark of any model's judgment
research into prompting
an argument that APE is for AI
a case for adding governance, audit or explainability features to the engine
```

Three of the five sit close to this candidate. The distinction that would have to hold is that a
*harness* is not a *benchmark of judgment*: the claim under test would be about whether the structure
supplies the signal, not about how well any model scores against it. The moment the protocol starts
comparing models, it is the excluded thing.

And the fourth is the sharpest constraint: **an argument that APE is for AI** is explicitly not what
the row is doing. This candidate has to be written so that a *no* is a real outcome — that the
structure is a good record and not a training substrate — or it is advocacy with an experiment attached.

---

## What is not decided here

Which of at least three readings it is, and they are three different experiments:

```text
a model taught the primitives     so that an agent uses them without prompt scaffolding.
                                  Nearest to the row's excluded prompting research, and the
                                  hardest to keep honest

the record as material            lineages as examples: knowledge, the worlds weighed, the
                                  one taken, and the derived consequence of each. What is
                                  being learned is choosing under a knowledge state

the record as the grader          the derivation as the thing a claimed reason is held to.
                                  This is the one the agents row has already exercised four
                                  times without calling it that — every narrative it sorted
                                  against the derived record was this, by hand
```

The third is the cheapest to test and the only one with four runs of prior art. The second is the one
that would need the history nobody has, which is the same missing thing every *cost* deferral has
named. The first is the one that would need a decision about whether this repository is doing that at
all.

Naming which, and whether *no* is an acceptable answer, is the protocol's first job.
