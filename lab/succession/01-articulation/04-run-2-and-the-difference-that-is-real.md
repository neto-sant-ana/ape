# Observation 4 — Run 2, and a carving difference that is real

The instrument repaired, the guards added, three fresh agents under the same isolation — with the
addition the operator named and one this laboratory found while running: `lab/`, the session
scratchpad, **and Run 1's own directory**, which held three `answers.md` a Run 2 agent could have
read.

```text
                 run 1   run 2    housed     unhoused established
A  flat              6      17     12 / 19    28, 30, 32, 33, 34
B  per entity        4      16     11 / 19    28, 30, 32, 33, 34
C  per decision      4      16     13 / 19    28, 30, 34
                                   ─────────
baseline                            19 / 17
```

Run 1's numbers were the instrument almost entirely. All three carvings now sit in the baseline's
neighbourhood, and A lands **exactly on the independent classifier's 17**.

## Five unhoused claims were established, and they are one of each kind

Run 1: zero. Run 2: five, and the five are not a cluster.

```text
28   loss            the witnesses finance's own decisions stood on
30   qualification   the new witnesses are true of merged, not finance's claims
32   want            no way to distinguish took-it-here from retook-it-there
33   exposition      the record never says why a fork introduced what it did
34   roadnottaken    the attribution chosen, and what it cost
```

One from each of five categories, established by readers who had never seen the categories. That is
`00-testimony`'s classification meeting three independent tests and surviving them.

**And the mechanism is the third arm.** All three agents, independently and unprompted, found the
same thing and named it the same way:

> Finance's arm records its two decisions as **witnessing 20 entries**; the same two decisions in
> the merged arm witness **21**. Everything else finance held reappears unchanged.

That single visible delta carries 28, 29, 30 and half of 34 — and it was unreachable in Run 1 by
construction, because only one arm had been carved. The claim the original agent marked as *the one
real loss* is now something a later reader **derives** rather than takes on trust.

## The carving difference, and it is not the one P2 predicted

The totals are 17 / 16 / 16 and say almost nothing. The **sets** do.

```text
union          18
intersection   14
only C          5
A vs B          differ on 29 alone
B vs C          differ on 5, 29, 32, 33
```

C reaches **more housed** (13) than A (12) or B (11), and **fewer unhoused** (3 against 5). The two
it loses are 32 and 33, and both are the same shape: a claim that the record **cannot** say
something. The two agents gave opposite reasons for the same pair, each in its own words:

> **B** — absence-claims are establishable, because the entity pages expose their whole field set:
> no field anywhere records who re-applied a decision, or why a fork introduced what it did.
>
> **C** — showing the record has *no way* to express re-application needs the schema, and these
> rendered pages are not a schema.

**Both are right about the carving they were handed**, and that is the finding: a page per entity is
a walk through the record's field set, so a reader can enumerate what is absent; a page per decision
never shows a reader the shape of an entity, only the decisions that mention one.

```text
what a carving lets a reader establish about what the record CANNOT hold
  depends on whether the carving shows the reader the record's FIELDS
```

P2 predicted B would win on motivation and C on accountability and own reasoning. Neither happened.
What happened is orthogonal to it, and it is the first thing in this experiment that behaves like a
property of carving rather than of the instrument.

## One disagreement between readers, and one of them is probably right

C established claim 5 — *each party's decisions name that party in `by`*. A **refused it as
contradicted**: the genesis decision reads `taken by: nobody` in every arm, so the universal fails on
its own terms.

A's reading looks right, and it is the same fact three Run 1 agents found from the other end. It is
recorded as a disagreement rather than resolved here, because resolving it is re-classifying a claim
after seeing what agents made of it — which is `00-testimony`'s business and not this experiment's.

## What is still not measured

**P4.** Cost was to be *bytes opened and pages traversed per question answered*. All three agents
read everything they were given before answering — A parsed and diffed the three arms mechanically,
B read all 19 files "in one sweep", C read all ten. With every page opened by every reader, the
denominator exists and the numerator does not vary. **That is itself worth reporting**: at this size,
a carving does not change what a reader opens, because a careful reader opens all of it.
