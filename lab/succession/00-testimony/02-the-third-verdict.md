# Observation 02 — The protocol's binary did not survive the first testimony

**A deviation, taken during Phase 2 rather than pre-registered.** It is written here, on its own,
because the row's whole hazard is a reader adjusting the instrument to fit what they wanted to find,
and a deviation buried in a result document is that with extra steps.

## What the protocol said

Two questions of every claim: **housed?**, and if not, **which of the five kinds?** A claim fitting
none of the five is *unclassified with its text*, never forced into the nearest box.

## What the first testimony did to it

Thirteen of `01-single-agent`'s forty-eight claims are the agent explaining **what the engine is**:

```text
That is the engine's division and not a workaround: a Commitment is knowledge, a Thesis is
which knowledge you mean.

Three hypotheses exist and they differ in what they assume about when unsettled commitments
land, never in what is known.

Cancelling is what removes an intention's movement; omitting is what removes the intention.
```

**Asking whether a record houses those is the wrong question**, and both answers are wrong in the same
way. *Unhoused* says the record is missing something — it is not; a record is not supposed to hold a
tutorial, and a representation that grew a field for this would be worse. *Housed* says the record
carries it, which is false.

They are also not the protocol's *skipped*: they are not headings, not restatements of the task, and
not narration. Every one is a true, load-bearing sentence somebody would want to read. They are just
not about **this** record.

## The deviation

A third verdict, `Exposition`, outside the housed/unhoused count and reported separately.

**Why it is not a widening of the five kinds**, which the protocol forbids: the kinds classify
*unhoused material a record might want a home for*. This is material a record should **not** want. The
two are on different axes, and adding a verdict is something the protocol had already done once — the
skip list is a third verdict that happened to be written in advance.

## What it costs, stated rather than argued away

**It makes P2 easier to confirm, and by a lot.** P2 predicted fewer than one in five unhoused claims
would land unclassified. Run 01 produced **zero** — but thirteen claims that would have been
unclassified under the protocol as written are now in a category of their own. Read the strict way,
run 01's unclassified rate is 13 of 29, which refutes P2 rather than confirming it.

So the honest statement of the result is two-headed, and both heads have to survive to the end:

```text
under the protocol as written        13 of 29 record-relevant claims fit none of the five kinds
under the protocol as amended        0 of 16, and 13 claims are about the engine instead
```

**The second is the reading this experiment argues for, and the first is the one a sceptic gets.** Both
numbers are carried in `99-result.md`, and P2 is answered against both.

**And the amendment was made after reading one testimony of eight**, which is the weakest moment to
change an instrument — early enough to shape everything, late enough to be shaped by what was found.
What makes it defensible rather than convenient is that `Exposition` is *not* available as a home for
anything the row wants: nothing in H4 asks for a representation that holds an explanation of APE, so
the category cannot absorb a claim that would otherwise support the hypothesis.

**What would make it indefensible** is if it started collecting claims that are about the record after
all. The check for that is Phase 4: the second classifier is asked the housed question **only**, with
no knowledge of this category, so a claim this reading called exposition and they called housed is a
disagreement that surfaces as a number.

## Why it is worth having anyway

It is a finding, not only an accounting change. **A third of the first agent's testimony is teaching**,
and the record has no reader to teach — which is a fact about what an agent produces when asked to
explain itself, and it bears on H5 rather than on H4. A navigable asset for a human and for an agent
indiscriminately may need exactly this, and no APE primitive will ever carry it, because it is not
about any particular record.

Carried to the queue rather than resolved here.
