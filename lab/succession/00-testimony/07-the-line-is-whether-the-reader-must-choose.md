# Observation 07 — The line is not the audience. It is whether the reader has a decision to make

**A correction to [`05`](05-the-kinds-depend-on-the-audience.md), and the third formulation of one
split.** That is a warning sign and it is written here rather than smoothed over.

## The measurement, seven of eight

```text
SingleAgent          0 of 16      a technical report
Hindsight            0 of 35      a technical audit
MultiagentA          1 of 14      a technical report
MultiagentB          1 of 20      a technical report
MultiagentBPrime     0 of 25      a technical report
                     ---------
                     2 of 110     1.8%

NarrativeMismatch    7 of 17      a letter to the house's principal
MultiagentReader     8 of 22      a technical report, asked what operations should DO
                     ---------
                    15 of  39     38%
```

**Twenty times apart**, and `MultiagentReader` is what moved the line.

## Why `05`'s formulation does not survive it

`05` said the kinds are a property of the **audience** — technician against principal. `run-reader`
is as technical as anything in the corpus: `HistoricalUnavailability`, `OnDueDateInAnyOrder`,
identities quoted to sixty-four characters, two probe repositories kept so its claims can be re-run.
It sits with `03-narrative-mismatch` all the same.

`06`'s formulation — the **task**, report against advise — is closer and still not it, because
`03-narrative-mismatch` was not asked to advise. It was asked to explain, and it volunteered *"Going
forward, a decision should join the plan on the day it is made"* with nobody asking.

## The formulation that fits both

> **The overflow appears when the reader of the testimony has a decision to make.**

The five reporting runs are written for somebody who will **check**: here is what I did, here is what
the record says, here is where I could be wrong. The two others are written for somebody who will
**choose** — and choosing is what accountability, recommendation, an account of one's own reasoning,
and a verdict on the system are all *for*.

It explains the two routes rather than picking one. `03` was addressed to the principal, who acts.
`run-reader` was addressed to operations, who acts. One volunteered the advice and one was asked for
it; the reader's position is the same either way.

## What this costs, and it is not nothing

**Three formulations for one split, each fitted after the data that broke the last.** The first two
were not wrong about their own runs — they were narrower than the phenomenon, and each was written
as though it were the phenomenon.

So this one is **pre-registered against the testimony that remains**, before it is read, which is the
only way the third formulation is worth more than the first two:

> `05-reconciliation/run-a` is a technical report to an operator, addressed to somebody who will
> check rather than choose. **It should land near zero — under 3 of its non-housed claims.** If it
> comes back near a third, the split is not what any of the three observations says it is, and the
> honest reading becomes that the rate varies for reasons this experiment has not found.

That is recorded here, and in the commit, before the file is opened.

## And a second thing this run settles

**P5 is answerable now, and it is not what the protocol predicted.** The protocol expected the reader
to produce *fewer roads-not-taken and more losses* than the writers. What separates `run-reader` from
the writers is nothing of the kind — it is the overflow above, which the protocol did not have a name
for.

The two readers do not resemble each other either: `02-hindsight` is 0 of 35 and `run-reader` is 8 of
22. **Reading is not the variable.** P5 is answered *no* in Phase 5, and the reason it is answerable
at all is [`03-the-corpus-had-two-readers.md`](03-the-corpus-had-two-readers.md) — with one reader it
would have been a sample of one and the *no* would have been unavailable.

## The plainest H4 sentence in the corpus is in this run

Worth pulling out, because an agent wrote it with no knowledge that the hypothesis existed:

> *A fork states omitted and introduced and **never says that another line of thinking is why**;
> `report.json` is not in the record either, and a question nobody acted on is not part of one.*

And the instruction that follows it: **record the provenance somewhere the fork is not.**

That is H4 — a home for what reaches no primitive, *beside* the entity — asked for by name, in an
agent's own words, in a testimony from before the question was written down.
