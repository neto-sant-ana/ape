# Observation 03 — The corpus had two readers, and Phase 0 said one

A correction to [`01-the-corpus.md`](01-the-corpus.md), found in Phase 2 and written where a reader
will meet it rather than folded into the document it corrects.

## What Phase 0 published

> **Two boundaries and one reader** […] `run-reader` is the control, and P5 is about it.

## What the second testimony says about itself

> *The audit reads only — it admits nothing to the canon and stores nothing in the archive.*
>
> — `02-hindsight/run-01/ANSWER.md`, last line

It is an audit. It was handed one world identity and asked how the account got there, whether any
decision was unsound when taken, and what alternatives existed. **It decided nothing**, and the
sentence above is the agent saying so unprompted, in the section on reproducing its work.

## Where the error came from, which is worth more than the error

Phase 0 sorted the corpus by **which experiment** each run belonged to, and experiment 04 is the one
with a run literally named `run-reader`. That name did the classifying. Nobody asked the other seven
what they had done, because the question looked answered.

**It is the same defect this row exists to study, committed by the row.** A name is not a record of
what happened; the record of what happened is in the testimony, and the testimony was right there. A
reader who trusted the corpus document over the corpus would have got P5's control wrong.

## What it changes

**P5 gets a better control than it was pre-registered with.** One reader against seven writers is a
sample of one; two readers against six writers is still small, and it is twice as much, and the two
readers differ in a way that is useful:

```text
02-hindsight      audits an existing record it did not make, constructs alternative worlds
                  in memory to interpret them, and persists nothing
04-run-reader     reads. Not yet classified — its testimony is fifth in the fixed order
```

**And it sharpens what the line is measuring.** The distinction is not *did the agent build a Thesis*
— `hindsight` built several, to enumerate what the house could have done. It is **what the run left
behind**, because P5 asks whether the kinds an agent produces depend on what it was doing to the
record, and a world constructed and discarded leaves the record as it was.

`Run::wrote` now says so, and names this file.

## What it does not change

**Nothing in the classification.** No claim's verdict depends on whether its run wrote, and the
sixteen unhoused claims of `02-hindsight`'s *What I could not determine* are what they are either way.
P5 is answered in Phase 5, from counts that are computed rather than stored.

**And the digests stand.** The corpus is the same eight files it was; what was wrong was a sentence
about them.
