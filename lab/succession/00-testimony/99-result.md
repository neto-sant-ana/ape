**Confirmed with a split**, and four of five predictions wrong.

> **Phase 4 ran: 36 of 46, 78%** — [`09`](09-what-the-second-classifier-said.md). A second classifier
> that had read none of this answered the housed question alone, and the comparison was fixed before
> it was invoked. Ten disagreements, and six of them are about **where a claim ends** rather than
> about the record: it sank a sentence on its weakest conjunct where this reading classified by
> operative content, and the protocol never specified which.
>
> **The check built to falsify this experiment's own amendment fired, and the amendment survived it.**
> One exposition claim came back established — and the diagnosis is an extraction defect of this
> reading's, not `Exposition` absorbing what the record holds. It is the first of the three times this
> row's hazard fired on the classifier that a *mechanism* caught.
>
> What that bounds is P1 below, by a few points either way, and nothing else.
>
> The banner is below the verdict rather than above it because `cli/tests/pedigree.rs` reads the first
> non-empty line after the heading — and it accepted this banner as a verdict when it was first. See
> the queue.

> *What did eight agents say that the record cannot hold — and is what is left over a bounded set of
> kinds, or is it prose?*

**It is a bounded set of kinds, and there are two sets.** Which one an agent produces is decided by
whether the reader of its testimony has a decision to make.

```text
423 claims across 8 testimonies, 13,382 words

housed        202     the record already holds it
exposition     50     about the ENGINE, not about this record
unhoused      154     five kinds
unclassified   17     none of the five, and all but two from two testimonies
```

## The predictions

```text
P1  most of the corpus is housed              SPLIT — 54% one way, 48% the other
P2  the leftover is bounded, under 1 in 5     CONFIRMED overall, and BIMODAL
P3  no unhoused kind is about the domain      REFUTED in the letter, holds in the spirit
P4  road-not-taken is the largest kind        REFUTED — method-limit is
P5  the reader differs from the writers       REFUTED — reading is not the variable
```

**P1 has no single answer and the two numbers are close enough that it matters.** Excluding
exposition — which is not about the record — housed is 202 of 373, **54%**. Exclude the markdown
table rows as well, because a cast list is the cheapest housed claim there is, and it is 146 of 305:
**48%**. The prediction is confirmed on one reading and refuted on the other, and the honest
statement is neither:

> **About half of what an agent says about a decision is already in the record, and about half is
> not.** Not *most*, either way.

**P2 is confirmed overall and the overall number hides the finding.** 17 unclassified of 171
non-housed is 10%. Split by whom the testimony addresses, it is 2 of 110 against 15 of 39 — see
below.

**And P2 has a second answer, promised in [`02`](02-the-third-verdict.md) and owed here.** Under the
protocol *as written*, before `Exposition` existed, those 50 claims would have been unclassified: 67
of 221, **30%**, refuted. The reading this experiment argues for is the first; the number a sceptic
gets is the second; both are carried because the amendment was made after reading one testimony of
eight.

**P3 is refuted in the letter.** Several unhoused claims are about the domain — a penalty owed to a
third party, goods that arrive, the direction of an invoice, what a magnitude counts in. But every
one of them is a limit of the **arrangement** rather than of the ontology: each is expressible by
composition above the engine, and simply was not admitted into the world the agent was handed. The
heavier finding the protocol feared — *the ontology is missing something operational* — did not
appear.

**P4 is refuted, and by the kind nobody predicted.**

```text
MethodLimit   35     how the agent verified its own work
Want          34
RoadNotTaken  30
Qualification 28
Loss          27
```

The largest kind is not about the record at all. **A third of what is unhoused is an agent telling
you how far to trust it** — what it measured, what it assumed, what it could not make go red. Roads
not taken are third, and not rare, so the protocol's own refutation clause — *the roads are in the
record and only their reasons are not* — is the reading that survives.

**P5 is answered no**, and it is answerable only because the corpus turned out to have two readers
rather than the one Phase 0 published — [`03`](03-the-corpus-had-two-readers.md). The two do not
resemble each other: `02-hindsight` is 0 of 35 and `run-reader` is 8 of 22. Reading is not the
variable.

## The finding

```text
written for somebody who will CHECK     2 of 110 unclassified     1.8%
  01-single-agent, 02-hindsight, 04-a, 04-b, 04-b-prime, 05-reconciliation

written for somebody who will CHOOSE   15 of  39 unclassified      38%
  03-narrative-mismatch, 04-run-reader
```

> **The kinds an agent produces are decided by the position of its reader.** Write for somebody who
> will check and you get wants, losses, qualifications and method limits — a bounded set, and the
> record can be measured against it. Write for somebody who will **choose** and a second set appears:
> accountability, a recommendation, an account of one's own reasoning, and a verdict on the system.

`03-narrative-mismatch` is a letter to a principal and `run-reader` is as technical as anything in the
corpus. They land together, which is what ruled out *audience* and *task* as the discriminator —
[`05`](05-the-kinds-depend-on-the-audience.md), [`06`](06-motivation-is-not-one-of-the-five.md),
[`07`](07-the-line-is-whether-the-reader-must-choose.md), in that order, each fitted after the data
that broke the last.

**Three formulations is a warning and it is recorded as one.** The third was pre-registered against
`05-reconciliation` before that file was opened, and it landed at 0 of 22 as predicted — which
confirms nothing, because the five kinds were derived from that testimony and fitting them is close
to a tautology. **The split rests on the seven testimonies the categories did not come from.**

## What this hands H4

**H4's material is at least two shapes, and only one of them has an anchor.**

```text
motivation       attaches to ONE object and explains that object — a reader of that
                 commitment is the reader who needs it
accountability   attaches to a person
recommendation   attaches to the future
own reasoning    attaches to the author
evaluation       attaches to the system
```

A representation designed against the first would have no place for the other four, and a
representation that tried to hold all five would be holding things with nothing in common but being
unhoused. **That is the question the next H4 experiment inherits, and it is sharper than the one this
one was given.**

And the corpus asked for it by name, twice, before the hypothesis existed:

> *A fork states omitted and introduced and **never says that another line of thinking is why**;
> `report.json` is not in the record either, and a question nobody acted on is not part of one.*
> — `04-multiagent/run-reader`, followed by: **record the provenance somewhere the fork is not.**

> *Nothing says what a commitment is **for**. In the record, "a courier slot costing 20" and "an
> inventory purchase of 60" are the same object with different numbers.* — `04-multiagent/run-a`

## What outlasts the verdict, and it is not about H4

**Two of twenty-one wants were selectable when this began.** A want or a loss now carries where it
stands, and the distinction that matters is not tracked-against-untracked:

```text
tracked     29     in QUEUE.md or a candidate — SELECTABLE
recorded     9     in a result document, and nowhere the queue reads
met          3     served since
by design    7     the record deliberately does not carry it
untracked   13
```

> **`Recorded` is not `Tracked`.** A finding in a result document is findable by somebody who already
> knows to look, and invisible to selection: the queue orders items *in the queue*, and ripeness — the
> laboratory's only stated ordering signal — cannot reach what never entered it.

`agents/01` recorded two frictions in its result at the very first experiment of that row, and neither
is in `QUEUE.md`. `agents/04`'s four requests are one sentence in it. **Nothing says which experiments'
findings entered and which did not**, and that is the mechanism behind the drift the charter was
written to stop, measured rather than argued.

**Twenty-two items are owed to the queue**, derived by the suite rather than transcribed, and added
with this result.

**And the three met wants are the only evidence the corpus has about what happens after a boundary
grows.** *Who took each decision* was met by `Taken.by`, and experiment 17 then measured that `by`
names who **took** rather than who relayed — the want was served and the serving exposed the real
gap. *The set of alternatives is a lower bound because nothing enumerates* was met by the journal, and
the queue's own re-run debt says whether an auditor lifts the caveat is unmeasured. Neither is a fix;
both are chains.

## Method

**Every one of the 423 claims is quoted from the testimony it is attributed to**, checked by the suite
against the file, whitespace and markdown normalised, elisions required side by side and in order. It
caught a real paraphrase of fourteen table rows before anybody tried to break it, and one blockquote,
and it was broken on purpose three times.

**Three amendments to the protocol, all recorded as deviations rather than folded in**: `Exposition`
([`02`](02-the-third-verdict.md)), `Standing` ([`04`](04-a-want-has-a-standing.md)), and the
reformulated split ([`07`](07-the-line-is-whether-the-reader-must-choose.md)).

**And the row's stated hazard fired three times, on the person doing the classifying.**

```text
03   a directory's name trusted over the testimony       caught by the corpus
04   a search for the agent's words over the lab's       caught by the operator
09   a quotation cut inside its own frame                caught by the guard
```

The third is the one that matters for method: the row opened saying its subject is prose and prose is
classified by somebody with an interest in the outcome, and the answer it offered was a second
classifier. **The answer worked** — and it found the one defect a human reader of this classification
could not have, because a quotation cut inside another quotation passes every guard that checks
quotations.
