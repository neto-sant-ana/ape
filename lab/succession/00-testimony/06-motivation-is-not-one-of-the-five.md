# Observation 06 — Motivation is not one of the five, and the technical reports produce one each

[`05`](05-the-kinds-depend-on-the-audience.md) said what would test the audience reading:

> If they come back near zero, the audience reading holds. If one of them produces accountability
> without being asked for it, it does not.

Two of `04-multiagent`'s four are now read. **Neither produced accountability**, and both produced
exactly one claim fitting none of the five.

```text
SingleAgent         0 of 16    a technical report
Hindsight           0 of 35    a technical audit
NarrativeMismatch   7 of 17    a letter to the house's principal
MultiagentA         1 of 14    a technical report
MultiagentB         1 of 20    a technical report
```

**The reading holds**, and the shape of the two exceptions is what the observation is for.

## The two, and they are not the same thing

**`run-a` produced a bare motivation.** Explaining why the new commitment reuses the old one's parties
and statement:

> *because the purchase takes the slot's place in the same relationship, and only its size and
> deadline differ.*

No alternative is named, so it is not a road not taken. Nothing is asked for, so it is not a want. It
is **why the thing was built the way it was built**, and the five kinds have nowhere to put it.

**`run-b` produced a deferral.** Having explained that the nature of an expense would need new
vocabulary:

> *If the house ever needs to tell a storage payable from a freight one, that is the shape the answer
> will have to take, and **it is a decision above my level here**.*

That is the *recommendation* shape `03-narrative-mismatch` produced four times, arriving in a technical
report — so recommendation is not purely an artefact of writing to a principal. It is rarer there, and
it is not absent.

## Why motivation belongs in the record and the other shapes may not

**This is the distinction the experiment now has to carry to the end**, because H4 is about a home for
material *beside the entity*, and not everything unhoused wants one.

```text
motivation      attaches to ONE object, and explains that object. "This commitment is built
                from the slot it replaces, because it takes its place." A reader of that
                commitment is the reader who needs it

accountability  attaches to a person, not to an object
recommendation  attaches to the future, not to the record
own reasoning   attaches to the author
evaluation      attaches to the system
```

**Only the first has a natural anchor in the record**, and the anchor is what a representation needs.
The other four are true, useful, and about something the record does not contain — which is not a
reason to discard them, but is a reason not to assume one shape of home fits all five.

## And `run-b` is where two agents disagreed about the record itself

Not unclassified, and worth naming here because it is the strongest argument in the corpus that H4's
material is load-bearing rather than decorative.

`run-a` made the house accountable and the house the executor. `run-b` made the house accountable and
**finance** the executor, and said outright:

> *The alternative — finance accountable […] — is equally defensible, and the two produce **different
> commitments and therefore different identities**.*

Two agents, one vocabulary, one brief, two admissible readings — and because identity is
content-addressed, the choice is not presentation. It produces a different object. The reason for
choosing was written in prose by both, and the record holds neither.

## What is still open

**Three testimonies remain**, and the sharper question they carry is no longer whether the audience
reading holds:

> Does **motivation** recur? Two occurrences in five is not a kind. If it appears in the remaining
> three, the five that the protocol fixed were five of six, and the sixth is the one H4 most needs.

`04-multiagent/run-reader` is next, and it is the second of the corpus's two readers — so it answers
P5 at the same time.
