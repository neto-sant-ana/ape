# Observation 05 — The kinds depend on who the testimony is talking to

**P2 is refuted for one testimony of three, and the split is not noise.** It falls exactly on who the
agent was writing for.

```text
SingleAgent         0 of 16 unclassified     a technical report
Hindsight           0 of 35 unclassified     a technical audit
NarrativeMismatch   7 of 17 unclassified     a letter to the house's principal
```

P2 predicted fewer than one in five. Two runs produced **zero**. The third produced **41%**.

## What is different about the third

`03-narrative-mismatch/run-b` was asked to explain the situation to the house's principal, and it
contains **no APE vocabulary at all** — no Thesis, no cut, no commitment, no hypothesis. The three
feasibility hypotheses arrive as:

> *assuming everything simply settles eventually, assuming each promise is paid on the day it falls
> due, and assuming the worst ordering of anything falling on the same day*

That is the same claim `01-single-agent` made as *"`FinalState`, `OnDueDateNet` and
`OnDueDateInAnyOrder`"*, and both are housed. **The translation costs nothing and changes nothing** —
which is a small H5 result on its own, arrived at by an agent nobody asked to translate.

## The seven, and they are four shapes

Not scattered. Every one is something a person asks of another person and never of a record.

```text
accountability   "That is the failure, and it is mine, not the counterparty's and not the
                 market's."
                 "That the shortfall exists at all is my error."

recommendation   "Going forward, a decision should join the plan on the day it is made."
                 "I would want it recorded the day it is agreed, not the day it arrives."

own reasoning    "The first time the answer changed my mind in time."

evaluation       "that is not luck either — it is the direct result of the discipline above."
                 "That you are hearing about it now […] is the system doing what it was built
                 to do."
```

## Why this is the sharpest thing the corpus has said about H4

**The five kinds were derived from `05-reconciliation`, which is a technical report to an operator.**
[`00-protocol.md`](00-protocol.md) says so, and says the candidate set is tested against the seven
that did not produce it. That test has now found the limit, and the limit is not that the categories
are wrong:

> **The kinds are a property of the audience, not of the record.** Ask an agent to report to a
> technician and it produces wants, losses, qualifications and method limits. Ask the same boundary,
> the same world, the same engine to explain itself to the person who has to act, and it produces
> accountability, a recommendation, an account of its own reasoning, and a verdict on the system.

Which reframes H4's question. *A home for what reaches no primitive* is not one shape of material —
it is at least two, and which one you get depends on who is being addressed. A representation designed
against the technical set would have no place for **any** of the seven above.

## And one of the four shapes is the most load-bearing thing in the row

**`own reasoning`** — *"the first time the answer changed my mind in time"* — is
[`02-hindsight`](../../agents/02-hindsight/99-result.md)'s central absence, arriving from the other
side. That audit's whole qualifier is:

> *What the graph does not show is whether anyone looked: projections are derived and never stored, so
> there is no record of a feasibility check having been run, or skipped.*

Here is an agent **asserting that it looked, and that looking changed the outcome** — in prose, in a
record that cannot corroborate it. The auditor said the record cannot say whether anyone checked; the
author said it did check. Neither claim can meet the other, and both are in the corpus.

That is `auditable`'s remaining half, stated by two agents who never met, from opposite ends. It is
the H2 material this experiment was not looking for.

## What it does not do

**It does not amend anything.** The five kinds stay fixed and the seven stay unclassified with their
text, which is what [`00-protocol.md`](00-protocol.md) requires and what makes the number above
readable at all. A sixth kind fitted now would be the instrument moving to fit the data for the third
time in one experiment.

**And it does not settle P2**, which is answered in Phase 5 across eight testimonies. Five remain, and
the useful question they now carry is a sharper one than the protocol asked:

> Do the other five split the same way — and is the boundary *audience*, or is
> `03-narrative-mismatch` simply the one run that was asked to be candid?

`04-multiagent`'s four testimonies are next, and they are technical reports. If they come back near
zero, the audience reading holds. If one of them produces accountability without being asked for it,
it does not.
