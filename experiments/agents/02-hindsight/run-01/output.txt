8. WHAT THE GRAPH DOES NOT CARRY
==============================================================================

questions this graph was interrogated for and did not answer:

  · why the cancellation happened. the event carries an observation name
    ('Cancelled'), an occurred_at (2026-01-06) and a recorded_at (2026-01-06). there is no field
    for a reason and none for who observed it.

  · who took each decision. a Thesis carries parent, cut and selection — the
    fields its identity is derived from. no author, no timestamp of its own.

  · when each decision was physically taken. the cut is *declared* knowledge,
    not attested provenance; the layer says so itself. the only ordering
    evidence recovered above is indirect: a cut naming a head the instant no
    longer addresses.

  · whether anyone ever ran feasibility. nothing records that a projection was
    taken; projections are derived and never stored.

  · the order of commitments recorded on one civil day. recording time is a
    date, and a commitment never enters the event chain, so there is nothing
    finer to read. days carrying more than one selected commitment: 2026-01-06 (C3 and C2)
