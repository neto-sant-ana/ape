# The substrate preserved every answer

The harness now stands on the CLI. What the migration measured, before any agent has been asked
anything, is the mechanical half of the predictions — and it measured it by moving the harness and
leaving the assertions alone.

That discipline is the CLI's own, from its divergence result: *a concluded experiment's subject
does not move; its harness does*. Twenty-one assertions across five suites were carried over with
their values and their wording intact. None moved.

```text
world          2   the two options are still told apart, and the settled past still arrives
run 01         4   the decision still reads as it did, and still reads innocent under a later cut
hindsight      8   the obligation is still out of reach, and the genesis cut still cannot be rebuilt
narrative A    7   six claims still hold, and the contradicted one is still contradicted
narrative B    8   every number, both timings, all four remedies, and the minimum that clears
```

The contradicted claim keeping its verdict is the one worth naming. A sorting that had shifted
under a change of substrate would have been a sorting about the substrate, and it was not.

---

## What the migration cost

**The floor is not expressible.** The world is *cash may not go below zero*, with no ceiling. A
journal records `Between { lower, upper }` and nothing else, so the floor was restated as a range
whose upper bound nothing in the scenario reaches, and the ceiling takes no part in any verdict.

Every number above is unchanged by that restatement, which is why it was made rather than waited
on. The absence is deliberate on the other side — an unused form is left absent rather than
approximated — so a floor-only form is a need this experiment now has and hands over. It is not a
change made here.

**Constructing an option is no longer a call.** Where a suite used to admit a commitment through
the Canon, it now appends to a journal and lets a coordinate place the decision against it. Two
suites got shorter for it: a prefix of the sequence leaves both halves a comparison needs — the
world as it was decided, and a canon that has since moved — where before each had to be built
twice.

**The sequence form survived contact.** The `Step` script written so a briefing would carry no
narrative folds cleanly into the two records the repository keeps. It was already the shape one
of the CLI's four candidate repairs took, for a reason that had nothing to do with this, and it
needed no revision to sit on top of the one that was kept.

---

## One thing is new and is not a migration

Every decision now carries the journal entry that stood when it was taken, the same prefix said a
second way as a witness, and the party that took it. A guard asserts all three, and the third is
the one to watch: every decision in this scenario is claimed by the house, through the constructor
whose name says it is a claim.

Nothing about a decision produces that party, so nothing about a decision can contradict it. That
is prediction S5 stated in the substrate's own words, and it is now sitting in the record where an
agent can reach it.

---

## What has not happened

No agent has run over this. Three briefings and three runs are the next thing, and until they
exist this document reports a harness that stands up, not a result.
