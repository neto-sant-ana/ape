# Result

**Confirmed** for monotonicity. **Confirmed in its first clause and refuted in its second** for
exclusivity — the two are exclusive, and the arrangement the protocol called *neither* turns out to be
one of them.

```text
monotonicity  exploration is monotonic, and a monotonic record still answers
              everything it answered before                                    CONFIRMED

exclusivity   bounding the record and auditing the deliberation are exclusive
              under exploration                                                CONFIRMED

              …and the default arrangement is neither                          REFUTED
```

## The answer, and it is not the one the protocol was arguing about

The question was *can a record be bounded and auditable at the same time, under exploration?* No. And
the protocol expected to report that as a **cost curve with a name** — a price, paid in one of two
currencies, and an application's problem which to pay.

It is not a price. It is one predicate read from two sides:

> **A record can drop what nothing points at. A reader can find only what something points at.**

Bounded and auditable are not two goods competing for the same bytes. They are the same fact —
*something refers to this* — asked once by the pruner and once by the reader. Which is why no
arrangement can have both, and why the failure is not a matter of degree: at the moment an admission
stops being referred to, it becomes simultaneously removable and unfindable, and there is no state in
between.

Measured, in the most literal available form: a repository that weighed twelve candidates and a
repository that never explored are the **same bytes**, in all three files.

## Against each criterion

1. **Expressible through existing primitives, none added and none repurposed.** Twelve candidates were
   weighed in three arrangements using `Fork`, `Genesis`, `Taken` and the journal as they stood.
   `Candidate` — the word the protocol singled out as the dangerous repurposing, because
   `CandidateSelection` already means something in Synthesis — appears nowhere in the subject's
   vocabulary for an exploratory intention.
2. **Consequences derived, never asserted.** The objective calls `feasibility_under` and folds
   `movement_of`, and the two candidates the floor refuses are refused by the engine reporting
   `OutOfBounds` at −10 and −20. The harness asserts *that* those two were refused and never *why*.
3. **The objective and the hypothesis are outside the engine, and the record shows neither.** Both are
   constants in the subject; `Judged` is never persisted; and Phase 5's closed set enumerates every
   place the three files mention a commitment — four — none of which is an objective, a hypothesis or a
   score.
4. **Everything the record answered before, it answers identically after, by value.** Projections at
   three instants, a reading of every world, feasibility, and the applicability report the protocol
   pointed at — compared as whole values across twelve candidates weighed and dropped.
5. **Three arrangements, one budget fixed in advance, literals before the run.** With two corrections
   made in the open rather than adjusted, both recorded where they happened: E4 gained a second clause
   before Phase 1 ran, and Phase 2's arrangement was rebuilt because its watermark never moved. **And
   one honest limit** — the *counts* were pre-registered and the *bytes* were not. A byte count is a
   property of the encoding; every byte figure in these observations was read after the run and is
   reported, never predicted.
6. **A closed set, checked by a test, and derived rather than listed.** Walking the three files for an
   identity's own text, so the enumeration cannot go stale when a record type gains a field — with the
   four places asserted as a literal, because two empty sets compare equal and a broken scan would
   otherwise read as agreement.
7. **Nothing was added to the engine.** `core/` is untouched across the branch: zero files.
8. **The six earlier experiments' conclusions stand.** Two are sharpened and one is extended; the
   details are below. One of *this* experiment's own observations is corrected.

**Failure conditions.** No projection moved. Exploration needed no asserted consequence. The cost is
not the harness's way of exploring, and that is measured rather than argued: the two arrangements
produce byte-identical journals and — checked candidate by candidate — the same worlds and the same
verdicts, so the only difference between them is the record. A repository that could not be
reconstructed **did** occur, deliberately, and is a finding rather than a failure: it is refused by
name, and the refusal was repaired to name the entry at fault.

## Findings that outlast the verdict

**The cheap arrangement had to be built.** The protocol said arrangement A is the default nobody
chooses. In an application whose whole vocabulary exists for writing things down — `Decision`, `Taken`,
a witness, `worlds.json` — A was not available: weighing a world without keeping it needed a seam
nobody had cut. Where the vocabulary is the engine's, A is free and B costs a record; where it is a
repository's, B is free and A costs the seam. So E3's cost is what an application arrives at by not
thinking, and E2's silence is what it has to choose.

**The deliberation outgrows the knowledge.** 23,553 bytes of lineage against 11,089 of journal — the
record of what was considered is 2.1 times the record of what is known, at twelve candidates on a
subject with one resource. Of thirteen decisions, eleven record something no application intends to do.

**E3's shape is the upper bound, not the measurement.** The lineage grows as the *product* of candidates
and decisions only when the decisions are deferred. Interleaved, it is the triangular sum: 260
witnessed entries against 326, two repositories identical by every count the protocol asks for.
Batching reads as amortizing and costs 21% more.

**A repeat costs readability, and the write does not say so.** A readmission with anything distinct
learned between the two occurrences produces a repository that is written successfully and refused on
being read. Adjacent, it reconstructs — and that is not a success, since it passes only because a set
cannot tell fifteen distinct entries out of sixteen records from fifteen out of fifteen.

**Unidentifiable does not imply unprunable. It is the same fact as prunable.** Nothing referring to an
admission is simultaneously why no reader can find it and why nothing is blocked by removing it. This
corrects **this experiment's own Observation 2**, which reported the two halves of arrangement A as
failing in opposite directions; they were one property counted twice. Corrected in Observation 5 rather
than edited into Observation 2, because the order in which it was learned is part of the result.

**Two silences, and neither is the question.** Under A, considered-and-rejected equals never-considered.
Under B, it equals *intended* — so a repository that weighed twelve candidates answers *which
propositions were ever intended* with fourteen where one is. Structural rather than an oversight: the
record has exactly two places that speak about a commitment's relation to a world, and both mean the
same thing.

**Pruning is not a third arrangement.** It is an operation available on any of them, and what it can
reach is decided by what still refers to things. It reaches the unexplored repository from A for free,
and from B by discarding precisely what B paid for. The only thing that stops it is a decision taken
*after* the exploration — and what that decision's witness audits is that propositions were admitted,
never that they were weighed.

**Everything missing lives in the decision.** Requests 2 and 3 of Phase 6 — *I weighed this* as
distinct from *I mean this*, and *I preferred this one* — are both properties of a decision rather than
of a proposition. Provenance put where an intention came from there; coordination put who decided there;
this puts what a decision was *for*. Third time, same place.

## What changed in the experiments before it

**Nothing was overturned.**

**The sequence witness, a fourth time, and this one is a bill.** Convergence declined to call it
redundant; coordination made it load-bearing for append-only. Here it is the dominant term of the
record — 77% of the lineage file — and the experiment reports that against a guard it is not proposing
to remove.

**Corroboration's tampering note is sharpened past its own wording.** It said the repository cannot tell
pruning from tampering because the difference is intent. Measured: there is nothing to tell apart. A
pruned repository and one that never explored are identical bytes, so this is not a check that is too
weak — it is a distinction the record does not contain.

**Provenance's rule extends once more.** *Nothing derived is persisted* now also covers a preference:
the world the objective chose is recorded as chosen nowhere, and Phase 6 asks for it as a **claim**, in
the terms coordination settled for a decider.

**One imprecision fixed, from the reconstruction era.** `Reading::canonical_head` said *the end of
canonical history* and holds the head of the Event chain. The two coincide wherever admissions are
Events, which is every arrangement before this one; here twelve Commitments are admitted and every
derived answer is expected to hold still, so the old wording invited reading an unchanged head as an
unchanged history.

**One guard's message repaired.** `WitnessedKnowledgeAbsent`, built by coordination, blamed the entry
learned between two occurrences of a readmitted address and said the journal does not offer it — false,
at position 15 of 17. The diagnosis moved to `rebuild`, which holds the whole journal. The original
refusal keeps its wording for the fault it is actually about.

**Two guards stopped being hypotheses.** `EntryAlreadyPassed` and `WitnessedKnowledgeAbsent` were
exercised by no suite in this laboratory. Both are now, by the same journal, differing only in where
the decision falls.

## Variables the protocol left open

**Whether the journal should deduplicate.** Still not decided, and worth more than it was. The trade is
no longer *faithful log versus smaller file*; it is faithful log versus addresses a decision can name.

**What an exploratory decision is, if anything.** This is the variable the result answers, and the shape
of the answer matters as much as the answer. **Nothing failed.** No primitive was missing, no invariant
violated, no call refused — exploration is expressible with what exists, and what it produces is a
record that answers a question nobody asked and cannot be asked the one they did. Reported as a need,
in the vocabulary of the need, and not built.

**Abandoned siblings.** No longer deferred, and they are the majority of the record: eleven of thirteen
decisions. What they cost is measured on both sides of the choice, and what they are *called* is
request 2.

**Cost, as an absolute.** Still not answered, and deliberately. What is reported is which term dominates
and what the work in it was for. How long a replay takes on a real history still needs a history nobody
has.

## What the result does not cover

**Benchmarking.** *Slow* was never the question and no timing was taken.

**A remedy.** Snapshots, a leaner witness, a scope an application may forget at, a tombstone — all
excluded, and Phase 6 names the needs without proposing shapes for them. A remedy is not measured by the
experiment that motivates it.

**Generated Events, and simulated time.** The sharpest dependency exploration-at-scale has on something
outside APE, and unchanged: whoever manufactures the surprise chooses its distribution, and whoever
chooses the clock chooses whether the watermark overtakes reality.

**An agent reading the repository.** Offered by the protocol as explicitly not a success criterion, and
not performed. It is a question about presentation; this result is about the record.

**Parallelism, atomic commit, authenticity.** Unchanged, and atomic commit is still the nearest
unanswered question after coordination.

# Architectural Consequences

## Established for the CLI

* **Producing a world and keeping one are two operations.** `produced` is the first half and `decide` is
  both. An application that only weighs needs the first, and until this experiment nothing did.
* **A level's criterion is the application's, and there is more than one.** `settled` is what has landed;
  `intended` is what will have landed if nothing slips, and it mirrors `Hypothesis::FinalState`
  deliberately — ranking by a level the engine never checks would prefer a candidate the engine refuses.
* **An address must be unambiguous or a decision cannot name knowledge.** A journal admitting one
  address twice is a journal in which `Taken::after` does not address an entry, and the record refuses
  rather than guessing which occurrence was meant.
* **A refusal names the fault, not the collateral.** Where the cause is a multiplicity and the guard
  weighs sets, the diagnosis belongs where the whole journal is in hand.
* **What a decision costs depends on when it is taken.** Every decision witnesses the prefix, so the same
  record costs more for having enumerated before judging.
* **Nothing referred to is nothing findable.** The condition for pruning and the condition for
  unreadability are the same condition.

## Candidates for later experiments

* **A decision that says it weighed rather than meant** — request 2, the one nearest the ontology, and
  about the decision rather than the proposition.
* **A preference, recorded as a claim** — request 3, in the terms coordination settled for a decider.
* **A witness that does not grow with the journal** — request 5, keeping what three experiments
  established the witness buys.
* **Whether a bounded record owes the worlds nobody chose anything at all** — request 4, and the only
  one where *no* is a coherent answer rather than a deferral.
* **Atomic commit**, **authenticity**, **two repositories meeting**, **saying two lines agree** — all
  unchanged, all open since before this.
* **Cost as an absolute**, deferred by seven experiments now.
