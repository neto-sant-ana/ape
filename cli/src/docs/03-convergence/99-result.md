# Result

**Confirmed**, for the stated experimental boundary.

A transfer between two lines of thinking is reconstructible from durable knowledge and durable
decisions alone. Nothing about the transfer is recorded, and the world it produced comes back
whole, by identity, through the same path as every other decision.

```text
a decision names the world it extends   →  and a lineage stops being a sequence
an archive is rebuilt, never read       →  so ancestry is derived like everything else
a transfer is a decision                →  recorded as one, and reproduced as one
```

All three claims held. The third was the one this experiment was least sure of, and it settled
plainly: a resolved transfer states what is left to do, a fork states an outcome by the same two
halves, and the translation between them adds no field to the record.

## Against each criterion

1. **Both lineages come back, and the world they share is one world.** Two decisions name one
   parent, the archive keys by content, and a fresh process returns the branching intact.
2. **Every reconstructed world has the identity of the world it reproduces** — and not because a
   phase asserted it. `worlds.json` is written from the living worlds and weighed inside
   reconstruction, so no read succeeds unless all seven agree.
3. **The archive resolves both lineages and its walk reaches the ancestor from either side.** A
   report at all proves the walk; a Base only the Source descends from is refused by name, which
   proves the walk was performed rather than survived.
4. **The report is reproduced whole, in both directions** — the three identities, the difference,
   the status, the conflict with both of its instants — and against literals written before the
   run, which is what equality between two derivations cannot do.
5. **A transfer applied reproduces through the ordinary path.** Seven decisions, three shapes, no
   new field.
6. **Every persisted derived value is compared on every read**, walked as a closed list of eleven
   corruptions rather than argued. Each is refused and each refusal names what disagreed, with
   every coordinate of a world record reached on its own — identity included, which is weighed
   last and would be masked by any case that moved two fields at once.
7. **What a decision records is a closed set** across all three shapes. `extends` answers both of
   Phase 4's questions: without it two siblings are inexpressible, and it is weighed twice over —
   by having to resolve, and by the world it produces being the world that was witnessed.
8. **Nothing entered the engine.** `core/` is untouched across the branch.
9. **Two earlier conclusions changed, and both are recorded here** rather than corrected in place.

## What the record does not say, and why that is the answer

The applied world is an ordinary fork. It names what was introduced and not that another line of
thinking is why, so the Base and the Source do not survive into it.

Counted rather than argued: given only the repository, **three** distinct transfers produce
exactly that world. The one that was taken, and two whose Source is the applied world itself. So
provenance is not recoverable by search, and no phase claimed otherwise.

That is the right answer to the question this experiment asked. Reproduction never needed the
Base — recording it would not change one bit of the world produced, only what a reader can be
told about why. **What must be preserved for a world to come back is not what must be preserved
for a reader to understand it**, and only the first was under test.

## What changed in the experiments before it

* **The reconstruction experiment's Phase 3** asserted that no derived world identity is
  persisted. A decision now names the world it extends, so one appears — as a reference. Its own
  guard is what said so, unprompted. What a decision still never records is the world it
  *produced*.
* **The corroboration experiment's conclusion that the sequence witness is redundant for
  detection.** It is not. This subject builds the case that one said it could not: a decision
  moved past an admission it never selects produces an identical world, every world record still
  agrees, and with the witness stubbed the repository reads clean.

  The property that decides it was not being tracked by either experiment — **whether the
  arrangement admits knowledge that some of its worlds decline**. An experiment concluding that a
  check is unnecessary is making a claim about its subject as much as about the check.
* **Three of the corroboration table's corruptions** are now turned by the reference rather than
  by the world witness, which fires earlier and says less. Two were restored by recomputing
  `extends`, because a phase that subtracts a corroborator must leave the repository otherwise
  consistent. The third keeps its refusal and gains a qualification: a reference corroborates
  every world that something extends, and a leaf is covered only by its witness.

## What the result does not cover

**Choosing a Base.** For either direction between the two tips, exactly one world is an ancestor
of both — counted. So this arrangement exercises a Base and cannot exercise choosing one, and
nothing here measures what a second admissible Base would change.

**Provenance.** Two of the three explanations for the applied world have the applied world as
their Source, so they are the answer read backwards. Whether a lineage can always exclude those —
and be left with one — is a rule this subject raises and does not test.

**A refused transfer's recourse.** Advancing the Target would make the refused direction
applicable. Advancing is a decision about which knowledge a world recognizes, taken by whoever
reasons in it, and treating it as a repair would let a transfer decide what is not its to decide.
Not measured, deliberately.

**Consistent forgery.** Unchanged from the previous experiment, and now more expensive: a forger
must recompute `extends`, which cannot be read off the file and only re-derives by running the
engine. Still undetected.

**Concurrency.** One writer, one process. Unchanged.

# Architectural Consequences

## Established for the CLI

* **A lineage is a tree, and the record says so.** A decision names the world it extends; order
  is preserved because it is the order decisions were taken, and it no longer carries the shape.
* **Source and Target are roles, not sides.** A difference is measured between Base and Source
  and never consults the Target, so the two directions of a transfer are different questions with
  disjoint answers. Symmetry would be a loss.
* **Which direction can carry an intention is readable from two cuts.** An introduced commitment
  is open in the Source and later than the Target's cut, so a refusal of this kind needs the
  Source strictly later — decidable before any set arithmetic.
* **A question is asked of a record, not stored in one.** Reconstructing a world needs the
  repository; reconstructing a report needs the repository and three identities, which a caller
  obtains by reading and naming.
* **The archive is built, never opened**, and it is invisible when it works. Its absence would
  appear as a decision naming a world nobody can find.
* **One reconstruction procedure, two callers.** The order between the journal and the lineage is
  what the divergence experiment spent a round repairing; a second copy is a second place for it
  to drift.

## Candidates for later experiments

* **Provenance** — what a record must hold for a reader to know *why* an intention was
  introduced, as distinct from what it must hold to reproduce the world.
* **Choosing between Bases** — an arrangement branching twice on one side, where two admissible
  Bases give two legitimate reports and the difference between them is the measurement.
* **Authenticity**, unchanged from the previous experiment and now with one more derived value a
  forger has to reproduce.
* **Cost**, which every experiment has now deferred. Seven worlds are read by replaying a journal
  and applying seven decisions, and eleven corruptions each pay for a full rebuild.
