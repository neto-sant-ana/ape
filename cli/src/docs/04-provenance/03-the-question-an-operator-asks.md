# Observation 3 — The question an operator asks is about the commitment

Necessity's last chance was to find a consequence of not knowing. A line of thinking was
discredited by the only means this repository admits — an Event voided the one commitment that
ever travelled out of it — and the question was asked as an operator would ask it.

```text
which worlds does this reach?
```

The repository answers, from selections alone:

```text
1  narrow      selects the tooling      reached
2  broad       selects the tooling      reached
4  adopting    selects the tooling      reached
5  recognizing selects the tooling      reached, and says it is Cancelled
```

Provenance would have answered a different question, and its answer is a **subset**: only the
adopted world and its advancement took anything from the broad plan. The narrow plan holds the same
tooling, donated nothing, and is reached all the same.

So provenance here does not merely add nothing. It names a set that is not the one an operator
needs, and it under-reports the one they do.

## Why that generalizes

What reaches a world is what that world **selects**, and selection is derived. A commitment is a
canonical identity: the tooling in the narrow plan, in the broad plan and in the adopted world is
one commitment, and whatever happens to it happens to all of them at once, through their own cuts
and not through any relation between them.

Donation is not a channel. Nothing flows along it after the fact, because nothing was copied —
what a transfer does is cause a selection to be written, and after that the selection is what
carries everything.

## An honest limit on the guard

The set of reached worlds is a function of the selections, not of the Event. Cancelling a different
commitment leaves it unchanged, which is not a weakness of the measurement — it *is* the
measurement: the answer does not depend on what happened, only on who selected what.

It does mean the guard cannot be made red by anything the discrediting does. Confirmed by changing
a selection instead: with the narrow plan wanting the expansion rather than the tooling, the set
becomes `[2, 4, 5]` and the guard fires. Every mutation that changes the answer changes the
arrangement, which is the honest statement of what the guard pins.

## Part A ends refuted

Three phases, and the necessity claim does not survive any of them.

```text
Phase 1   search does not recover which line an intention came from    established
Phase 2   nothing about a world depends on which line                  established
Phase 3   nothing an operator asks depends on it either                established
```

The first was supposed to support necessity and turned out to support only unrecoverability. Two
Sources that explain one world are the same transfer in essence, and after that there is nothing
left for the answer to change.

What survives is not a repository question at all. *Who was consulted, and when* is a fact about
the relation between two lines of deliberation — real to want, with no consumer inside a boundary
that has one writer and no agents of decision. It is named for whichever experiment has those.

Part B still runs, because the protocol said it would: a cost measured for something unnecessary is
knowledge, and an unmeasured one is why a question comes back. What it builds does not stay.
