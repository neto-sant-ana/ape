# Result

**Refuted**, in both halves, for the stated experimental boundary.

```text
necessity    there is a question about a world that the record cannot answer
             and no search over the repository recovers               REFUTED

possibility  where an intention came from is recordable as a claim that
             reconstruction refuses when it is false                  REFUTED
```

The protocol wrote a refuted necessity as the most likely outcome and said plainly that it would be
a complete result. It is, and possibility fell to the same fact.

> **What a transfer moves is all a transfer leaves.**

Two Sources that explain one world produce one resolved transfer, so they agree about everything
that reached the Target. There is nothing left for the answer to change, and nothing derivable for a
claim to predict.

## Against each criterion

**Necessity**

1. **More than one non-degenerate explanation, measured.** Two, with the Target pinned by what the
   record says the decision extends. Two rival plans that disagree about what they *introduce*, and
   whose transfers coincide because the receiving line already held the difference.
2. **A question the record does not answer and no search recovers — but not one anyone asks.** The
   question exists; its content does not. Nothing about the adopted world depends on the answer,
   because a transfer applied and an independent decision are one record and produce one world.
3. **The consequence was demonstrated and dissolved.** An Event voided the one commitment that
   travelled. The question an operator asks — *which worlds does this reach?* — is answered from
   selections alone, and provenance would have named a **subset** of the right answer: the narrow
   plan holds the same tooling, donated nothing, and is reached all the same.

**Possibility**

4. **The closed set gained one optional field**, absent from every decision not chosen from a
   transfer. No earlier suite noticed it.
5. **A false claim is not refused.** Every Base and Source the record could name was written down,
   one repository each. The check rejects the incoherent, the impossible and the temporally absurd
   — and no claim is ever *contradicted*, because every possible one predicts the same transfer.
6. **Nothing derived is left uncompared**, but the comparison is the one that fails to discriminate.
7. **The arrangement reproduces whole through a fresh process**, claim included, against literals.
8. **What the claim couples is measured**: a line of thinking cannot be discarded once anything
   claims to have adopted from it.
9. **Nothing entered the engine.** `core/` is untouched across the branch.
10. **One earlier conclusion changed** and is recorded here.

## Three findings that outlast the refutation

**There is no room on a world for where it came from.** Identity is derived from content, and two
routes to one content are one world — measured by deciding the same fork with no transfer behind it.
So provenance could only ever be an annotation on a decision, and no rebuild reaches it. That
constrained Part B before it started.

**A claim is refusable exactly insofar as it predicts something derivable.** This one predicts the
transfer, which is what every rival account has in common, so the check accepts precisely what a
search finds and separates them no better. Nothing was built wrong; the check does everything a
check could do, and the space it cannot enter is the space provenance was for.

**A claim cannot name the world its own decision is about.** At the moment the check runs, that world
has not been decided and is not in the archive. The degenerate-exclusion rule convergence proposed
and never tested turns out to need no rule — it falls out of when things happen.

## What changed in the experiment before it

**Convergence counted with the Target left open**, which counts transfers producing the same
*selection* rather than the same world. A parent is part of an identity, so a fork of a different
Target produces a different world holding the same commitments — measured here.

Pinned, its own arrangement leaves two explanations with one degenerate, so the rule it proposed
would have left exactly one, and its claim that provenance is not recoverable by search did not hold
of its subject. Nothing else it concluded moves: reproduction never needed the Base either way.

## What was reverted, and where it lives

Part B's shape is removed. `Adoption`, the `from` field, and the check that re-asks the question are
not in the repository, and Phases 4 through 7 go with them — a guard that guards nothing keeps a
thing alive, which is the failure mode this experiment just spent three phases naming.

The measurement is not lost, it is archived: it is reproducible at the commit that carried it, which
is the only place code for something a laboratory decided against belongs. What survives in the tree
is Part A, which measures a repository that records no claim and must stay able to say so.

## What the result does not cover

**Coordination.** *Who was consulted, and when* is a fact about the relation between two lines of
deliberation rather than about anything either produced. Real to want, with no consumer inside a
boundary that has one writer and no agents of decision. It is the experiment this one hands the
question to.

**Authenticity.** A claim that predicted something other than the transfer would have to predict who
wrote the record. That is a signature and a key, unchanged from the corroboration experiment.

**Abandoned siblings.** Deferred by five protocols now, and this one measured a cost against it for
the first time.

**Concurrency.** One writer, one process. Unchanged.

# Architectural Consequences

## Established for the CLI

* **Provenance is not a property of a world.** Two routes to one content are one world, and a record
  can say more than a world can hold.
* **Infeasibility is a property of a selection, not of a member of one.** The commitments that make
  a plan impossible are feasible inside a world that holds all of them and one more.
* **What reaches a world is what that world selects.** Donation is not a channel; nothing was
  copied, so nothing flows along it afterwards.
* **A repository of decisions is enough.** Stated as a result rather than as a preference, and it is
  the strongest thing this experiment has to say.

## Candidates for later experiments

* **Coordination** — the experiment that has agents and more than one writer, and therefore the
  first one with a consumer for who consulted whom.
* **Abandoned siblings**, with the cost of pointing at them now measured.
* **Authenticity**, unchanged.
* **Cost**, deferred by five experiments.
