# Observation 3 — The coordinate instructs; only the world corroborates

Phase 5 removed things and watched what stopped working.

Two of the four the protocol lists were answered before it ran, and answered by the boundary
rather than by measurement. The decisions cannot go: a `Thesis` does not deserialize, so a
recorded world is not a world and nothing else produces one. The instant and the intention
cannot go: a cut is resolved from an instant, a genesis selects what it selects, and a
decision without them is not a decision. Neither subtraction is expressible.

What remained was the pair this experiment added.

---

## The coordinate is necessary, and not as a check

Neutered — every decision pointing at the journal's last entry, so a reader admits everything
before deciding anything — the repository stops being readable at all. Not "detects less":
the world it produces disagrees with the world it recorded, and reading is refused.

```text
without the coordinate  →  every world comes back diverged  →  refused, always
```

So the coordinate is an **instruction**, not a witness. It tells a reader which knowledge to
derive against. The witnesses tell a reader whether the derivation agreed with what was
recorded. Those are different jobs and this experiment had been treating them as one.

---

## The sequence witness is redundant for detection

Neutered — recomputed from the coordinate it names, so it says only what a reader would work
out anyway — an intact repository is unaffected, and the corruption it was introduced for is
*still refused*.

```text
coordinate repointed, sequence witness present  →  refused: "entry E was admitted, and the
                                                    decision was not taken against it"
coordinate repointed, sequence witness absent   →  refused: "world 0 disagrees with what was
                                                    recorded, in the chain it recognizes"
```

A wrong coordinate produces a wrong world, and the world witness sees every wrong world. Phase
3 turned a row that Phase 4 would have turned anyway.

That is the shape of an honest negative: the field earns nothing that another field does not
already earn, and by this experiment's own criterion — a derived value is worth persisting
only if something compares it — a redundant comparison is closer to ceremony than to a check.

---

## What it does earn, and what this subject cannot ask

Two things keep it from being simply deletable, and the second is the interesting one.

**It names the cause rather than the symptom.** "The decision was not taken against this
entry" says what is wrong with the repository; "the world disagrees in the chain it
recognizes" says what the repository produced. A reader holding only the second has to work
back to the first.

**It catches a corruption of deliberation that produces no wrong world.** A coordinate moved
to another entry that resolves the same cut, over the same selection, yields the same world —
so the world witness is silent and the record of *when the decision was taken* is false
anyway. The divergence experiment's whole argument was that this record is what makes a
lineage auditable.

This subject cannot express that case. Every entry the genesis's coordinate could be moved to
either changes its cut or is refused as unselectable, so the difference between the two
witnesses is argued here and measured nowhere.

---

## Consequences to carry

* The closed set a decision records is now: an instant, an intention, a coordinate, and a
  witness — and the fourth is the only one this experiment can show is not load-bearing.
* Whether it stays is a question this subject cannot settle. Deciding it needs an arrangement
  where a coordinate can be wrong without a world being wrong, which is a subject of its own.
* Instruction and witness should not be conflated again. A repository holds some things so a
  reader can derive, and others so a reader can disagree; the first cannot be dropped for
  redundancy and the second cannot be kept without being compared.
