# Observation 1 — Knowledge defends itself; decisions do not

Six edits, each well-formed and each false, applied to a repository this laboratory had
already declared correct.

```text
a coordinate repointed at an entry that exists   SILENT
the genesis's intention narrowed                 SILENT
two roles reordered                              HARMLESS
two commitments reordered                        REFUSED
an eligibility removed                           REFUSED
the cancelling Event removed                     REFUSED
```

Half of them were caught. Not one was caught on purpose.

---

## Nothing is looking

Each refusal came from an invariant that exists for another reason entirely.

```text
two commitments reordered  →  a selected commitment must be in canonical history
an eligibility removed     →  an agent must be eligible for the roles its statement requires
the Event removed          →  an address a decision names must resolve
```

The first two are the Axiom's, protecting the meaning of an admission. The third is the
application's, and it is the only one this laboratory added — added, moreover, to make a
coordinate usable, not to detect anything.

So a corrupted journal tends to trip over something on its way past. That is not detection; it
is a graph of references being dense enough that a hole in it usually shows. Which holes show
is a property of the subject's dates and its reference structure, not a property of the
repository.

**The prediction that failed:** the eligibility removal was expected to pass in silence,
because nothing addresses an eligibility by identity and no world names one. It is refused at
the moment a commitment is admitted, by an invariant that reads it without ever referring to
it. Recorded because it was written down as a prediction before it was measured, and it was
wrong.

---

## What passes is what was decided

The two silent rows are the two that alter the lineage rather than the journal.

```text
the coordinate    →  which knowledge the decision was taken against
the intention     →  which commitments the world proposes
```

Both come back as a different genesis, both lose the refusal at −70, and neither produces a
word of complaint. The asymmetry has a plain cause:

> *The journal is a graph of references under invariants. The lineage refers to the journal and
> is referred to by nothing.*

An admission has to be admissible, and admitting it proves things about it. A decision is
accepted as stated. Nothing downstream of it can disagree, because everything downstream of it
is derived *from* it — which is exactly the condition under which a derivation cannot be a
check.

---

## The control, and why it matters

Two roles admitted on the same day, neither referring to the other, swapped: the lineage comes
back identical. An entry's identity is its content, so their order carries nothing.

That row is not filler. It brackets the target from the other side:

```text
a check that refuses the harmless row   →  over-strict; it refuses variation the record admits
a check that accepts the silent rows    →  useless; it is the state this observation describes
```

Without it, a witness that refused everything would score perfectly.

---

## Consequences to carry

* Phases 3 and 4 are measured on the two silent rows. The three refusals are already refused
  and cannot improve; the harmless row must stay harmless.
* Corroboration is needed for the lineage, not for the journal. That is a narrowing of this
  experiment's own question, arrived at by measurement rather than by design.
* The three refusals are incidental and should not be counted as protection. A subject whose
  commitments were recorded on different days, or whose journal had fewer cross-references,
  would refuse fewer of them for reasons that have nothing to do with being more correct.
